**Method:** read the canon, verify the current `crates/` sources and design docs against it by grep and by reading, distinguish design-sanctioned deferral from silent drift. Then (per op's addendum request) review the two landed panel deliverables in full, mirrored against this same canon.

## One-line verdict

The soul is intact in the prose and the skeleton, and inert in the wiring: the round's identity, its two-artifact model, its twelve-crate taxonomy, and its twelve-form Core all survive faithfully in the docs and the crate shapes, but the three mechanisms that ARE the round's heart, the one-signature-projected-into-both-artifacts, the graded proof compiled away, and the CFG-of-blocks the runtime interprets, are declared as types and prose and not yet wired to do anything, so the framework today is a correct skeleton wearing the right identity with its load-bearing organs stubbed.

## Part 1: what the round's soul actually is

Distilled from the closing statement, the round's soul is four coupled commitments, in priority order. Everything else (crate count, dependency layering, bench corrections) is body, not soul.

1. **One graded algebraic signature is the single source, projected.** "A single declarative language definition, a graded algebraic signature, is the one source: the families are the operations of an algebraic-effect theory... the value domain and the IR are two views of the same operations. The Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig runtime consts are both projections of this one signature, differential-checked by a content-addressed manifest" (`202607240100:70-74`). This is the deepest heart. `vehje-signature` exists to BE that source; `vehje-runtime-gen` exists to PROJECT it into validated data; `vehje-check` exists to READ its per-family schema.

2. **Certified generation: the proof is compiled away into the structure of the generated Zig.** "The proof is compiled into the structure of the generated Zig (illegal states unrepresentable, total dispatch over the family set, comptime-folded inclusion)" (`:60-65`). The graded proof spine (effect, lease/reach, binding-time, assurance) is the proof; it must be discharged compile-side and erased, so "the runtime carries no prover" (`:108`).

3. **Two artifacts, Rust emits data not source.** "Rust emits data, not Zig source. The specialised Zig is produced by Zig comptime from that data" (`:56-58`).

4. **The identity: authoring/templating majority, native never the driver.** "It serves a census of consumers equally, and the representative and largest lean toward authoring and templating... A native and JIT endgame exists as one point on the output spectrum, never the driver, and one hard consumer (ikiuni's scripting surface) is a consumer, never the identity" (`:41-44`). The round explicitly struck the opposite: "the nonlinear speed layer is struck, the runtime-performance-as-lone-purpose framing is removed" (`:15`).

## Part 2: the drift audit, per soul-element

### Soul 1 (one signature, projected): DECLARED, NOT WIRED. The sharpest drift.

`vehje-signature` is a well-formed data contract: `Operation { family: FamilyId, effect: EffectMask, lease: LeaseRule }`, `Signature`, `TargetDecl`, and a `Projection` enum with the three canonical projections `Eliminator` / `Introduction` / `IntroductionCompiled` (`vehje-signature/src/lib.rs:65-72`, `16-31`). Structurally faithful. But nothing projects from it:

- `vehje-runtime-gen::generate<'a>(_signature: &Signature<'a>, slices: &'a [Slice<'a>])` takes the signature **underscored and ignored** (`vehje-runtime-gen/src/lib.rs:108`). It concatenates opaque caller-supplied byte slices and content-hashes them (`:108-127`). The canon's "runtime generation is Deegen-style from the one signature: for each family operation the generator emits an interpreter arm, a copy-and-patch stencil, and a differential test" (`202607240100:112-113`) is entirely absent. The one crate whose job is to project the signature into validated data does not read the signature.
- `vehje-typecheck` declares the `vehje-signature` dep in `Cargo.toml` and its module doc claims it reads the schema ("reading the per-family lease-rule schema from `vehje-signature`", `vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:8-9`), but there is **no code use**: no `use vehje_signature`, no call to `Signature::lease_rule`, nothing. The claim is a doc comment the source does not honour (a `cl-claim-sketch-discipline` violation: a claim about source state that source does not match).

So the signature's two canonical consumers both ignore it. By the canon's own test, "the closest call in the taxonomy: if the signature's only real consumer turns out to be the packaging crate, it folds into `vehje-runtime-gen`" (`202607240100:276-279`), the reality is worse than the fold-trigger: neither the check pass nor the packaging crate actually consumes it. The single deepest commitment of the round, the one signature projected into both artifacts, is inert. This is the finding that most threatens the soul, and it is not marked deferred anywhere.

### Soul 2 (proof compiled away): HALF-WIRED, within canon's shippable-core line but not yet demonstrable.

The graded vocabulary is real (`Effect`, `EffectMask`, `BindingTime`, `Knowledge`, `ReachMask`, `Lease`, `Assurance`, `Grade` all present, `vehje-ir/src/grade.rs`, `effect.rs`; old `Pure`/`Reads`/`Writes` gone except in a supersession doc-comment, `effect.rs:9-10`). Effect inclusion is wired (thermometer OR). The lease depth-floor is wired (with the known `#30` slot-collision catalogued red). But two of the four axes are inert: `check` sets `binding: Knowledge::empty(), assurance: Assurance::Unclaimed` unconditionally (`vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:311-312`), and const-fold folds only syntactic literals, not knowledge-graded values.

Canon sanctions this as the shippable core ("the shippable core is the concrete grade enums and bitmasks and the bottom-up inclusion checks"; the gradual logical relation is deferred, `202607240100:184-186`). So the half-wiring is not a violation. But the consequence for the soul is worth stating plainly: certified generation means "the proof compiled into the structure of the generated Zig", and with two axes inert and `runtime-gen` not projecting, there is no live proof being compiled into any generated structure yet. The mechanism is declared; it has never run end to end. That is honest for a first-light core, but it means Soul 2 is a promise the code does not yet keep, and the promise is load-bearing enough to track as such.

### Soul 3 (two artifacts, emit data not source): PROSE INTACT, projection passthrough.

Root DESIGN carries it faithfully: "There are exactly two artifacts, and Rust is entirely out of the runtimes", "emits validated data (the family table...)", "the dev-time compiler emits data, not [source]" (`DESIGN.md.tmpl:24-40`). Identity preserved. But because `generate()` emits data only by concatenating slices handed to it rather than projecting them from the signature (Soul 1), the "Rust emits validated data" is realized as "Rust forwards byte slices someone else built." The artifact boundary is right; the generation behind it is a passthrough. Partial, and downstream of the Soul 1 fix.

### Soul 4 (identity, native never the driver): FULLY PRESERVED. The positive finding.

This is where the audit expected drift (a bench-heavy arc pulling the project back toward runtime performance) and found none. Root DESIGN: "vehje serves a spectrum of consumers equally. The representative and largest lean toward authoring and templating, where the speed concern is the compile stage and the emit rather than runtime logic; a native and just-in-time endgame exists as one point on the output spectrum, never the driver" (`DESIGN.md.tmpl:19-22`); native is "a method-agnostic point at the far end of the output spectrum, not a privileged [tier]" (`:91`). Certified generation named as the identity mechanism (`:11`). The struck runtime-performance-as-purpose framing did not creep back. The soul-in-the-prose is intact.

### Body: what holds, what is honestly deferred, what drifted in the canon itself

- **Holds:** the twelve crates exactly, no rogue crate, reserved seats (`vehje-lex`/`vehje-syntax`/`vehje-stencil`) correctly not created; `vehje-fixpoint` is `vehje-ir`-free per canon (verified, no dep); all twelve Core forms incl `Handle` present; `Handle` covered in the match arms the src CL names; D6 incremental present in `vehje-fixpoint/src/incr.rs` (differential + content-addressed, `Delta` reserved, provenance-semiring deferred); dependency layering acyclic.
- **Honestly deferred (flagged, not drift):** the `vehje-typecheck` to `vehje-check` rename, attempted and reverted by a real bootstrap paradox (doc templates freeze at doc-lock, the git rename stages them, blocked by the changelist gates), explicitly FLAG FOR OP in the src CL (`202607240200:36-44`). The dev-time e-graph, mutable-lease extension, stencil toolchain, all BACKLOG with FIXME sites.
- **The canon cites retracted evidence (Fallin's find, confirmed):** the closing statement's "settled and not reopened" list locks "the native 1.0x-to-1.5x ceiling" (`202607240100:419-420`), but `mock/research/202607220300_bench-remeasure-synthesis.md` retracted that number two days earlier (the old figure was native-vs-native; re-measured FFI-opaque, a switch interpreter is ~2.0x a shape-specialized native loop). The canon quotes a number its own research tree had already withdrawn. A doc-vs-evidence drift no lint can catch.

## Part 3: the diagnosis

The round did not drift in DIRECTION. There is no rogue architecture, no recentering on the wrong purpose, no lost crate, no abandoned form. The identity, the taxonomy, the Core, and the two-artifact model are all faithfully present. What drifted is the gap between the soul as STATED and the soul as WIRED: the single source projects nothing, the proof compiles nothing away yet, and the CFG the runtime is meant to interpret is unbuilt (Fallin's Finding C, below). The framework is a correct skeleton with the right identity and three stubbed organs. That is a recoverable, honest first-light state, but only if the stubs are marked as stubs (two of the three are not) and the next work re-lands the wiring rather than building further body around inert organs.

The risk is specific and near-term: the live redirection (the IR-to-IR expansion topic + the JIT/AOT panel) is about to build significant machinery ON TOP of these inert organs. If that machinery is built without first wiring the binding-time axis (Soul 2) and the signature projection (Soul 1), it will encode the inertness deeper. The good news, developed below, is that the redirection's own answer IS the canon's inert axis, so wiring it is the same act as answering the panel.

## Part 4: course corrections, ranked by leverage

1. **Wire the binding-time axis, or accept the panel is re-deriving canon.** (Highest leverage; re-lands Soul 2 and answers the panel at once.) Rompf's entire compile/runtime-line answer is that the "line" is the four-point `BindingTime` lattice `{LanguageAuthor, Bundler, HostLoader, Runtime}` already in `grade.rs:105`. That axis is exactly canon's binding-time graded modality (`202607240100:159-163`), and it sits inert (`binding: Knowledge::empty()`). Wiring it turns const-fold from syntactic-literal folding into staged partial evaluation (fold a value when its `Knowledge` grade includes the current stage), which is the LMS `Rep[T]`-vs-`T` distinction Rompf names, and it makes the compile/runtime commitment a checked fact rather than prose. This single correction re-lands the graded-spine soul AND resolves the panel's question, because the panel's question was always canon's own inert axis.

2. **Wire the signature projection, or fold the crate (canon's fork).** (Re-lands Soul 1.) Make `generate()` project the family table, effect masks, and lease schema FROM the `Signature` rather than taking them as opaque slices, and make `check` actually call `Signature::lease_rule`. If projection stays deferred past this round, honour canon's explicit fallback and fold `vehje-signature` into `vehje-runtime-gen` rather than leaving a data contract nobody reads. The current limbo (a real type, zero real consumers) is the one state canon's own text rules out. Adopt Fallin's Finding D as the first concrete projection: add `binding_time_ceiling: BindingTime` to `Operation` so the macro binding-time constraint (Soul 2) becomes a schema fact the check pass reads, which forces the check-reads-signature wiring in the same stroke.

3. **Bound the IR-to-IR fixpoint before it ships (the topic contradicts locked canon).** The closing statement locks "no unbounded saturation" for exactly the cheap const-fold+CSE stratum (`202607240100:96`), and the `202607241330` IR-to-IR topic reopens it as "iterate... until a run produces no change" with no bound. Per `canonical-design-outranks-intermediate-rounds`, the fresh topic is the thing to fix: state the node-count cap the eqsat bench already validated (Fallin Finding E: bounded 512 e-nodes extracts identical optimal cost, 17x-17000x faster) so it is "a bounded pass that iterates a small capped number of rounds," which is what the round actually settled.

4. **Mark and resolve the CFG-of-blocks gap (Fallin Finding C, the sharpest unflagged drift).** Canon locks "the interpreter is a CFG of straight-line blocks" as settled (`202607240100:117-124`, `:417`); the only encoder that runs is a flat tree re-tag, and `Block`/`Function`/`TerminatorKind` are unreferenced dead type surface with no FIXME. Add the missing `// FIXME:` at those definitions today (the workspace's own placeholder discipline requires it), and force the design question into the next doc round: does `Tier::Arena` need a real CFG (then tree-to-CFG is a named owned pass, closure-conversion-plus-control-flow, missing from the taxonomy), or is `Tier::Arena` a tree-walker and `Block`/`Function` are `Tier::Bytecode`-only (then they must move off the unconditional `Residual` struct).

5. **Guard the identity through the redirection (Soul 4).** The JIT/AOT panel is exploring native/tier depth; canon struck runtime-performance-as-purpose. The panel's own evidence agrees with canon (native is per-region not a tier; effort belongs on the binding-time axis, "fold more, earlier", not a native JIT for the scalar-authoring majority), but make it an explicit guardrail in the next topic so the exploration does not silently recenter the project on the one consumer (ikiuni) canon named "a consumer, never the identity."

6. **Fix the retracted native-ceiling number in the canon's settled list** (Fallin Finding F) as its own small doc round, since a locked canonical statement is citing evidence its own tree withdrew.

7. **Complete the `vehje-check` rename** in a dedicated doc-phase round (the bootstrap paradox the src CL flagged), before the crate name and its own prose (`grade.rs:9` already says "a `vehje-check` pass") drift further apart.

### Optimizations the redirection makes available (op asked for these)

- **Collapse CSE into a hash-consing `Builder`** (Rompf 5, Fallin Addendum 1, LMS citation): once every productive pass emits through `Builder`, structural sharing falls out of a hash-cons lookup on construction, subsuming the separate redirect-table CSE. This is canon's own "one structural-hash definition, three consumers" (`202607240100:267-268`) realized more directly. Bench before locking, but the a-priori case is LMS's actual mechanism.
- **Defunctionalization as the standard recipe for the "no recursion in Zig" wall** (Fallin Addendum 1, Reynolds/Danvy citations): every recursive pass (lease walk, the CFG-construction pass if Finding C needs one, the e-graph extractor) hits the same wall `congruence.rs:1-8` already notes; naming defunctionalization as the citable derivation turns "did we get the iterative version right" into "does it follow the recipe," with a correctness proof attached.
- **The `RuleTable` lens-law differential test** (Fallin Addendum 1, Foster et al.): the shared const-fold/CSE table crosses to both artifacts and canon only asserts in prose that they "do not drift." Write the cross-language put-get property as a red differential-test target now (per catalogue-edge-cases), turning the prose assertion into a checked acceptance gate.

### The mirror's net

The panel and this audit converge, not by coordination but because the canon is coherent enough that three independent reads (a staging theorist, a compiler-backend engineer, a drift auditor) land on the same structure and the same gaps. Combining all three, the canon has exactly these live wounds, ranked by depth into the soul:

1. `vehje-signature` projects nothing; both its consumers ignore it (Soul 1, this audit; sharpens Fallin D). WIRE or fold.
2. The binding-time axis is inert, which is both the graded-proof soul (Soul 2, this audit + Rompf) and the entire answer to the panel's question. WIRE.
3. The CFG-of-blocks interpreter is locked canon, unbuilt, unflagged (Fallin C). MARK now, decide next round.
4. The IR-to-IR topic reopens "unbounded saturation" the canon locked shut (Fallin Addendum 2 + Rompf 1 + Fallin E). BOUND it.
5. Macro hygiene is unaddressed in canon and impl (Rompf 4). Owe the fix.
6. The canon's "settled" list cites the retracted native-ceiling number (Fallin F). CORRECT the doc.
7. `NodeRef` has no arena identity; `fold_core` cannot carry the IR-to-IR fold (Fallin A, B). Fix before the emit path ships.

None of these is a direction change. All are the gap between a faithfully-stated soul and its wiring, plus one stale number. The single highest-leverage act, wiring the binding-time axis, simultaneously re-lands Soul 2, answers the panel, and (via Fallin D's `binding_time_ceiling`) forces the Soul 1 signature wiring. That is where the redirection should start, and it starts inside the canon, not beyond it.
