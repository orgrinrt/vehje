# Imported: the mockspace procedural-documents arc

**Imported:** 2026-07-19, from `mockspace/mock/research/202607190000_procedural-docs-language/`.
**Status:** reference material for the extension-point contract round. Not vehje design.

## What this is

A four-phase design and review arc run in mockspace's research tree between 2026-07-19 and its import here.
It began from a mockspace need (documents that query a registry rather than restating it), established
early that the work belongs to vehje with mockspace as one consumer, and then lost track of that framing.

The arc's own founding document, `00_seed.md`, assigns ten of its twelve parts to vehje crates and states
that only two are specific to mockspace. The panel briefs that followed inverted that, naming mockspace the
immediate consumer and vehje the longer-range one, and all sixteen expert files inherited the inversion.

`13_vehje_canon.md` records the inversion, what it invalidated, and what survives.

## How to read it, and what to distrust

**Read for the mechanisms.** The target-agnostic material is the reason this is imported: inclusion rather
than coverage for feature checking, target-decided staging (which phase four identified as polymorphic
embedding, Hofer/Ostermann/Rendel/Moors, GPCE 2008), total renderers over a declared family set, structural
hashing as identity, an explicit frame stack with a branch quota, and a borrowed non-allocating scope chain.

**Distrust every number.** All costs, corpus sizes, form counts and reduction arguments were measured
against mockspace's registry corpus, which is not vehje's workload. The nine-form core and the argument for
reducing it further both rest on a corpus that lacks call syntax and iteration because the feature being
designed does not exist there yet.

**Distrust the claims that were not corrected.** The arc's own record is poor. Seven count disputes, none
arithmetic and all caused by undefined denominators. Three structural findings that dissolved when someone
executed against them. A citation that propagated wrong through two phases.

**Read the ledger first.** `panel4/04_hoffman_ledger.md` sorts every load-bearing claim into established,
novel, or unsupported, with a corrections register. `11_standards_judgment.md` and `12_delta_evaluation.md`
assess the process, which produced 8653 lines of prose and zero lines of code.
