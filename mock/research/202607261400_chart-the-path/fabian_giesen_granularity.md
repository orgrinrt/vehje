## Verdict in one line

The near gates (G0 through G10) are mostly sized right or one split away from right; the far gates are not gates at all (G14 and G18 are tracks wearing gate numbers, G19 through G23 are captions), the track partition reintroduces a seam the synthesis had already dissolved (G2 and G7 rewrite the same function on "independent" tracks), and the single UNPROVEN status conflates "needs a sketch" with "needs a design round with op", which makes roughly a third of the TODO-sketch column unspecifiable as written.

## Gates that are harder than stated

**G3.** Reads as "tail calls plus a reclamation rule, Task #49". The reclamation half contains an escape analysis with a trusted-base consequence. The no-pop discipline is load-bearing for closures by the module's own contract ("a closure that outlives the scope it was written in still reads the bindings it captured, which a popping stack cannot promise", `runtime.zig:13-17`); reclaiming a loop body's slots requires proving no closure captured them, which is either a runtime liveness check paid per binding or trusting a compile-stage reach proof for runtime memory safety, and the latter is an enlargement of `positive:29`'s enumerated rim, which that section says must be stated, not scattered. On top of that the whole loop story rides on the loops-as-recursive-bindings-plus-exit-effect encoding, which lives in the census (presumed-wrong rung, `202607251530:59-61`) and per the mirror still owes its two-expert agreement. G3 is one line in the roadmap and it is a design question, a canon-rim question, and an unratified encoding stacked on each other.

**G8.** "Mechanism open" is stated but not priced. The canon's three candidate mechanisms (round-trip validation, the lens projection, a self-checking typed manifest, `positive:31`) differ by an order of magnitude in work, and the per-slice manifest-hash unsoundness is already FIXME'd in tree (`vehje-runtime-gen/src/lib.rs:77-81`: whole-package hash only, per-slice hashes owed). Whichever mechanism op picks, this is translation-validation-shaped work, not a wiring gate. It also can never terminate at "seam closed", only "seam narrowed, residual named" (the mirror's point, which the roadmap absorbed into G8's property but not into its size).

**G11, G12, G13 jointly.** Worded as a type fix plus two relocations. What they actually are, once the per-script proof locus is answered: the semantics of the 830-line graded check re-landing on the other side of the artifact boundary, in a no-alloc, defunctionalised form, either interpreted from extracted data or comptime-specialised, plus the axis-interaction rule and its red test that `positive:27` makes the spine's theorem bar. That is the largest coherent chunk in the roadmap after the input side, split across three paragraphs whose shared dominating sub-problem (the form the relocated proof machinery takes) is named in none of them.

**G16.** The bench decides the representation; the canon says plainly that the hard problem survives the bench: reinstatement without a heap is "intended and unproven" and effect-subtraction soundness is an open obligation (`positive:25`). One real cost the budget-fit design must start from, derived in the Wingo read and verified by me against `runtime.zig:176-181` (bump, no pop): k resumptions of a body binding b names cost k*b environment slots monotonically, so multi-shot memory is linear in resumptions. The gate as written is a fork; the work behind the fork is a mechanism design either way.

**G10.** "The load verifier" as one line hides that parse-don't-validate over this wire format means typed no-alloc views over borrowed bytes covering nodes, pool, blob, and clause sections, with every cross-index proven in the decode rather than checked per access (the current `Image.parse` at `runtime.zig:208+` bounds-checks lazily via `try` at each `word()`, which is exactly the validate-while-using shape `negative:14` replaced). It also silently owns, or fails to own, version and tier refusal; see missing edges.

## Wrong boundaries and missing dependency edges

**G1 cannot be finalised before the region-kind census is, and the census is set by G2, G5, G16, and G17.** The canon names seven lent region kinds (residual, frames/environment, values, handler scratch, N expansion buffers, CR1 budget, diagnostics budget; `positive:45,33,25,39`); the shipped descriptor lends two (`vehje-runtime-abi/src/entry.rs:36-48`). ## Sketches that would not prove their gate

**G1.** A sketch that replaces the ~96 KiB of entry-point arrays (`runtime.zig:529-542`) with a five-field lent descriptor passes trivially and proves nothing about the actual risk, which is kind-extensibility. The sketch must add a region kind after the descriptor is defined and show no ABI break and no recompile of the host contract. Without that, G1 goes green and re-opens at G5, G16, and G17 in turn.

**G6.** The bar "OPS carries no empty entries and no compound operation has a hand-written Zig body" is satisfiable by moving the five hand-authored bodies (`eval.zig:483+`) behind a Zig-authored table of function pointers: data in name, nominal binding in a new coat. The property that matters is that a constructor's meaning is carried in the emitted signature data and is evaluable at every discharge site, which is only checkable once the meaning crosses, or is expressed in, the same vocabulary the signature carries. As written the gate can pass its proof with the meaning still living in Zig.

**G7.** A crossing sketch that emits a generated `pub const OPS = ...` file would satisfy "OPS is derived, not written" while deciding the crossing-form question (byte image versus generated const) that is op's. A WORKS on one form will be read as the decision; that is refusal-laundering by sketch. The sketch spec must either wait for the op call or run both forms and hand the choice back with the measurements.

**G8.** Hash-keyed agreement between the bytes Rust emitted and the bytes Zig consumed proves transport integrity, not emission correctness: it certifies the loader did not corrupt the package, and says nothing about whether the emitter produced bytes that mean the signature. That second property is what the mechanism-open design work (round-trip, lens, typed manifest) exists for. A G8 that lands the hash check and declares the seam gated has narrowed the trusted seam to "the emitter plus the hash function" and must say so; a version that claims more would pass its proof and be wrong in exactly the `negative:27` way, one layer up.

**G2.** The Wingo sketch spec carries a cost bar (per-node cost within noise of the current 4.4 ns) that the roadmap's gate does not. Per `bench-in-bench-harness-never-sketches.md` that half is a harness cell, not a sketch. Split them explicitly: the sketch proves constant native depth at 1e6 and the two adversarial programs; the cell proves the cost. A sketch-only G2 could pass with a 2x-slower walk and silently make a design decision the canon says is bench-decided.

**G16's buildable half.** The CPS/defunctionalisation cell run "now" must contain an actual handler and an actual perform. The two artifacts currently wearing the multi-shot label measure a nested loop and an arithmetic delta (verified: `multishot-scaling/ms.zig` is an odometer over a pure function with a 160 MB page-allocator buffer; `hx_multishot` differs from its `__single` twin by an inner arithmetic loop). The gate's bench spec needs the contains-a-continuation guard stated, or PE2's "run now" gets satisfied by a third artifact of the same class.

## What the roadmap has structurally wrong

**The tracks are organised by which expert saw the item, not by dependency.** Track A is the Wingo read, B/C the Rompf read, D the graded spine, E the language, F the benches. The synthesis's whole contribution was showing the two reads name one interlocked structure; the roadmap re-partitioned it back along the author seams and then asserted the partitions independent, which is how G2/G7 ended up as two rewrites of one function and G5 ended up in a track that cannot contain it. Redraw the tracks by dependency and most of the missing edges above draw themselves.

**One status word covers three different states.** UNPROVEN currently means any of: design settled, sketch owed (G1, G2, G4); design open, op call owed before a sketch is even specifiable (G6's coverage question, G8's mechanism, G11/G12/G13's locus, G14's locus, G18's module system); and merely unbuilt with no open question (parts of G15). A TODO-sketch column over the second class is unactionable: you cannot write a sketch hypothesis for a gate whose design question is unanswered, and an agent that tries will resolve the op call by construction. Add a DESIGN-OPEN tag and route those gates to rounds, not sketches.

**Precision falls off with distance and the roadmap does not say so.** G1 through G10 carry cites, bars, and statuses; G19 through G23 carry eleven words each and the same status vocabulary. Either size the far gates or mark them as unsized placeholders pending op's track-sequencing answer. A reader (or a dispatching agent) cannot currently tell a specified gate from a caption.

**One dropped citation drift, minor.** G17 cites the census's macro-axis paragraph at `:63`; it is at `202607251530:65`. The fact stands; the pointer does not, and this round has spent enough on pointers that do not resolve.

## Where the canon is genuinely silent

Op's calls, exposed by the granularity pass, additional to the roadmap's own list of nine. Tradeoffs stated; no preference.

1. **Whether the operation vocabulary must cover introduction forms, or nominal binding to hand-authored code remains admissible.** The four-way findings hand this to the lead designer explicitly (`findings.md:84-85`); G6's acceptance bar currently answers it by default. Covering constructors keeps one-definition across the census's widest escape at the cost of a larger primitive set and a harder admissibility proof; hand-authored constructors keep the vocabulary small and write every compound value's meaning once per artifact.
2. **The seam-crossing form at the Zig build.** A byte image decoded at comptime keeps one wire format and the byte-image hash as identity, at ~27 us/record comptime decode cost; a generated data-only const file is free at comptime and trivially auditable, at the cost of a second emitter surface and Zig syntax inside the `positive:29` trusted base, under a `negative:37` letter that kills engine-emission and does not clearly speak to data-only files.
3. **Environment reclamation's trust story.** A compile-stage reach proof trusted for runtime memory safety enlarges the `positive:29` rim and must be added to its enumeration by a round; a runtime liveness check keeps the rim as-is and pays per binding. G3 cannot be specified until this is picked.
4. **Whether a resumption may cross the C ABI.** If yes: a continuation handle, a re-entry point, and the end of the retains-nothing contract, buying async host effects; if no: host-serviced operations are abort-only forever and the resumable discharge stays internal. Constrains G16's representation and the G1 descriptor, and wants deciding before the version word is spent.
5. **The loop encoding's ratification.** Recursive-binding-plus-exit-effect (census-derived, unratified) versus a loop Core form. The encoding keeps twelve forms and makes G3's machinery load-bearing; a form makes loops trivially bounded and reopens the form set. `negative:30` fixes the method (by algebra), not the answer.
6. **The module system's shape.** A load-time module graph puts resolution at HostLoader grade and interacts with the lattice; bundle-time concatenation with namespaces is simpler and worse; the sketch's text concatenation is neither. Sizes G18 and part of G14.
7. **One generic table-reading core, one comptime-specialised binary per language, or both.** `positive:45`'s letter says comptime-specialised; call 9 licenses the generic core. Specialisation buys the measured near-free dispatch and full inlining at the cost of one binary per language and a full engine rebuild per definition edit; the generic core is one binary for everything at the cost of load-time decode and indirect dispatch; both doubles the surface that must agree, which is the exact seam G8 gates. The roadmap's open question 5 gestures at this via the certification perimeter; the artifact question is broader than the mechanism question and both are op's.
