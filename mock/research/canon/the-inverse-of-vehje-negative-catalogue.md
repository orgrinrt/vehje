# The inverse of vehje: the negative catalogue

**Status:** canon. Companion to `the-soul-of-vehje-positive-catalogue.md`. This document is the inverse: what was well-intended, sometimes proudly stated, and did not survive. A claim is here for one of two reasons: a better composition superseded its shape, or a measurement overturned its choice. A third, smaller part records how a bench lied, so the same error is not remade, and a fourth records the honest gap between what a doc claimed and what shipped, so surface existence never again stands in for a satisfied mandate.

The one discipline governing this catalogue: **chronology, and the latest evidence.** A proud line in an early topic still lands here if a later, more rigorous re-measure or audit overruled it. The same fact ("eqsat is the genuine research keystone," "copy-and-patch is the keystone accelerator," "the runtime is one relational engine under all three analyses") can be soul when written and inverse now. Each entry sits where the latest evidence puts it, cited to where the evidence is, not where the enthusiasm was. A living conviction (the positive catalogue) is one that survived both filters; this is everything that did not.

## Part A: killed by construction (a better composition superseded the shape)

These are mechanism kills from the design-adjudication trail; they stay killed.

- **shared-implies-promoted.** Promote every shared value node to the root region. Killed: it is the ML Kit region leak the reachability paradigm was adopted to avoid. Replaced by Perceus exact-meet (a shared node's lease is the meet of its referrers' reach-sets, finalised at emission by a reversed referrer count).
- **copy-everything.** Copy every leased shared value to dodge sharing. Killed: exponential blowup on the huge nested values the transfer model exists to carry.
- **PE-as-extraction.** Model partial evaluation as e-graph extraction. Killed as a false label: extraction is a preference not a well-formedness constraint, the mix equation is an equation not an optimisation, and saturating an inlining rule diverges, so a no-alloc cap would make lowering non-deterministic. Replaced by a well-founded graded unfold that terminates by binding-time grade well-foundedness, not by a cap, deterministic by confluence on the static fragment. This is the terminator precedent that governs the positive catalogue's item G: termination is well-foundedness; op's recursion-depth cap is only a hang-guard diagnostic, never cap-as-semantics.
- **tnum-for-structure.** Cast the whole load check as one depth-bounded tnum abstract interpretation. Killed on both domain and cost: tnum is non-relational and cannot express `child_index < arena_size`, and abstract-interpretation cost is program-size not nesting-depth. Replaced by a parse-don't-validate typed structural decode (simdjson lineage); tnum kept for the numeric residual only.
- **the depth-cap pun.** One depth cap doing six jobs. Killed: two of the six were a width or a length wearing the word "depth." Replaced by three genuine finite structural bounds (environment-width, nesting-depth, input-length), each real, AARA-inferable, folded into the graded spine. The *aesthetic* (find the shape where one decision buys many) survives as soul; this instance of it was a pun, and it is dead.
- **the Beck-distributive-law hunt for effect-coeffect.** Killed: no distributive law is owed; a read then a write is graded def-use (sequential composition), which is Scala 3 capture-plus-separation shipping.
- **one-one-shot-coroutine as the whole streaming substrate.** Killed: the shared substrate is one level up, a semi-naive differential monotone-fixpoint. Note the tension the positive catalogue carries forward: this kill's justification leaned on equality saturation being a live multi-shot consumer, and eqsat is now itself parked (Part E). So the relational-fixpoint substrate must be re-justified on its surviving consumers alone (lease inference and load verification), not on eqsat; a justification that leaned on a now-parked consumer must not ride into canon unexamined.
- **scannerless PEG by default / depth-bounded packrat memo.** Killed: PEG's silent shadowing and GLL's silent ambiguity are the wrong default for independently authored family grammars. Replaced by inclusion-not-coverage grammar composition with declared-and-detected conflicts via Brzozowski derivatives.
- **the original full-pipeline proposal's asserted synergies.** Four synergies asserted, three of them puns. Replaced by convergence under independent attack: construction where there had been assertion.

## Part B: a generation behind (superseded by a better recombination that was already in the banked citations)

Each was well-cited and correct on its own terms, and is dead in favour of its right.

- **unify-by-analogy (a filing system) -> unify-by-construction (grading).** A filing system gives you one vocabulary; it does not give you one theorem. (This exact slip recurred in the panel: the positive fork's early "the four commitments are shadows of one idea" was unify-by-analogy, copied from the idealistic synthesis, and was corrected; see Part H.)
- **lease-as-effect -> lease-as-coeffect.** A per-operand usage grade, not a per-op effect flag; the effect flag loses per-operand resolution.
- **the differential gate on the kernel -> translation-validation on the emitter seam.** The kernel is one source compiled twice, so the two binding times agreeing is near-tautology; the trust actually leaks at the Rust-to-Zig emitter, which nothing gated. The intent to close that seam is living (positive catalogue F); the mechanism is open. This is exactly the layer where the per-slice manifest-hash unsoundness lives.
- **binding-time-as-a-chain -> binding-time-as-a-lattice** over the four knowledge sources, because a partly-bundled partly-arriving script is a join, not a point on a chain.
- **value-kinds as a parallel closed set -> the introduction-form projection** of the one algebra.
- **eleven-forms-complete-by-census -> twelve-forms-complete-by-algebra** (`Handle` added by algebra, not by another census cut).
- **certified-as-a-binary -> certified-as-a-grade** (assurance a dial).

## Part C: identity errors, both directions, and the locus drift

- **The drift:** narrowing vehje to a single-purpose JIT-and-native scripting runtime with a game engine as its reason to exist. Rejected: the tech was right, the identity slipped; vehje serves a census and the authoring end is first-class.
- **The over-compensation:** dropping the native endgame, demoting JIT and copy-and-patch, treating the last topics as wrong turns. Rejected as firmly as the drift it reacted to. Both directions are dead; keep the whole shape, remove only the narrow framing.
- **"Rust emits Zig source"** (a Rust metaprogram text-printing the specialised engine). Killed at topic 1627 as the hard-to-certify LMS-hard route; Rust emits data, Zig comptime specialises.
- **The "dual-locus" framing** (a Rust-side per-script analysis locus; one generator emitting a specialised Rust half and a specialised Zig half). Killed: Rust never runs per-script analyses, the runtime does, for all scripts; and the Rust-emits-Zig half is the rejected LMS route.
- **Two stale 1513 lines:** "there is no native endgame" (wrong: there is one, ours and method-agnostic) and "generate Zig and let the Zig compiler own the platforms" (stale: the native path is not locked to Zig).
- **The old canon "vehje is a general-purpose scripting language for game modding."** Corrected 2026-07-19: that language is one consumer.

## Part D: re-tiering / YAGNI-demotion, rejected at the frame

Carmack's remedy for every hard piece was the same shape: move the ambitious unified mechanism to a "later north star" and put a simpler local mechanism on the critical path so the floor ships (one relational engine becomes three analyses sharing a bitmask; eqsat becomes hash-consing plus a specializer; the unified logical relation becomes a skeleton; AARA becomes three constants). Rejected as YAGNI in sheep's clothing and the exact reasoning this workspace exists to reject. Both halves are named so neither is re-trusted: op's own earlier relay of the re-tier, and the re-tier itself. The concrete faults were kept as the design agenda; the remedy was not. Some of what this remedy wanted to demote, the benches later demoted anyway (Part E), but on evidence, not on "it is more work": you demote on a measurement, never on effort.

## Part E: overturned by measurement (the sharpest layer)

The consolidation topic locked several numbers its own round's later, PMU-armed, panel-audited re-run had already revised. Chronology governs; the PMU re-run and the full-arc findings are the latest word.

- **equality saturation, from "the one genuinely original research piece" to OUT.** Marginal contribution to the full pipeline is zero-or-negative everywhere; `cse+eqsat` is bit-identical in node count to `eqsat` (CSE recovers nothing on top); standalone it inflates a case 5.6x (the mechanism behind the first run's misreported "142x win," which was eqsat-alone pessimisation presented as a speedup). Ship fold+CSE+DCE always-on; park eqsat / DAG-aware extraction behind a named trigger. This is the single biggest gap between what the topics proudly kept and what the evidence supports, and per op (XVII9) it is moved from soul to inverse.
- **tail-call threading, flipped.** Imported on the dynamic-language literature ("beats LuaJIT hand-asm by 31%"); measured slower for vehje's ~25-primitive flat IR (switch fastest on straight line; threaded ~1.12-1.17x slower) because the switch branch is well-predicted. It wins only on real control flow (the trace cell ~0.44x of switch), so it is reserved for CFG terminator transfers, not the default. The flat "switch beats threading 1.7x" of the consolidation is the stale form; per op (XVII9) it is moved from soul to inverse. Read the rejection as scoped, not blanket.
- **copy-and-patch as THE keystone accelerator, demoted.** Direct instruction selection ties it warm within 1% and compiles ~2x cheaper, so for the short-lived residuals the authoring majority produces, direct isel is the native point. The native ceiling over the best interpreter is a modest range, not a 10x keystone; the 10x class needs a vectorising JIT copy-and-patch cannot be. Per op (XVII9), the privileged-copy-and-patch framing moves from soul to inverse.
- **the stack-bytecode middle tier, closed.** Register beats stack every profile (1.41x-3.06x); the middle tier is redefined as predecoded-register plus fold/CSE/DCE.
- **the 24-byte three-operand record, washed to a null result.** Record width within 1% at every size (pick REC16 as a density choice); only "operands stay inline" survives, because pool indirection is the measured expensive part.
- **eval-all branch strategy under interpretation, worst everywhere** (up to 4x the field); one global branch shape is wrong because the ranking is tier-dependent.
- **semi-naive delta for the lease fixpoint, beaten by whole-column** on realistic shallow graphs (delta bookkeeping not repaid in two rounds).
- **CSE for speed, a non-result.** LLVM already CSEs the recompute; CSE is kept for a 76% node-count reduction, not latency.
- **cells deliberately not built, with reasons.** Perfect-hash dispatch degenerates to the function-pointer table on a dense opcode set; interned operands cannot win on a value-DAG where operand tuples do not repeat. Named absences, because a non-representative cell is worse than a named absence.

## Part F: how a bench lied (measurement precedent, not a design kill)

Carried so the same error is not remade.

- **The ABI first-pass over-claim.** "W>=8 threshold" was circular (the SoA cell ran its scalar remainder below its 8-lane width, so at W<8 the SoA cell IS the scalar path); the "2.4x" was dispatch-amortisation plus partial NEON, not an ABI property; the decisive cheap-payload regime was never built on the first pass; ~12% sequential thermal drift confounded the cross-W magnitudes. Corrected to: crossing ~9 ns and effectively free, the SoA 2.3x-4.1x vectorisation win the real justification, expose one runtime-W batched entry at W>=2.
- **Inverted native cell tags.** The cell tagged `copypatch` was direct instruction-selection; the cell tagged `stencil` was the real Xu-Kjolstad copy-and-patch (OOPSLA 2021, not PLDI); a summary finding inherited the inversion.
- **The cost-model k-sweep, the design's own "central measurement," was never built.** Everything shipped is a single warm point at k=16, so every setup cost S is hidden and the tier breakeven k* is unknowable. Owed.
- **vert8 6x to 4.8x** (the scalar baseline ran the wire interpreter, not the predecoded one, so ~20% of the "win" was decode asymmetry), and the native wins were measured against the plain rather than the best interpreter.
- **The Futamura-artifact trap, the deepest of these.** The naive native-ceiling bench measured the framework's own central mechanism (partial evaluation, the first Futamura projection) as a measurement artifact, because the compiler specialised the interpreter into native and the bench compared native to native; the honest re-measure required forcing the workload across an opacity boundary. The framework's own soul can hide inside its own benchmark; cross the opacity boundary or be fooled by it.
- **The prototype-versus-shipped-crate slip.** The 8M-node / 16M-edge / ~50 ms fixpoint figure that justified the relational engine measured a standalone Zig prototype, not the shipped Rust `Engine`. The number is real and drove a real decision, but "measured" must carry which artifact it measured; an algorithm-level bench certifying a shipped-crate claim is the same category slip as the Futamura trap, one notch smaller. The assurance ladder now tags measured-prototype versus measured-shipped for exactly this.

## Part G: uncertain, with negative precedent (not a definitive no; may return)

Named so reaching for one is a deliberate, evidenced choice.

- **eqsat / DAG-aware extraction.** Out as a shipped default, parked behind a named trigger. A consumer whose profile the optimiser genuinely helps, with a machine-and-effect-aware extraction objective (not pure rewrite-count, which can pessimise), could re-open it; the no-alloc slotted-and-colored e-graph remains genuine systems work.
- **copy-and-patch native.** Demoted, not deleted; feasibility proven at the mechanism level. Warm-parity with direct isel; a run-many-times residual whose compile cost amortises could still justify it once the unmeasured k* breakeven is measured.
- **AARA / full potential analysis.** Deprioritised to three constants when nothing load-bearing needed it, re-opened by CR1's bounded-multi-shot budget-fit, the first thing that genuinely needs a resource bound. Negative-then-positive; live again.
- **heap / full multi-shot continuations.** Excluded in favour of the no-alloc bounded CR1 form; a genuine backtracking need could reopen them only behind an explicit escape. And note the sharper current reading (fresh audit): no census consumer backtracks today, so even CR1's multi-shot half has no current customer; its representation is being benched now per op, and the novelty claim rides that result.
- **tail-threading dispatch.** Negative for the straight-line default, positive for CFG terminator transfers. Do not read the straight-line rejection as blanket.
- **the native-as-a-tier framing.** The word "tier" applied to native is dead; native as a spectrum-point is alive.
- **a specific record width / operand count.** Null on the Python-stdlib proxy corpus; a real census corpus, when consumers exist, could re-justify a particular inline count. Measure-then-lock is owed.
- **N host-provided allocation spaces for the IR-to-IR expansion.** Per op (XVII7) this supersedes the older single-versus-double-arena binary: not two, but N, benched to find where multi-buffer wins, host-configurable once proven, the `NodeRef` cross-arena branding decided with it. No precedent yet; the bench is owed and named here so it is on the record.

## Part H: claimed but not built (surface existence standing in for mandate satisfaction)

The fresh audits found a class the design-versus-design panels structurally could not: present-tense doc prose contradicted by shipped source, where a type or a crate exists (so the doc-source-mismatch lint passes on the noun) but does not do what its doc says. This is not a kill list of ideas; it is the honest ledger of the design-versus-shipped gap, kept so no reader mistakes a finished-looking doc for a satisfied mandate. The root cause, named precisely, is surface existence standing in for mandate satisfaction; the fix is behaviour-shaped catalogued red tests, not more prose, because red tests scale and adversarial prose audits do not (the workspace had exactly one catalogued red test against the audit's twenty-three rows).

- **vehje-schedule is dissolved.** It was a DAG scheduler behind a finished DESIGN, over a compile pipeline of four statically-ordered passes with no consumer family pass in existence; `Pass` is an empty trait, `PassDag` a unit struct, `Schedule::build()` returns success unconditionally. Per op (PE1), verified against source (no `vehje_schedule::` use anywhere, the canon never referenced it): it is redundant, and pass scheduling is really a runtime concern, not something the Rust compile side needs. The crate is deleted, the four-pass sequence hardcoded in `vehje::run`, the unused dep dropped; it is re-addable if a consumer ever registers a family pass whose ordering is non-obvious.
- **The graded spine is two axes, not four.** `binding` is hard-coded `Knowledge::empty()` and `assurance` hard-coded `Unclaimed`; no soundness theorem exists as an artifact. The "one theorem" is intent until an axis-interaction rule exists in code with a red test. (Positive catalogue D states this honestly; it is repeated here because the candidates once read it as shipped.)
- **check reads no signature schema; runtime-gen ignores the signature; Anf/MacroExpand are no-ops; the CFG residual types are defined and never constructed; vehje-fixpoint has no in-tree callers; the distribution-composition entry point is claimed with zero implementation.** Each is intended, not shipped; each is owed a FIXME and a red test; several are already re-tensed or FIXME'd in source after the second-direction audit.
- **Two silent-correctness bugs in the small amount of code that exists, direction-shaped not wiring-shaped.** First, the verification pass discards a failure: `check` drops `GradeTable::set`'s `Bool(false)` return when the grade region is undersized (`vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:314`), so grades are silently dropped, `check` still returns `Ok`, and a `Graded` is minted over ungraded nodes, a silent-truncation hole in the exact pass whose identity is "the type system is the verification layer." Per op (PE3), the fix is to make the illegal state unbuildable: a size-refusing grade-region constructor and a `must_use` outcome. Second, `infer` and `structurally_equal` recurse on IR depth, violating the project's own bench-proven "defunctionalization is the only route" rule, so a deep-skewed 50k-node program overflows the host stack in exactly the embedded contexts an embeddable framework courts. Both are catalogued as red tests, then fixed, in the IMPL rounds.

## The through-line

The soul is compose-proven-pieces-until-a-new-shape-supersedes-them, and choose by measurement. The inverse is therefore exactly this: the shapes a better composition superseded (A, B, C, D), the choices a measurement overturned (E), the ways a measurement lied (F), the mechanisms a measurement parked but did not kill (G), and the honest gap between what a doc claimed and what shipped (H). A living conviction survived all of these. The most important standing correction this catalogue hands forward: the design record, however audited, does not verify code; only a bench or a red test does, so every canon-grade claim earns its status verb and, where the claim is about behaviour, its executable, or it belongs in Part H until it does.
