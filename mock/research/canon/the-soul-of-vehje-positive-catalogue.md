# The soul of vehje: the positive catalogue

**Status:** canon. This document and its companion (`the-inverse-of-vehje-negative-catalogue.md`) are the two catalogues of what vehje is and is not, written from scratch and synthesised from the whole record: the canonical consolidation round, the ir-to-ir panel, the two fresh expert audits (Lattner, Torvalds), and op's ratifications and post-audit calls (`op-ratification-answers.md`, batches 1 through 8). Where an earlier document and this one disagree, this one governs.

**Precedence (narrowed, per op PE4).** This canon's *letter* governs. It is amendable only by a superseding round, never by an appeal to its spirit. The older rule that "the spirit outranks the letter" is retained only for its original job: reading *stale intermediate artifacts*, where a line written early may no longer carry the intent that evolved past it. It is not a licence over this canon. The drift test (Section 12) is the letter; when a proposal is questioned, it is checked against the letter, and the letter is changed by process.

**Assurance verbs.** Every claim below carries its epistemic status, because a design document that reads as fact what is only intent is how a stub survives behind a finished-looking doc. The ladder: **proven** (a checked theorem or a type-level guarantee that exists), **demonstrated** (a sketch or prototype shows it works), **measured (prototype)** or **measured (shipped)** (a committed bench cell, tagged by which artifact it ran against), **intended** (designed, not yet built). A bench number certifies only the artifact it ran; a prototype figure never silently certifies a shipped crate.

**Benches are provisional (op, batch 7).** Trust the data for exactly what it measured; extrapolate no further proof than it proves. The current benches are rudimentary relative to what better, more representative ones will show; building those is standing work. "Bench decides a which-is-faster fork" and "the bench is the last word forever" are different claims; only the first is canon.

## 1. The centre of gravity

vehje turns a language definition into certified data rather than a compiler. It discharges every operation it knows, an effect, a host-call, a macro, or a proof obligation, with one handler discipline at the earliest binding time that operation's inputs are known; it proves each discharge statically and then erases it; and what remains ships as validated data. That data is one signature projected into two certified artifacts, which a single general runtime specialises to itself across a spectrum of outputs from interpretation to native, with no heap and no collector.

That single idea is the fusion of three true things a reader should be able to recover from it. It is *data, not a compiler* (nothing that ships carries a prover or emits source). It is *discharge at the earliest binding time* (the one mechanism that draws the compile-versus-runtime line, unifies effects with macros with host-calls, and makes staging a build step rather than a JIT). And it is *prove-then-erase* (the type system is the verification layer, the runtime a dumb evaluator of proven-safe programs). The rest of this catalogue is that idea held at maximal shape, grounded on the real consumer census, and settled wherever it is a which-is-faster question by measurement.

## 2. The frontier: the genuinely novel and hard-won (the foreground)

This is what vehje learnt that is not sitting finished in another system. op's instruction (batch 4) is explicit: these are the highlight; the foundational identity statements in Section 3 are the base they rest on, not the headline. Each carries its status.

**A. The compile/runtime line is a four-point binding-time lattice, and const-inlining is the first Futamura projection staged.** The "line" between compile and runtime is not a line; it is the lattice `{LanguageAuthor, Bundler, HostLoader, Runtime}`, already in the IR (`vehje-ir/src/grade.rs:105-115`, verified by two independent reads). Ahead-of-time work lives at LanguageAuthor (specialising the general Zig runtime to the language definition at our build, which is the first Futamura projection as a build step, the technique weval demonstrates for wasm interpreters in production) and at Bundler (the Rust side inlining a known constant). Load-time compilation, not profile-guided JIT, lives at HostLoader. Interpretation is a Runtime execution form of the already-specialised residual. Literal const-inlining therefore mandates a specialisation *stage* at the binding time the input becomes known, never a native step: the pipeline is coherent per-stage and incoherent across the whole. **Proven** as the axis (the lattice is in code and an independent staging read reconstructed it); the discharge machinery is **intended**.

**B. One handler discipline the system itself is agnostic about, over three discharges.** An effect is an operation; a handler is what services it; a host-call is a runtime-effect operation the host handles; macro expansion is a compile-stage-effect operation the compile stage handles. So the build-versus-runtime split is not drawn by hand: it is "which stage provides the handler," which is the binding-time coordinate. The twelfth Core form `Handle` (Plotkin-Pretnar, adapted no-alloc) carries it. The unifying frame op wants stated (PE / XVII6): `Handle` is *one dispatch discipline the system has no opinion about*; the three discharges (compile-time IR emit, runtime value-return, runtime resumable continuation) are implementation realisations of that one dispatch, differing only in operational feasibility. Generative freshness (Section 2, item on hygiene) is a property of the generative discharge under that one discipline. **Intended**; the first discharge is the macro-expansion path (owed), the third is the hard one (C).

**C. CR1: no-alloc bounded multi-shot continuations on a host-lent budget.** `Handle` is designed to support multi-shot resumption without a heap, via a host-lent bounded budget with a static fit-proof. This is the sharpest original contribution and it carries its scope honestly. **Demonstrated:** bounded multi-shot by *re-execution* of a pure defunctionalised continuation (re-run a pure comptime function per choice; unconditionally no-alloc; the McCarthy way to do bounded search). **Intended and unproven:** continuation *reinstatement* without a heap (prefix sharing is the real capture problem), and multi-shot under other effects in the handled body (a soundness obligation, since re-execution is only sound over a pure prefix; effect-subtraction is a FIXME at `vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:301-303`). The runtime has no continuation model yet. Per op (PE2): the continuation *representation* is a bench-decided fork run **now** (segmented capture-and-reinstate over a host-lent budget versus compile-time CPS/defunctionalisation of the handled body; measure capture cost, resume cost, ABI shape, under `mock/benches/`). The novelty claim ("no shipping effect system does no-alloc bounded multi-shot this way") is earned only if heap-free reinstatement lands; the canon states it scoped until the bench resolves it.

**D. One graded (co)modal proof spine, unified by construction.** The static proof axes plus binding time and assurance are one graded judgment: binding time a graded necessity modality (Davies-Pfenning), effects a graded monad (Katsumata), lease/usage a coeffect whose grade is a reachability qualifier (Petricek-Orchard-Mycroft; Bao et al. reachability types the primary lease paradigm), assurance itself a grade, combined by the graded adjunction (Gaboardi et al. 2016; Granule the implementability evidence). One soundness theorem; the per-axis results are its corollaries and the axis interactions are its typing rules. **The status is exact and load-bearing:** of the four grade coordinates, `effect` and `lease` are computed by the join fold in the shipped check; `binding` and `assurance` are hard-coded constants (`Knowledge::empty()`, `Assurance::Unclaimed`, `vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:311-312`). So the spine is **intended**, not proven: today it is a vocabulary over two live axes, and it earns the word "theorem" the day an axis-interaction rule exists in code with a red test that exercises it. The unification's payoff is precisely at those axis interactions, which the templating majority barely exercises; the canon carries the spine as designed, prices the unbuilt half honestly, and does not present a filing system as a theorem.

**E. Certified generation: data not source, doubly certified, certifying not certified, assurance a dial.** The proof is discharged in Rust before any lowering, then compiled into two artifacts that carry no prover. Rust emits validated *data*, never Zig *source* (the LMS-hard, hard-to-certify route, rejected at topic 1627). Both artifacts are certified at *our* build, neither at the consumer. The certification is stated with its perimeter, per Leroy's trusted-rim discipline: rustc certifies well-formedness, inclusion, and grading of the emitted data; Zig comptime certifies the specialisation type-checks against that data; **neither certifies semantic preservation of any lowering.** The trusted base is named, not scattered: the Zig compiler, rustc, the C ABI marshalling, the cross-artifact byte-image hash, the still-open Rust-to-Zig emitter seam, the caller-supplied sets, and the load verifier's decode. "Certifying" (per-instance re-checkable, `Assurance::ReprovenPerInstance`) is distinct from "certified"; assurance is a dial per property (static / structural / re-proven-per-instance / dynamically-checked / tested / unclaimed), composing by lattice meet, so proof and the differential test are one object at two grades. **Intended**, with the trusted-rim enumeration **proven** as a written perimeter (a certification claim without a stated perimeter is marketing; with one it is engineering).

**F. One signature, many projections; no trusted emitter.** One declarative signature (a graded algebraic signature carrying its own grammar) is the single source; the Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig runtime consts are lens projections of it (Foster et al.), differential-checked. The node algebra itself has three projections: the eliminator IR you fold, the introduction value-domain (content-as-values), and the introduction-compiled stencils. The identity boundary is stated precisely, because conflating the two hashes is unsound: *within-stage* identity is the structural word-fold hash (interner-local); *cross-artifact* identity is the byte-image hash. The trust seam is the Rust-to-Zig emitter; closing it (round-trip validation, or the lens projection, or a self-checking typed manifest) is **intended**, the mechanism open. Today the check pass reads no `vehje-signature` schema (zero uses; the wiring is owed), so "one signature drives the projections" is **intended**, not shipped.

**G. IR-to-IR staged expansion: N host-lent arenas, recursive macros, well-founded termination.** Macro and const expansion is a node-creating catamorphism over host-provided allocation spaces. Per op (XVII7): the arena strategy is not a single-versus-double binary but **N host-provided allocation spaces**, benched to find where multiple buffers win (independent expansion loops in parallel where they do not touch, or scratchpad storage), host-configurable once proven, our end adapting to whatever the host provides; the `NodeRef` cross-arena branding decision rides that same bench. Recursive macro expansion is **supported** (op corrected the panel here). Its termination is, in three layers: stage well-foundedness terminates the stage dimension (binding-time grade decreases); a within-stage per-operation productivity condition lives as data on the operation, not as a convention; and against infinite recursion, static cyclicity detection on the Rust typestate if it proves feasible (a sketch is owed), else a **host-configurable recursion-depth cap, default 255**, as an always-on safety backstop. That cap is categorically distinct from the two killed caps: it is a hang-guard diagnosed as a named error, not cap-as-semantics (which residual you get must never depend on the cap) and not the six-jobs depth-cap pun. Termination is by well-foundedness; the cap only refuses to hang. **Intended** (the `Anf` and `MacroExpand` passes are FIXME no-ops today).

**H. What the benches taught, held as current evidence not eternal law.** The value is in the cheap binding-time-directed reducer and the vertical-SIMD batched ABI, not in the e-graph or the copy-and-patch stencil. Specifically, all **measured**: the batched-column C ABI (one runtime-W entry, W >= 2) is the single biggest lever, vertical/SoA SIMD ~4.8x with the FFI crossing effectively free (~9 ns); the optimiser is fold + CSE + DCE always-on (CSE kept for a 76% node-count reduction, not latency; the e-graph parked behind a named trigger at zero-or-negative marginal); dispatch is tier-and-shape-dependent (plain switch fastest for the lean ~25-primitive straight-line IR, preserve-none threading winning only on real control flow, the CFG terminator transfers); native is a modest scalar multiplier, a **range** with its baseline index (~1.5x over the best predecoded interpreter, ~2.0x over switch, the spread owed a reconciling cell), never the driver, with the 10x class reserved for a vectorising JIT the authoring majority does not need; direct instruction selection compiles ~2x cheaper than the copy-and-patch stencil at equal warm speed, so it is the native point. The capstone: a 50k-node script runs end to end in ~3.4 ms, compile-dominated (3.24 ms) with a trivial runtime (127 us), which is exactly the authoring-majority profile. The meta-lesson is soul: a bench that measures the wrong thing is worse than none, and the framework's own central mechanism (partial evaluation) can hide inside its own benchmark, so a workload must cross an opacity boundary or the number is a fiction.

**I. The maximal-shape, unify-by-construction ethos, with its honesty valve.** Design the unification completely before code; a hole is filled by finishing the design, not by choosing a design with fewer holes because it is cheaper to reach today. Reject re-tiering the ambitious mechanism off the critical path as the defer instinct (a sound floor is not a sufficient floor). Compose established pieces until a new shape falls out that supersedes them; a design that congratulates itself on reaching the field's standard answer has told you exactly where the missed recombination is; unify by construction (one theorem), never by analogy (one vocabulary). **The valve is equally load-bearing:** where a piece genuinely cannot be built under the constraints, prove the exact blocking constraint and design the maximal thing that *does* hold, never a smaller architecture chosen for being easier. And the ethos only stays honest paired with the assurance ladder: design-first without a status verb on every claim is how a locked, finished-looking mechanism reads as built for rounds. Ethos and verb ladder are one matched pair.

**J. op's fresh convictions (2026-07-24).** Two, both first-class. **N-buffer host-lent resource adaptivity:** do not fix the allocation-space count; explore N, find where multi-buffer wins, expose host configuration once proven, and adapt to whatever the host provides. **Diagnostics first-class:** informative errors *and* actionable suggestions with span links that integrate with LSP, everywhere, not just at one seam. This is a standing conviction, and on a no-alloc substrate it is real architecture (message formatting, suggestion synthesis, and span mapping all want buffers the discipline forbids), so diagnostics get their own host-lent budget discipline, designed as deliberately as everything else, before the conviction becomes the first place an allocator is smuggled in. **Intended.**

## 3. The base: the foundational identity the frontier rests on

These are ratified and stable; they are the ground, not the highlight.

vehje is a framework, not a language. A grammar plugs into the input side and lowers a surface syntax into the shared IR; a target plugs into the output side, declares what it supports, and emits. Every language is a consumer, the rich general-purpose one included, on equal footing with jomini, w3, ts4, lua, polka, ikiuni, viola, and mockspace. It serves a census of consumers, and the authoring-and-templating majority (mockspace documents, polka dotfiles) is first-class; the game runtime (ikiuni) is one consumer, not the reason vehje exists. The census is a **needs-signal**: we expect and match the consumers' needs, we do not design vehje *for* any one of them (op, VII3); the design is driven by benches, prior art, and the maximal shape. It ships two artifacts with Rust in only one: a dev-time Rust language compiler (emits validated data and structural proofs, never sees an end-user script, never ships) and a shipped Zig composed runtime (one hand-authored engine comptime-specialised to the Rust-emitted data). It refuses by inclusion, not coverage: a target declares the family set it `Supports` and the effect set it `Permits`, and anything outside is refused with the offending construct named, never silently degraded. It has no garbage collector and no null (absence is an explicit `Maybe` value the consumer matches, not a nullable reference), and no heap on either side (`no_std`, no alloc, host-owned memory via caller-lent arenas and host-lent budgets; PE3: this is an axiom on both sides, with the ergonomic and safety costs named and their mitigations mandated, `must_use` and size-refusing constructors so an undersized region cannot be built, and defunctionalised non-recursive passes). It is strict by design: an inference failure is a compile error forcing an explicit annotation, never a silent fallback. Output is a spectrum from interpretation to transpilation, and native is a real point on it, method-open, not a privileged tier. The bare word "codegen" is retired for three named operations: runtime generation (per language, dev time), macro expansion (IR to IR, per script, compile stage), and output generation (IR to output, per script). And the Core changes to fit the tech, never the reverse.

## 4. The governing ethos, stated plainly

Design the whole unification before code, through the hard parts, or prove the exact blocking constraint and design the maximal thing that holds. Reject re-tiering the ambitious mechanism off the critical path; demote only on a measurement, never on "it is more work." Compose proven pieces until a new shape supersedes them; treat the field's standard answer as a signpost to the missed recombination; never novelty for its own sake, and run a fairness pass against exactly that failure mode. Keep the whole shape: reject both the drift that narrows vehje to one corner and the over-correction that drops the endgame (change the boots, keep walking). Docs are design and are trusted; source is untrusted, a transient approximation of the design. Supersede forward, never rewrite a locked topic; the evolution is recorded on top. And, per op (PE5), the working posture is *most ideal and optimal*, not fastest: the fastest resolver for an open fork (rush a consumer to decide it) is declined where it trades the ideal design for speed; the consumer arrives when vehje is genuinely ready.

## 6. The working discipline: bench it, and audit the bench

A which-is-faster or which-shape-is-cheaper fork is resolved by building the candidates and measuring, not by first principles or prior-art authority. The harness is a first-class asset we improve upstream (per-variant cdylib subprocess isolation so no cross-variant LTO blurs a comparison, byte-exact cross-validation against an oracle so a divergent variant is rejected not timed, hardware counters, and the upstreamed cost-model fit, axis-matrix variant generation, disassembly duplicate-check guard, reference-floor column, and calibrated duration floor). Audit your own benches adversarially: the reporting layer lies before the harness core does, and the framework's own soul can hide inside its own benchmark. Benches are provisional (Section preamble): trust the data for what it measured, tag the artifact it measured (prototype versus shipped crate), extrapolate no further, and keep building better ones. And, the discipline this round learned it had underused: every canon-grade claim carries a catalogued red test or a bench cell at authoring, because an adversarial prose audit does not scale and a red test does; prose is an index over executables, not a substitute for them.

## 7. Prior art, by degree of commitment

How tightly we hold each borrowed idea is itself canon, because a bench trumps any prior-art cite. **Married** (load-bearing spine, adopted by construction): graded (co)modal types (Davies-Pfenning, Katsumata, Petricek-Orchard-Mycroft, Gaboardi 2016, Granule); reachability types (Bao et al. 2021 and successors); algebraic effects and handlers (Plotkin-Pretnar); the certified-generation lineage (CompCert's trusted-rim framing, proof-carrying code, certifying-not-certified); the lens principle (Foster et al.); zero-copy value transfer (Cap'n Proto / FlatBuffers / Arrow / rkyv, the reserve/commit sink as an iteratee after Kiselyov, the host-lent window as an object-capability after Miller); region inference (Tofte-Talpin / MLKit / Cyclone, dropping borrow-exclusivity because produced values are immutable); defunctionalization (Reynolds; Danvy-Nielsen; Ager et al.), promoted by the bench from the principled route to the only route; A-normal form (Flanagan et al.); hygiene (Kohlbecker et al.; Flatt); notko's fallibility ladder. **Half-married** (scoped, gated, a bench can still unseat): copy-and-patch (Xu-Kjolstad; demoted below direct isel but an in-tree accelerator whose compile-versus-warm breakeven is unmeasured); Deegen-style triple emission (shape adopted, stencil half de-prioritised); the eBPF-verifier tnum bounded abstract interpretation (scoped to the numeric residual only; tnum-for-structure was killed); scannerless PEG / GLL-plus-attribute grammars; one-shot and linear continuations; QTT for the assurance grade vector; NaN-boxing plus the register VM (bench-leaning, register VM 1.41x-3.06x per profile, NaN-boxing low-to-medium confidence); CHERI as optional hardware assurance. **Heeded, not concretely aligned** (respect the lesson, refuse the mechanism): oatlog / relational-engine-as-proc-macro (heed the one-engine idea, refuse the heap-using proc macro, build our own no-alloc generator); Carmack's floor-first verdict (its faults are the agenda, its re-tiering remedy rejected at the frame); LMS on the emission axis (the anti-pattern to route around, while LMS's staging insight is married on the reducer axis); the full Rust borrow checker (drop aliasing-exclusivity, values immutable); Iris-scale logical relations and MLIR/IREE/SPIR-V/Slang multi-target IR (existence proofs and audience framing, not mechanisms adopted whole); AARA (deprioritised to three constants, re-opened by the CR1 budget-fit). The tiers that a bench has *already* moved (equality saturation to negative-precedent, tail-threading scoped) live in the negative catalogue.

## 8. The drift test (the letter)

When a proposal, a doc, or a piece of code is questioned as drifting, it is checked against identity *and* mechanism jointly, not against a mechanism-only shorthand. The two axes are separate: a vehje with the identical handler discipline but a game-runtime-first identity is perfectly coherent, which is exactly why the identity clause and the mechanism clause are both required and neither is a corollary of the other. Concretely: does the thing keep the convictions (Sections 2 and 3, does it serve the census equally, prove-then-erase, no heap, inclusion not coverage), does it take the maximal shape or quietly re-tier (Section 4), does it honour op's latest call and not a superseded one (Section 5), is a which-is-faster fork being asserted instead of benched and does it respect what the benches taught (Sections 6 and 2H), and is a demoted prior-art cite being treated as still-married (Section 7 and the negative catalogue). The centre of gravity (Section 1) is the fastest single test: does the thing serve the one binding-time-directed handler discipline that ships certified data, or does it fragment it. This test is the letter; it is sharpened by process, and it, not an appeal to spirit, is what a future reader checks against.

## How to read this with its inverse

This catalogue states what the soul is. Its companion states what the soul is not: the shapes a better composition superseded, the choices a measurement overturned, and the measurement errors that taught us how a bench lies. A living conviction is one that survived both filters. Read together they are the intended pair; where a mechanism's exact live-or-dead status is in question, the negative catalogue governs on that mechanism.
