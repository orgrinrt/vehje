## The verdict

The certified-generation resolution survived four expert lenses (embeddable-runtime first-principles;
codegen and metaprogramming; programming-languages theory and verified compilation; systems and Zig-build-time)
and three insight forks (a compiler-theory veteran; an NN/ML take; a unifying-mathematics take), with its
architecture intact. No auditor reopened it. The framework identity holds throughout: no rustc, no LLVM, no
typestate at the consumer, one pre-compiled per-target binary, with Zig's cross-compilation serving "one binary
per target." What the panel produced is not a redesign but a stack of precision corrections, two cost-cliff
fixes, and a set of decisions that are op's, plus a clear gate before any doc changelist.

## The unifying frame (from 08)

Stripped of engineering, the design is one mathematical object: a two-input engine over
`(language_definition, script)` that discharges each safety property at the earliest binding time its input is
known. That object has a name in the literature: **staged gradual verification with binding-time-indexed
obligation discharge**, the composition of gradual verification / hybrid type checking (Flanagan;
Bader-Aldrich-Tanter), Abstracting Gradual Typing's Galois-connection derivation (Garcia-Clark-Tanter), and
binding-time analysis with the Futamura mix equation (Consel-Danvy; Jones-Gomard-Sestoft). All five mechanisms
are five instances of that one frame: family inclusion and effect inclusion are gradual with a decidable,
re-provable dynamic residual; non-null and dispatch totality are fully static; lease safety is gradual with a
residual conditional on per-family axioms; bounds validation is pure dynamic; family semantics sit outside the
verification system (tested); termination is not in the system at all (no totality axis). This gives every axis
one soundness-argument shape instead of five, and it makes the scoped meaning of "certified" a mechanical
labelling of each axis by its fragment of the gradual system. Detail and citations in `08_theory_insights.md`.

## The settled corrections (fold these; the experts converge)

The assurance vocabulary is partial evaluation plus per-instance re-type-checking plus translation validation,
not typed metaprogramming and not verified compilation; "certified" scopes to structural well-formedness,
semantics are tested, termination is not claimed. The bridge from Rust-side data to the Zig runtime is validated,
not verified, and for the two decidable axes (family, effect) it is better than validated by re-proving them
after the comptime decode (proof-carrying-code witness re-checking). The Zig mechanics stack holds with named
corrections: exhaustive-switch totality and comptime tagged-union illegal-states-unrepresentable are real;
comptime-folding of the inclusion check is the strongest single idea; optionals carry non-null and error unions
carry rejection; but the untrusted path must be panic-free (a Zig panic aborts the host across the C ABI), the
kernel must be iterative (comptime recursion segfaults the compiler), decoding must be byte-shift not
pointer-overlay (`@embedFile` alignment), the kernel takes caller buffers not an allocator (vtables are
comptime-illegal), dispatch is name-keyed (so a tag cannot silently route to the wrong handler), the forcing
root is owned, a differential-testing gate runs the kernel at both binding times, and Zig pins to 0.16.0. The
lease axis has a real resolution, the depth-ladder schema (leases as lexical body depth, inference as depth-min
propagation over one link-or-consume bit per operand, promotion-to-output as a sound fallback, monotone
annotations as consumer-safe), whose metatheorem is provable (the degenerate LIFO region calculus, by
Calcagno-Helsen-Thiemann technique) as a conditional theorem needing a short formal-semantics artifact.
Generation-checked references are an armed verification harness (never shipped); distinct ref types are engine
hygiene detached from the lease axis. Per-doc detail is in `01_carmack.md`, `02_giesen.md`, `04_theory-review.md`.

## The two cost-cliff fixes (from 05)

Three loci, not two binding times: comptime specialises the engine to the small language table (its sweet spot),
but folding bundled content at comptime hits a measured superlinear cost cliff, so content validation moves to a
native `build.zig` step running the identical kernel; comptime is for type generation, the build step for
content, native at the consumer. And spill is a host-lent reserve/commit sink policy, never baked filesystem
I/O, so the per-frame consumer (ikiuni, no mid-frame disk) and sandboxed hosts survive with a memory-only
backend while batch consumers use disk. The good news the same audit surfaced: streaming and the depth-ladder
lease inference are mutually reinforcing through the backward-only post-order invariant, so the lease pass spills
payload bytes while retaining a bounded frontier, and the depth cap (owed anyway for untrusted input) keeps that
frontier fixed-size so no_alloc survives streaming. Detail in `05_systems-review.md`.

## The one thing on the critical path

The cross-chunk lease lemma ("a cross-chunk reference is by construction consumed or promoted, because chunk
boundaries are whole-subtree boundaries") is the same lemma as the crux immutability lemma at chunk granularity,
and it is the load-bearing invariant that makes streaming and linear-scan lease inference compatible at all. It
must be proven or designed-in (constrain the chunker so it holds by construction) before the streaming spine is
committed, not left trailing.

## The proof document the round now owes

08 gives it a formal target: a two-level core `lambda_veh` over the eleven forms with a region store indexed by
a live-depth stack, per-axis static and AGT-derived gradual judgments, theorem T1 (staging soundness, the mix
equation, by Consel-Danvy / MetaML annotation soundness), T2 (the gradual guarantee per axis, by
Garcia-Clark-Tanter), T3 (region soundness, by Calcagno-Helsen-Thiemann restricted to the LIFO chain), plus the
crux immutability lemma, the cross-chunk lemma discharged by construction, and backward-link acyclicity as the
load-checker totality argument. "Proven once" is therefore a bounded, known task, not a promise.

## The seven decisions (op's; blessed by the veteran and the theory, awaiting op's ruling)

1. Bless the depth-ladder lease schema as the debt-2/3 resolution.
2. Bless the three-loci split (content validation to a native `build.zig` step).
3. Bless spill as a host-lent sink policy, not baked disk.
4. Accept a hard depth cap as a first-class runtime limit that no_alloc-under-streaming depends on.
5. Elevate the cross-chunk lemma to build-blocking (proven or designed-in before the streaming spine).
6. Bless the scoped meaning of "certified" (the per-axis guarantee inventory).
7. Sequence: run the widened experiment first, then the short proof document, then the doc changelist.

Three refinements land on these from 08. **Call 1's trusted surface shrinks:** because retention through a host
boundary is an effect (`Writes<HostEnv>`) and region systems and effect systems are one system (Talpin-Jouvelot),
the lease link/consume bit is derivable from the operand's declared effect, collapsing the "single most realistic
unsoundness vector" (the effectful-rim link bits) into the already-tested effect table, one axiom instead of two;
a proposal to test against the census consumers' host-boundary ops. **Call 6 becomes mechanical** (label each
axis by its staged-gradual fragment). **Call 7 gets the concrete target above.**

## The supplementary findings

The NN/ML fork (`07_nn_or_ml_insights.md`) is a guardrail and a composability proof, both off the now-critical
path: ML fits only the optimization-policy rim (a deterministic arvo fixed-point model, bench-gated behind a
swappable heuristic), never the correctness path; NPUs and GPUs are ruled out of the core, their one legitimate
entry being a consumer whose values are tensors opting in through the effect seam; and exposing accelerators,
autodiff (a `[Differentiable]`-style attribute is a certified IR-to-IR macro pass), and vectorization to
consumers all fall out of the target axis, the effect axis, and the value transport as they already stand, which
corroborates that the founding seams are right. All maturity features, none now-work. The speculative appendix
(`08b_speculative_pde_cross-pollination.md`) parks one genuinely-novel idea off the critical path: a quantitative
gradual guarantee via parabolic-stability estimates that would put a proved bound on the cost-cliff question the
systems audit could only bench.

## The gate to the doc changelist

Three things, all named, none a redesign: the widened experiment (Giesen's four negative arms, the theory
review's differential-testing gate, and the systems review's scaling-cost probe, forced-spill round-trip, C-host
panic-free arm, and ReleaseFast-versus-ReleaseSafe axis), the short proof document (per the 08 skeleton), and the
condition lists committed as build gates. Then the crate taxonomy, which now must carry the third build locus
(the `build.zig` content-validation infrastructure), then the doc phase. The macro-expander debt is resolved in
principle by the streaming spine, contingent on the cross-chunk lemma and the depth cap, and still owes its own
experiment arm.
