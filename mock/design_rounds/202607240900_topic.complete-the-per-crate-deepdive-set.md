# Completing the deepdive set

The goal is a documentation set that represents the whole round: not only what each crate is (the DESIGN) and
how to reach for it (the README), but why it is shaped as it is, grounded in the benches and research the round
produced. That "why" is deepdive material. Eight crates lack one. This round adds them, each pulling the
relevant findings into the crate whose design they justify, so the durable crate documentation, not only the
round artifacts, carries the evidence.

## The deepdives to write

Each is grounded in the crate's DESIGN plus the specific findings and research that bear on it. The findings
topic `202607240015` (and its completed-run confirmation `202607240700`) is the citation surface for the bench
numbers; the `research/*_synth_*.md` files are the prior-art grounding.

- `vehje-ir`: the no-alloc arena and node model, and the structural hash. Why operands stay inline (pool
  indirection measured at 10.7 ns/node against 4.4 ns inline), why the record width is an open
  measure-then-lock decision (the 24-byte flip washed to a null result), and how the hash folds leaf content
  within one interner.
- `vehje-fixpoint`: the relational engine and the incremental scaling layer. Whole-column versus semi-naive
  (whole-column wins on the realistic shallow graphs, about 50 ms at 8M nodes), congruence closure for the
  e-graph client, and the D6 incremental numbers (4 ms cold, 0.003 ms warm, 7.7x parallel at 8 threads) as the
  answer to the load-bloat concern.
- `vehje-lower`: the cheap stratum and the deferred saturation stratum. The node-count-reduction win (76%
  fewer nodes, the design-relevant result rather than a per-op time win), the redirecting `Rewrite` and the
  variable-free CSE capture-safety, and why the e-graph extractor must be an iterative work-stack (recursion is
  a hard Zig-comptime wall).
- `vehje-codegen`: the output spectrum and proof-directed strategy selection. Interpretation to transpilation
  as one spectrum, the native ceiling (1.0x to 1.5x for scalar code, not a 10x keystone; the 10x class needs a
  vectorising JIT), plain-switch dispatch winning for the lean op vocabulary, and the tier-dependent branch
  strategy map.
- `vehje-signature`: the language-level graded algebraic signature, and how it splits from the per-program
  `vehje-ir` (what a language is versus what a program is), with the operation catalogue, lease-rule schema,
  and the attribute-grammar seam.
- `vehje-resolve`: the borrowed, non-allocating scope chain (the flat shadow-stack at 0.91 ns per reference,
  6x to 14x over a scope-chain walk over an owned table), and the recursive-let and family-extension seams.
- `vehje-schedule`: the compile-side pass DAG over `arvo-graph`, its narrowed remit (ordering consumer family
  passes against the Core stages, since the compile-and-verify side is one object, not a resolve-and-check
  peer pipeline), and why the runtime side uses hilavitkutin's morsel execution instead.
- `vehje-runtime-gen` and `vehje-runtime-driver`: the validated-data package plus content-addressed manifest
  and the deferred lens handoff (gen), and the tier-tagged dispatch plus the typed structural decode and the
  runtime embedding (driver).
