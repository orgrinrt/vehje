## Preface: the two-artifact model, and why it governs this synthesis

Before the synthesis, the frame it rests on, because the confusion it fixes reappeared inside the passes being
synthesised. There are exactly two artifacts, and Rust lives in only one of them. Rust is entirely out of the
runtimes.

The Rust language compiler is dev-time only. It compiles the language spec (grammar, families, effects, lease
rules, targets), proves that language's disciplines sound, and emits validated data (the family table, effect
masks, lease-rule schema, wire layout) plus the proofs carried structurally. It never sees a script and never
ships. The Zig composed runtime is the shipped artifact: the general engine (parser, analyzers, macro expander,
interpreter, output targets) authored once by hand in Zig, specialised by Zig comptime to the Rust-emitted data,
certified by the Zig compiler at our build. It runs every script, bundled or arriving. No Rust, no rustc, no LLVM
at the consumer. The crux precision is that Rust emits data, not Zig source; the specialised Zig is produced by
Zig comptime from that data, which is exactly what 1627 chose and 1845 blessed, and exactly the "Rust metaprogram
emitting Zig source" that 1627 rejected as the hard-to-certify route.

This is the frame because one of the three load-bearing holes (the dual-locus divergence) is not a hole at all
once the frame is applied: it is Cluster A having drifted off this frame, and it dissolves on return. The full
statement lives in the identity recenter's addendum; it is repeated here in brief so the synthesis reads on its
own.

## The convergence is the signal

Four independent passes now sit over the same holes. The honest-keeper (Cluster C) found them; the solution set
filled them; the counter-audit attacked the fills; the reconciliation mapped them to the identity. On the three
load-bearing holes they land on one shape, and the counter-audit's own net (three of the five surviving problems
are answered by prior art or the round's own canonical design, not by unbuilt work) is the same conclusion from
the adversarial side. Convergence under independent adversarial attack is the signal the whole arc has trusted,
and here it says the design is closer to whole than the raw five-hole tally implied. The synthesis below takes the
sharper version wherever the passes differ, keeps the constructive designs where they do not, and names what all
four agree to remove.

## The three load-bearing holes, resolved

### 1. The reachability bound: the binder rule plus the region-promotion bound

Cluster C caught a real bug: Cluster A's `Reach(p, b) :- Child(p, _, c), Reach(c, b)` is unbounded as written, so
a node accumulates the binders of its whole subtree, `O(N)` per node, not the intended `O(W)`. The solution set's
first fix (filter the propagation by `InScope`, route the killed-but-reached binder to a separate `Escape`
relation) is on the right track but the counter-audit shows it is half-right, and the design takes the
counter-audit's version.

Two bounds are needed, and they come from two prior-art mechanisms, both already in the round's chosen lease
paradigm:

- The naming set (the in-scope binders a node reaches, width at most `W`) is bounded by the reachability-type
  binder rule, not by a filter on the generic propagation. At `Let` and `Lambda`, the reach set is not the naive
  union of the children's reach sets; it is the body's reach set with the bound variable eliminated by
  substitution, where the body reaches the variable splice in the bound expression's reach set, then drop the
  variable. This is the avoidance operation from reachability types (Bao-Wei-Bracevac-Jiang-He-Rompf, OOPSLA
  2021; the polymorphic successor, Wei et al., POPL 2024), the very paradigm 2001 adopted. A filter that drops
  the variable without splicing under-approximates and is unsound in the opposite direction from A (a value
  reaching through the bound variable into a still-live outer binder would lose that reach and be judged safe when
  it is not). So the rule fires at the binder node; plain child-propagation plus the in-scope filter then carries
  the already-scoped set upward.
- The escape set (the out-of-scope binders a value reaches, which `Escape`/`LeaseResidual` carry and resolve at
  the avoidance boundary) is not bounded by `W`, and no_alloc needs it bounded too. Its bound comes from region
  promotion: Tofte-Talpin region inference bounds the live-region count by the program's let-and-region structure,
  and the converged shape's own shared-implies-promoted discipline (2001) collapses escaping shared subvalues into
  a bounded promoted-region set finalised at emission.

So the complete fix is the binder rule (bounds the naming set to `W`) plus the promotion bound (bounds the escape
set to the region count), both prior art. The small vindication worth noting: the sound rule falls straight out of
the reachability-types paradigm 2001 already chose, so the choice pays for itself here.

### 2. The dual-locus identity dissolves by returning to canon

Cluster C's sharpest structural catch was that Cluster A's default (a generator emitting the analysis engine as
both specialised Rust source and specialised Zig source, two backends compiled by two toolchains with independent
overflow and evaluation-order and undefined-behavior surfaces) makes "identity by construction" mean deterministic
divergence, not agreement. On that two-backend shape, the catch is right. But the counter-audit and the
reconciliation both land the correction: the divergence-free shape is not the exotic "assurance-maximal fallback"
Cluster A filed it as; it is this round's own canonical certified-generation resolution, and Cluster A drifted
from it.

The canonical shape (1627, blessed 1845) is one hand-authored engine, specialised by Zig comptime to Rust-emitted
validated data, a single backend. Per-script analysis runs in one locus, the composed Zig runtime, for bundled and
arriving scripts alike; the Rust side compiles the language, never a script, so there are not two script-analysis
results to reconcile. The corrected reachability rules of hole 1 therefore live in exactly one place: they are the
language's lease-rule schema (data emitted by Rust) consumed by the one hand-authored Zig lease evaluator. Where a
dev-time act genuinely needs to run the engine, it links the one compiled Zig engine, rather than a second Rust
emitter of the same analysis. Cluster A's two-backend emitter was the LMS-hard "Rust metaprogram emitting foreign
source" path 1627 explicitly rejected, reappearing at the engine level, and it is the same drift pattern the
identity recenter names at the framing level: reaching for the elaborate shape when the canonical simpler one was
already decided. The resolution is return to canon, which is less engineering, not more (author the engine once,
maintain no second source emitter). The differential test over the census corpus stays, but as defence in depth on
the data handoff, not as the primary assurance a two-backend design would be forced to lean on. This is the hole
the preface exists to frame: it is not engineering to do, it is a drift to undo.

### 3. The interpreter is a CFG of straight-line blocks over the existing Core forms

Cluster C is right that Cluster B's `for n in 0..len` forward scan describes only a straight-line dataflow DAG and
is silent on branches, loops, and script-internal calls, so as written it would evaluate both arms of a
conditional (wrong under effects), cannot iterate, and has no return path. The counter-audit is right that this is
standard, not novel, and that "the single largest hole" overstated it: vehje's eleven Core forms already include
`If`, `Match`, `Iter`, `Interp`, and `Apply`, so control flow is first-class Core the interpreter dispatches
specially, not an unmodeled surprise. The solution set supplies the clean encoding.

The residual is a control-flow graph of blocks. A block is a maximal straight-line region (a contiguous node range
with backward-only child indices) evaluated by Cluster B's forward linear post-order scan, which keeps the
cache-friendly two-stream-plus-L1-hot-backward-reads win exactly where it applies, the dominant case. Only the
block terminator touches control flow: `If`/`Match` evaluate the scrutinee and transfer to the taken successor
block, so both arms are never evaluated; a loop is a back-edge to a header block with loop-carried values passed as
block arguments (the SSA block-argument discipline, cleaner than phis in a no_alloc setting), one fixed window per
loop-nesting level so iteration allocates nothing; a call saves a return record on a fixed-capacity frame stack,
sets up the callee window, and transfers, with the frame stack bounded by the depth cap `D` (the same finite
quantity that already bounds the no_alloc frontier, the untrusted traversal, and the load verifier). Tail-threaded
dispatch composes with this perfectly: once the successor is a computed cursor rather than `n+1`, the tail call is
a jump to an arbitrary handler, which tail-threading already handles.

The owed work is real but bounded: a wire-format block table (node range, terminator kind, successor block ids,
block-argument signature) and function table (entry block, argument and result signatures), and the per-frame
budget re-derived on dynamic executed node counts rather than static counts (the counter-audit's genuine addition).
Block and function boundaries are whole-subtree boundaries, so they coincide with the chunk boundaries the transfer
topic and the cross-chunk lemma already use, and the block boundary is exactly where the `Escape` of hole 1 fires,
so the three load-bearing fixes share one seam.

## The smaller resolutions

**Dispatch and `preserve_none`: optimisation tier, not a correctness dependency.** Cluster C grounded the
`preserve_none` absence against Zig 0.14, but the project pins 0.16 (1845), and 0.14 is precisely the release that
made `CallingConvention` an extensible tagged union, so more conventions surfacing later is the expected
trajectory. More important, correctness never depended on it: tail-call threading with `@call(.always_tail)` and
the three walk pointers (node, results, blob) passed as the first explicit arguments keeps them register-resident
under every mainstream ABI, because a tail call is a jump that leaves the argument registers in place (Haberman's
2 GB/s protobuf result is `musttail`-plus-explicit-args and predates the `preserve_none` convention, which is
Clang and LLVM 19, 2024). So the core dispatch ships correct on stock Zig today; `preserve_none` is an incremental
gain (it frees callee-saved registers for heavier handlers and drops the prologue and epilogue) and a
fix-the-stack-upstream ask. The sketch on the pinned toolchain is still owed, with the generated `switch` and
computed-goto fallbacks as the floor for any target that guarantees neither.

**The engine remit "narrowing" is a relabeling, not a defect.** Every relational engine has a trusted front-end
that materialises the extensional database from input; the structural decode being a linear front-end rather than
an engine query is standard database architecture, and the engine's remit (lease inference, eqsat lowering, and
the load residuals, all genuine fixpoints) is the right remit. The one substantive fix under this heading is a
phrasing correction: for untrusted arriving input, children-before-parents is a property the decode checks (a
complete linear validation), not "by construction"; the by-construction wording holds only for trusted bundled
emission.

**The lowering seam is a three-way split, and its cheap subset repays the templating majority.** Full
equality-saturation lowering (macro expansion, const-fold, CSE, algebraic identities, the graded partial-evaluation
unfold) stays dev-time for bundled scripts, where the heavy e-graph can take its time. A cheap runtime lowering
subset (const-fold plus CSE as a bounded single bottom-up pass with hash-consing, a fixed rule set, no unbounded
saturation, so no_alloc and cheap) runs at load for arriving scripts. Heat-based native acceleration applies on
top where the platform permits. The cheap subset is exactly what the authoring and templating consumers (the
mockspace columnar-query and procedural-document DSL, polka-dots' `.polka`) need: it folds the load-time-known
configuration away and shares repeated structure at the emit stage, which is where their speed concern lives and
where load-time bloat is prevented, with no dependence on the native tier. The same subset keeps a hot arriving mod
on a runtime-logic consumer from running fully un-optimised where stencils are unavailable. One cheap pass serves
both ends of the census.

**The e-graph extractor is dev-time cost-model extraction, not a blocker.** With the seam resolved, the full
e-graph is dev-time, so the extractor is off every hot path: bottom-up cost-model selection over the bounded
window, the cost function encoding the two constraints already named (respect the effect-ordering edges, prefer
the maximally-residualised form at the known binding-time grades). This is egg's linear extraction adapted to the
bounded window, a known algorithm. The one genuinely novel piece remains the no_alloc bounded streaming e-graph
itself, which stays flagged as original work and dev-time, where its cost is affordable. The cheap runtime subset
needs no extractor.

**Diagnostics: adopt Cluster C's Part 2 and land the schema now.** The lazy-witness design (provenance carried
cheaply on the happy path, the derivation witness reconstructed on demand over resident tables on the failure
path, bounded by derivation depth, the Souffle proof-tree technique on the provenance-semiring foundation of
Green-Karvounarakis-Tannen 2007) is a full design, not a hole, and it serves the authoring majority most of all,
whose first contact with vehje is a lease or effect error that must name which binder escaped where. Land its six
schema additions in the wire format in the same pass as the block and function tables: the span table, the
binder-site table, the per-relation provenance arrays, the violation seeds, the optional residual debug section,
and the provenance-complete engine C ABI (which is what makes the single hand-authored engine lossless for
diagnostics, so this and the dual-locus resolution are one decision). The counter-audit's refinement: a join rule
with two recursive premises makes the reconstruction fork, so the bounded path buffer becomes a bounded path tree
sized by depth times max-recursive-arity, still finite, still failure-path-only.

**The format decisions are measurements, not paper calls.** Three items are genuine bench questions, resolved as
measure-then-lock with provisional values so work proceeds and the experiment is the confirm-gate: the node record
width (provisional 16 bytes with two inline operands, on the arity-at-most-two assumption, gated on the real arity
distribution across the census families, going to 24 bytes with three inline operands if calls and record
construction are hotter than assumed); the region-id column width (provisional `flags` byte when the live-region
count is at most 256, else a parallel `[u16; N]`, gated on the live-binder and live-region width distribution);
and whether the native tier beats the cheap-lowered interpreter by enough to justify the stencil trusted base
(gated on a stencil-versus-cheap-lowered-interpret bench, with PEP 744's note that CPython's first-generation
copy-and-patch JIT is currently interpreter-speed as a warning that cuts both ways, so the honest posture is "must
bench," and native being a keystone-among-many means a modest win still earns the accelerator where the platform
permits it and no win means the interpreter-only builds lose nothing). These run against the two gating
experiments the round already owes.

## Strike the nonlinear-dynamical speed layer

All four passes and both prior experts agree, and this is the one piece of tech in either 2055 or 2205 that is
actively bad for the endgame. The nonlinear-dynamical speed layer (widening as limit-prediction, Aitken and vector
extrapolation over the analyses' lattices) is empty on the finite bitmask and small-finite-height lattices in
play: Kleene iteration over them converges in at most the lattice height, and semi-naive evaluation is already the
complete and correct acceleration; numeric sequence extrapolation has no meaning on a boolean lattice. It is struck
cleanly, keeping plain widening only if a genuinely numeric domain (tnum) ever iterates. The reason to be explicit
about striking it, beyond the wasted layer, is that carrying an empty frontier-sounding layer teaches the next
reader that the design values the appearance of frontier work over its substance, which corrodes the credibility
of the genuine novelty around it (the graded spine, the one-engine composition, the no_alloc bounded e-graph).
Striking it is not a retreat from bleeding-edge; it is the discipline that keeps bleeding-edge honest.

## The strategy portfolio: native is one evaluator chosen where it provably wins

The solution set's generalisation of the interpreter fix is worth recording as its own resolution, because it is
the shape that puts native performance in its recentered place structurally rather than by assertion. The backward
linear scan is not the one ruling evaluation model with control flow bolted on. It is one strategy in a bounded
portfolio, optimal precisely in the regions where its precondition provably holds (straight-line dataflow with
backward-only child indices), and the runtime uses each strategy only where it is the winner. The pieces have names
in the literature: region-based compilation (Hank-Hwu-Rau, MICRO-28, 1995; the superblock lineage) for
partitioning into independently-evaluated regions; trace selection (Bala-Duesterwald-Banerjia, Dynamo, PLDI 2000;
the tracing-JIT lineage) for on-the-fly selection from observed shape; the partial-evaluation treatment of
conditionals (Jones-Gomard-Sestoft 1993) for folding a statically-known predicate and residualising a dynamic one;
and algorithm selection (Rice 1976) and algorithm portfolios (Gomes-Selman 2001) for the meta-principle.

What vehje adds, and where the name is owed, is that the selection is proof-directed and is itself a graded
discharge. The same graded (co)modal system that proves family, effect, and lease carries, per region, the grade
naming which evaluation strategy is legal and optimal there, and that selection follows the same static-or-dynamic
discharge as every other axis: where the grades prove the winning strategy from statically-known inputs it is
chosen at lowering (folded, zero runtime decision), and where they cannot it is a bounded on-the-fly decision, the
same gradual shape that governs the whole spine. The proposed name is proof-directed strategy selection (or graded
strategy selection). It fits the identity (the proof does not only certify safety, it selects the optimal evaluator
per region, so the grades earn their keep at runtime and not only at the gate) and it fits the recenter exactly:
native and copy-and-patch are one strategy in this portfolio, chosen in the regions where they provably win (hot,
stencil-permitted, worth the trusted base), not a ruling path. The honest boundary is that a portfolio is a win
only if the selection is cheap and the strategy set is bounded: a static grade lookup or a bounded on-the-fly test,
never a search, over a fixed small vocabulary (linear scan, folded branch, block-transfer branch, bounded-loop
forms, frame-transfer call, stencil-accelerated hot region), each with a proven precondition. That boundedness is
what keeps the portfolio no_alloc and its selection off the hot path.

## The demotion ledger, superseded

The honest-keeper's Part 3 concluded the arc held the maximalist line on only about one-and-a-half of Carmack's
seven and adopted the re-tiering on the rest while calling it maximalism, so the "we reject the demotion" framing
was theatrical. On the raw facts (the logical relation is a north star nothing waits on, AARA is three constants,
the semiring is scoped to the proof, the runtime parser is a table, the speed layer is struck) that is accurate,
and the arc did over-claim "designed through everything." The counter-audit and the reconciliation both reach, from
opposite directions, that the ledger is trapped in a win-or-loss frame the identity recenter dissolves: those
placements were never ambition-demotions to reject or adopt; they are correct engineering placement a maximal
design includes, and the debate's real error was neither rejecting nor secretly adopting Carmack, it was letting
the runtime-performance and native corner read as the whole identity. So the honesty note stands as a rigor
correction (a reader should not think all seven were refuted), and the reject-or-adopt scoring (which both the
rebuttal agenda and Part 3 do, from opposite ends) is the category error. Read the ledger through the recenter that
supersedes it: the engine's buildability is the genuine win, the placements are just correct placement, and the
identity of the whole is the framework serving a spectrum of consumers, of which the native and JIT corner is one
part.

## The vindication, made concrete

The reconciliation's central contribution is that the effort in 2055 and 2205 is vindicated in the recenter's own
terms, not consoled. The semi-naive relational-fixpoint engine of 2055 is the compile-stage brain whose primary
beneficiary is the authoring and templating majority, not the native minority: equality-saturation lowering is
macro expansion, constant folding, and common-subexpression elimination unified, which is exactly the compile-and-
emit stage a procedural-document DSL or a dotfile-templating language runs to turn template shards and cascades
into configured data, and the load-time speed those consumers need (so mod and content stacks do not bloat like
Clausewitz or RimWorld) comes directly from that engine being ahead-of-time compiled and from the cheap
const-fold-plus-CSE subset. The runtime-first narration hid the engine's largest beneficiary. The graded
provenance-semiring spine is the type-system-as-verification identity stated precisely, the very thing the recenter
names as vehje's core, and 2055 is where that core became one unified graded object instead of three parallel axes.
And 2055's frontier theory landed, almost entirely, on shapes that already ship in verified systems (Perceus,
slotted and colored e-graphs, simdjson, Scala 3 capture checking, oatlog), which is the practical proof of the
novelty-through-implementation stance the identity embraces. 2205's discipline (design through the faults, do not
build smaller) is the spirit the recenter keeps, and its diagnostics (problem 6) and dual-locus authoring (problem
7) serve every consumer, the authoring majority most of all. The walk was right; only the boots and the one empty
layer come off.

## What is actively bad for the endgame (only two)

1. The nonlinear-dynamical speed layer (2055): empty on the finite lattices in play, novelty theater. Struck.
2. The framing in both 2055 and 2205 that centered runtime performance, the native and JIT output end, and one
   hard consumer (ikiuni) as the primary and lone purpose. Not tech, but it is what lost the identity, and its
   concrete damage was Cluster B's interpreter designed only for a game's straight-line dataflow, broken on the
   control flow most scripts use. Removed by the recenter: the design proper presents the spectrum of consumers and
   the type-system-as-verification identity first, and the native and runtime-performance work as one part of the
   output spectrum, never the driver.

Everything else in 2055 and 2205 is kept, vindicated as the under-the-hood tech and the general design work that
empowers the framework's full breadth.

## The consolidated worklist and the owed-next topics

The worklist, resolved in shape here, owed in execution to the design proper: the reachability binder rule plus the
region-promotion bound (the `N*W` fix); the return-to-canonical single comptime-specialised engine (the dual-locus
resolution) with the differential harness as defence in depth; the CFG-of-blocks interpreter with its block and
function wire tables and the dynamic per-frame budget; the three-way lowering seam with its cheap load-time subset;
the diagnostics schema landed in the wire format now; the `preserve_none` sketch on the pinned toolchain and the
upstream calling-convention ask; and the three bench-gated format decisions run against the two gating experiments.
Strike the nonlinear speed layer wherever the grounded stack is worked into the design proper.

The owed-next topics, which the narrow focus never wrote and which this synthesis clears the ground for: the
transpilation end of the output spectrum (output generation that re-expresses the lowered IR in another language's
surface, a first-class pole, not a lesser interpretation); the authoring-and-templating consumer story (the
compile-stage-for-templating is the same equality-saturation lowering aimed at emitting configured data); and the
input-side grammar-composition story (Brzozowski-derivative inclusion-not-coverage, the framework-of-many-DSLs
identity applied to syntax, already grounded in 2055). These are the other sides the recenter turns to next.
