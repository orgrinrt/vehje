## What this topic is, and what it is not

This is the closing design statement, not a new design. Nothing here reopens a settled direction. It exists
because the round produced its architecture across seventeen topics and a large bench and sketch corpus, and the
doc phase needs one place that says, plainly and in current form, what vehje is after the round, what the crate

## The identity

Vehje is an embeddable, multi-input, multi-output IR framework whose identity is the type system as the
verification layer and certified generation as the mechanism. It is not a language. A grammar plugs in on the
input side and lowers surface syntax into one shared Core IR; a target plugs in on the output side, declares what
it supports, and emits. Vehje owns the shared Core substrate, the proof axes and their mechanisms, the output
machinery, the runtime ABI, an incremental scaling mechanism, and the value transport, and nothing above that. It
serves a census of consumers equally, and the representative and largest lean toward authoring and templating
(mockspace's columnar-query and procedural-document surfaces, polka-dots' `.polka`, the typst and scribble
register), whose speed concern is the compile stage and the emit rather than runtime logic. A native and JIT
endgame exists as one point on the output spectrum, never the driver, and one hard consumer (ikiuni's scripting
surface) is a consumer, never the identity.

## The two-artifact model

There are exactly two artifacts, and Rust is entirely out of the runtimes. The dev-time Rust language compiler
compiles a language spec (grammar, families, effects, lease rules, targets), proves its disciplines sound, and
emits validated data (the family table, effect masks, lease-rule schema, wire layout) plus proofs carried
structurally. It never sees a script and never ships. The Rust crate ecosystem under `mock/crates/` is this
compiler. The shipped artifact is a Zig composed runtime: one hand-authored general engine (parser, analyzers,
macro expander, interpreter, output targets) that Zig comptime specialises to the Rust-emitted data and the Zig
compiler certifies at our build. It runs every script, bundled or arriving, and carries no rustc, no LLVM, and no
typestate at the consumer. The crux precision, re-confirmed by the certified-generation sketch: Rust emits data,
not Zig source. The specialised Zig is produced by Zig comptime from that data, which is the route chosen over the
harder-to-certify "metaprogram emitting foreign source." The Zig runtime is a separate artifact, not a Rust crate;
the Rust crates model the boundary and emit the data the runtime specialises from.

Certified generation means the language typestate proves the composition sound at language-compile time, and the
proof is compiled into the structure of the generated Zig (illegal states unrepresentable, total dispatch over the
family set, comptime-folded inclusion). Rustc typing the generator and data is the first certification; the Zig
compiler typing the specialised engine is the second; both are at our build, neither at the consumer. The scope of
"certified" is structural well-formedness plus per-instance re-checking plus translation validation; semantics are
tested by a differential harness; termination is not claimed.

## The full architecture, stage by stage

Read top to bottom, the compile-and-verify side is one object, not a pass pipeline, though it is realised as
passes. A single declarative language definition, a graded algebraic signature, is the one source: the families
are the operations of an algebraic-effect theory, each carrying its effect grade and per-operand reachability
discipline; the grammar is an attribute grammar on the same signature; the value domain and the IR are two views
of the same operations. The Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig runtime consts are both projections of this
one signature, differential-checked by a content-addressed manifest.

Parse. Arriving scripts parse inside the composed Zig runtime, so the shipped parser is a general engine over a
resolved grammar table, not a Rust combinator. Per D1 the framework mandates only that a grammar produces
well-formed IR and does not police how; a general parser or grammar-as-data is legitimate opt-in tooling, never a
mandatory built-in front-end. Parsing emits a graded IR in one pass where a grammar-composition mechanism is in
use (the synthesised attributes of an L-attributed evaluation are the initial grades), but that mechanism is
opt-in and dev-time; the framework's contract is the IR, not the front-end.

Resolve. Name resolution over the Core binders, on a flat shadow-stack symbol table (measured at 0.91 ns per
reference, order-one, well ahead of a scope-chain walk), generic over the family set with family-extension hooks.

Check. The graded proof axes discharge as bottom-up bitmask propagations over the resolved IR. Effect inference is
a graded monad with the grade thermometer-encoded so the lattice join is a bitwise OR (measured at 6.14 ns per
node, the inclusion gate at 0.39 ns per node); lease and reachability inference is a coeffect over the same
fixed-capacity bitmask machinery, one bottom-up DAG join differing only in payload; binding-time is a modality
over the knowledge-source join-semilattice; inclusion-not-coverage checks the program's unhandled operations
against a target's `Permits`. The semi-naive relational-fixpoint shape these share reuses the columnar contracts of
`hilavitkutin` and the bitmask and graph primitives of `arvo`, rather than a bespoke engine.

Lower. Lowering, constant folding, macro expansion, and common-subexpression elimination are one stage with two
depths. The cheap runtime subset (const-fold plus CSE as a bounded single bottom-up pass with hash-consing, a
fixed rule set, no unbounded saturation, no alloc) runs at load for arriving scripts and is what serves the
authoring and templating majority; it reduces node count (measured 76% fewer nodes) rather than per-op time. The
heavier dev-time form is a binding-time-graded, effect-constrained equality saturation over a bounded e-graph,
which grows a measured 5.2x linear (not exponential) because hashcons congruence deduplicates equivalent forms;
its output normal form is A-normal form so effect order is explicit. The dev-time e-graph is the one genuinely
original research piece and is deferred behind the experiments; the cheap subset is proven feasible and ships.

Proof discharge, folded into lowering. Reachability computes the lease grades, effect inclusion checks the graded
monad, binding-time discharge is the extraction preference (discharge each property at the earliest binding time
its inputs are known). At the avoidance boundary, where a value escapes and its type cannot be expressed without
the escaped binder, the property's assurance grade drops and a per-reference generational-reference check is the
dynamic residual for exactly that reference. All of it erases into region tags in the residual; the runtime carries
no prover.

Emit. The compiler emits the validated data, a wire-format residual (the value-arena and the CFG-of-blocks
residual, below), and a content-addressed manifest binding the data to the runtime. Runtime generation is
Deegen-style from the one signature: for each family operation the generator emits an interpreter arm, a
copy-and-patch stencil, and a differential test.

Execute (the Zig runtime, not a Rust crate, described for completeness). Execution is a bounded strategy portfolio
(proof-directed strategy selection): interpret the tier-0 arena; tier hot regions to copy-and-patch native where a
per-region grade proves it wins. The interpreter is a CFG of straight-line blocks over the Core forms: a block is a
maximal backward-only-child-index straight-line region evaluated by a forward linear post-order scan (plain switch
dispatch, which the benches show beats tail-threading for vehje's lean op vocabulary); only the terminator touches
control flow (`If` and `Match` transfer to the taken successor, loops are back-edges with block-argument-passed
loop-carried values, calls save a return record on a fixed-capacity frame stack bounded by the depth cap). The
untrusted-load verifier is three mechanisms, not one fixpoint: a parse-don't-validate typed structural decode
(complete and linear, child-before-parent making acyclicity a single monotone index check) that feeds the engine
as its trusted front-end, a tristate-number numeric residual, and the per-reference lease generational check.

Value transfer (crossing back). The produced value crosses through a reserve-then-commit two-function sink (an
iteratee, a host-lent object-capability whose backpressure is inherent): in-process a `#[repr(C)]` struct of two
function pointers plus opaque userdata, out-of-process the executable's stdout pipe. The value serialises
depth-first, children-before-parents, so a parent records already-known child indices with no back-patching. The
streaming producer is a defunctionalised one-shot coroutine whose suspended frames are the chunks; frontier
boundedness is the linearity of its continuation. The host-to-runtime C ABI exposes one runtime-W batched column
entry (W records per call, W at least two), justified by the vertical-SIMD vectorisation win (the biggest measured
throughput lever, surviving the object boundary byte-exact) and by crossing amortisation at the native tier; the
return contract is a value property (an `Outcome`-shaped value in the value-arena where fallible), batch completion
a stream property on the sink, with no separate status vector.

## The twelve Core forms, the effect split, and content-as-values

The Core substrate is twelve forms. Eleven are evaluation shape unchanged from the ratified cut: `Lit` (a literal),
`Var` (a name reference), `Let` (binding, with a recursive flag; multi-binding desugars to nested `Let`), `Lambda`
(single-parameter abstraction; the first-class closure-escape is the load-bearing region-soundness obligation),
`Apply` (application with variadic args), `Project` (field access), `If` (conditional; its lowering is
proof-directed strategy selection), `Match` (destructuring, kept Core because it does not desugar to `If`
cleanly), `Iter` (iteration producing a monoidal accumulation, fusion mandatory and pull-based), `Interp`
(interpolate a value into content, kept a distinct form because a staging rule attaches to it, now a binding-time
grade), and `Raw` (the one typed escape hatch, the seam through which a family's own node kinds attach). There is
no `Seq`: content is a monoid and concatenation suffices.

The twelfth form is `Handle`, user-defined resumable algebraic-effect handlers, adopted this round on the principle
that the Core changes to fit the tech. It is Core because it is a general evaluation primitive (invoke an
operation; a handler services it, optionally resuming). It supports bounded multi-shot resumption via a host-lent
budget plus a comptime fit-check plus deterministic enumeration, with overflow failing closed at compile time
(proven feasible by sketch). The handler discipline unifies three previously separate mechanisms: an effect is an
operation, a handler services it, a host-call is a runtime-effect operation the host handles, and macro expansion
is a compile-stage-effect operation the compile stage handles. Which stage provides the handler is the
binding-time coordinate.

The effect lattice splits. The old `Pure` / `Reads<E>` / `Writes<E>` over a two-member `BuildEnv` / `RuntimeEnv`
axis conflated effect (what world-change) with binding time (when known). Effect becomes a pure graded monad (now
the operations of the handler discipline), and build-versus-runtime staging moves onto a binding-time graded
modality over the knowledge-source join-semilattice (language-author, bundler, host-loader, runtime, joined), so
partial bundling is a join rather than a special case. Content-as-values is the introduction projection of the one
signature: a family that adds a constructor gets its value-kind for free, so the deferred value-node-kind schema is
subsumed, and a document consumer's `Doc` family is its introduction fragment (an ecosystem crate, not framework).

## The graded proof spine

The three static proof axes plus assurance are one graded (co)modal judgment, unified by construction. Binding time
is a graded necessity modality over the knowledge-source join-semilattice; effect is a graded monad (graded
algebraic effects); lease and usage is a coeffect whose grade is a reachability qualifier; assurance is itself a
grade, a projection of the binding-time lattice crossed with the property (a property whose inputs are all
early-bound discharges statically and folds to a compile error; one whose inputs arrive late is a bounded dynamic
residual). One soundness theorem, with the per-axis results (staging soundness, the gradual guarantee, region
soundness) as corollaries and the axis interactions as its typing rules. The proof-document target is `lambda_veh`
(theorems T1 staging soundness, T2 the gradual guarantee per axis, T3 region soundness, plus the immutability,
cross-chunk, and backward-link-acyclicity lemmas), a bounded writing task that drafts in parallel with the
experiments.

The scoping discipline is load-bearing: the grades live in the proof and in the design's coherence argument, not in
the runtime types. At the machine level the grades are a small number of concrete closed enums and bitmasks (about
two enums and two bitmasks), and the entire graded apparatus is compile-side and compiled away, so the runtime
inherits proven-safe programs and re-runs nothing except the genuinely-unknowable-until-load structural rejection
and the numeric and lease dynamic residuals. The assurance-indexed gradual logical relation (the proof that holds
where assurance is high and degrades to its operational shadow, the differential test, where assurance is low) is
the highest-value original work and is deferred; the shippable core is the concrete grade enums and bitmasks and
the bottom-up inclusion checks.

## The lease and reachability axis

Reachability types are the primary lease paradigm (op's call, overriding the earlier generational-references lean),
which fits because the lease is independently a coeffect and reachability types are a coeffect. The lease coeffect's
grade is a reachability qualifier realised as a fixed-capacity bitmask over in-scope binders, the same shape and
inclusion primitive as the family and effect axes, so three axes share one bitmask machinery and one inclusion
check. The immutable strictly-LIFO depth-lease is the degenerate reachability qualifier and the risk valve: it
ships first on the proven-cheap metatheorem, and the grade lattice grows into the full qualifier additively,
unlocking the mutable, aliased, and escaping cases. Reachability inference is compile-side and erased into
certified region structure, so the runtime inherits proven-valid references with no collector. Generational
references demote to the gradual dynamic residual at the avoidance boundary, for exactly the un-placeable reference
rather than a whole-value collapse.

The naming set (the in-scope binders a node reaches, width at most W) is bounded by the reachability-type binder
rule (at `Let` and `Lambda`, splice the bound expression's reach set where the body reaches the variable, then drop
the variable), not by a bare in-scope filter, which would under-approximate and be unsound the other way. The
escape set is bounded by region promotion. The effect-coeffect interaction is graded def-use, not a distributive
law, and retention through a host boundary is itself an effect, so the link-or-consume bit is a monotone function
of the operand's declared effect. Sharing uses Perceus exact-meet (a shared node gets the exact meet of its
referrers' reach-sets, finalised at emission), which also licenses in-place reuse, not shared-implies-promoted (an
ML-Kit-style leak) and not copy-everything (exponential). Absence is an explicit `Maybe` value form, never a
nullable reference. The mutable-consumer story is gated on the avoidance-rate experiment (measure the inference
failure rate on real Lua-shaped census code); the immutable floor is proven and ships.

## The output side, the input side, and the framework-scope decisions

Output generation is one spectrum from interpretation to transpilation; a target is a point on it. A target
declares its `Supports` family set and `Permits` effect set, inclusion-not-coverage gates emission (a program using
a family or effect outside the declaration is refused with the construct named, never silently degraded), one
generic fold walks the Core plus the program's families, and a single typed escape hatch offers the
representations a value can be for a value the model does not know. "Native" is a target at the far end of the
spectrum, not a tier, and its codegen method is method-agnostic (LLVM, cranelift, copy-and-patch, or direct per
target), a keystone among keystones rather than the primary purpose. Per D2 the framework mandates only the
IR-to-output contract and does not police how a target is defined; a data-driven or generated target is opt-in
tooling on the same footing as a hand-written one.

The input side is symmetric (D1): the framework mandates only that a grammar produces well-formed IR, and offers
opt-in lex and parse building blocks and a node builder as libraries, never a mandatory front-end machine. Grammar
is separate from families (a family is an IR-and-semantics unit, a grammar is a front-end that produces IR), and
any grammar-composition scheme is unpoliced opt-in tooling, dev-time only, with the shipped runtime parsing one
fixed resolved grammar.

Two framework-scope decisions govern the taxonomy. D6: the framework owns an incremental scaling mechanism
(content-addressed keying, cross-artifact dedup by structural hashing, differential recompute over a host-declared
artifact set), so mod and content stacks do not bloat load times (measured: a 2000-mod stack cold-loads in 4 ms,
warm-loads editing one mod in 0.003 ms, cold parallelises 7.7x); the framework owns the mechanism, the host owns
policy and the storage backend. D7: the framework is core plus mechanism only, and everything above it (grammars,
families, `Doc` families, languages, grammar-as-data tools, manifestation helpers) is an ordinary ecosystem crate
that anyone publishes and anyone depends on, including the census "first-party" surfaces, which are on the same
footing as any third-party crate. The framework does not provide, promote, defer, track, or privilege any consumer
surface.

## The crate and library taxonomy

The round named the taxonomy as owed and gated it behind the experiments and the proof document, then never wrote
it. It is derived here, from the settled design and the debts, and cross-checked against an independent
compiler-infrastructure review before the doc phase executes against it. The governing constraints are D7 (the
framework is core plus mechanism only, so no consumer surface is a framework crate), the reuse discipline (arvo
for graph, bitmask, bits, sparse, and content hashing; notko for the fallibility primitives; hilavitkutin for the
AccessSet typestate, the sink and capability traits, the interner string, and the columnar Column-Resource
contracts the semi-naive compile stage and the incremental mechanism build on), the no-std and no-alloc floor on
every framework crate, and the anti-over-engineering pressure that each crate earn its existence.

The organizing principle that pays Debt 1534 is structural, not a crate: the Rust crate set is the dev-time
language compiler and the model of the boundary, and the Zig runtime is a separate artifact (a repo-level
`runtime/` tree, never a Rust crate). The debt "the taxonomy must reflect a runtime that contains a compile stage"
is paid by the rule that every Rust crate owning a compile-stage mechanism also owns the validated-data slice that
specialises the Zig runtime's copy of that mechanism, with one aggregation crate that composes the slices into the
manifested package. There is no `vehje-runtime-compile-stage` crate, because the compile stage is the stage crates,
projected.

The target set is twelve framework crates (the current eight extended, one rename, three new) plus three reserved
seats. Each crate, its role, and its status:

- `vehje-ir` (extend). The shared program-level representation: the twelve Core forms (the eleven plus `Handle`),
  the `Family` trait plus its effect classification, the grade vocabulary (the effect, binding-time, lease, and
  assurance grades as concrete closed enums and fixed-capacity bitmasks over the reused AccessSet and arvo-bitmask
  machinery, with the binding-time knowledge-source lattice carrying Debt 1's two build environments as two
  lattice points), structural content-addressed hashing of IR subtrees (defined once here because three downstream
  mechanisms consume the same identity: hash-consing in `vehje-lower`, dedup keying in `vehje-fixpoint`, and the
  manifest in `vehje-runtime-gen`), the node builder, the arena, the interner, the span side-table, and the
  diagnostic. The effect module is reworked from the `Pure` / `Reads` / `Writes` lattice to the graded monad plus
  the binding-time modality. The crate every per-program pass depends on.
- `vehje-signature` (new). The language-level definition, split from per-program `vehje-ir`: the graded algebraic
  signature (families as operations of an algebraic-effect theory, each with its effect grade and per-operand
  coeffect discipline; the attribute-grammar attachment point; the lease-rule schema; the declared target set),
  and its three projections (the eliminator IR, the introduction value domain, the introduction-compiled
  stencils). `vehje-ir` is what a program is; `vehje-signature` is what a language is. Data only; the compilation
  over it is `vehje-runtime-gen`'s. This is the closest call in the taxonomy: if the signature's only real
  consumer turns out to be the packaging crate, it folds into `vehje-runtime-gen`; it is kept a crate because the
  check pass reads its lease-rule schema and per-family axiom table, and ecosystem grammar-as-data tooling and the
  proof document both need a citable one-source language-definition contract.
- `vehje-fixpoint` (new). The compile-stage engine: an ahead-of-time, no-alloc, semi-naive relational-fixpoint
  evaluator with congruence support, generic over caller-defined relations in caller-provided columnar storage,
  deliberately free of any `vehje-ir` dependency. It reuses the hilavitkutin-api columnar and AccessSet contracts
  as its storage vocabulary and arvo's graph and bitmask as its algorithms, and adds the semi-naive deltas,
  congruence closure, and provenance grades those do not carry. It earns its existence on three clients, one of
  which ships now: the lease and reachability inference in `vehje-check` (benched at 8M nodes and 16M edges in
  about 50 ms), the equality-saturation lowering in `vehje-lower` (later), and the diagnostics witness
  reconstruction. Debt D6's incremental scaling mechanism lands here as a differential module (the round's own
  lean: a capability of the engine, not a separate crate), reusing `vehje-ir`'s one structural-hash definition for
  content-addressed keying.
- `vehje-resolve` (keep, extend). The Core resolve pass on the flat shadow-stack scope chain, plus the resolution
  side-table the later passes read and the family-extension resolve hooks.
- `vehje-check` (rename from `vehje-typecheck`, extend). The graded (co)modal judgment: reachability and lease
  inference as fixpoint queries (the depth-lease degenerate floor first, the full qualifier lattice additively),
  effect inclusion (thermometer-encoded OR, benched negligible), binding-time discharge, and assurance grading. It
  reads the language's lease-rule schema and per-family axiom table from `vehje-signature`, and it owns the
  validated-data slice for the runtime's analyzer stage. The `Checked` witness moves here from the output crate
  (the proof witness belongs with the prover; emission consumes it, it does not mint it).
- `vehje-lower` (new). The lowering stage the current surface lacks, and the one Debt 1 and the retirement of
  "codegen" (1513) make unavoidable: lowering is neither checking nor output generation. Two strata along the
  three-way seam: the cheap runtime subset now (const-fold plus CSE by hash-consing over `vehje-ir`'s structural
  hash, a bounded single bottom-up pass, a fixed rule set, no alloc, benched at 76% node reduction), and the
  dev-time equality-saturation e-graph later (the flagged original work), behind the same fold interface so it is
  additive. The crate owns the cheap subset's rule table as its validated-data slice, the same rule set the
  runtime's load-time lowering of arriving scripts is specialised from.
- `vehje-schedule` (keep, narrowed). The pass-DAG ordering of consumer family passes over arvo-graph. The
  one-engine frame removes its old job of sequencing resolve and check as peers; what remains is ordering a
  consumer's family passes against the Core stages by declared dependencies, a genuine extension mechanism. If
  family passes turn out to be handler instances of the compile-stage effect discipline, this folds into `vehje`;
  it is kept because it exists, is small, and deleting it now bets on an unproven unification.
- `vehje-codegen` (keep, extend). The output machinery: the `Target` contract (`Supports` and `Permits`),
  inclusion-not-coverage gating on the `Checked` witness (now imported from `vehje-check`), the one generic fold
  (extended to the twelfth form), the typed escape hatch, the output spectrum from interpretation to
  transpilation, and compile-time target composition. The independent review recommended renaming this to
  `vehje-emit` (1513 retired "codegen"); that rename is deferred as an unmandated cosmetic change, since the round
  mandated only the `vehje-typecheck` to `vehje-check` rename, and it can follow later at the same pre-1.0 cost.
- `vehje-runtime-gen` (new). Debt 1845's home, and the first of 1513's three named operations (runtime
  generation): compile the language definition into the validated-data package (family table, effect masks,
  lease-rule schema, cheap-lowering rule table, wire layout, and a stencil section whose schema exists day one and
  whose producer is a deferred toolchain), and bind the package to the Zig runtime with the content-addressed
  manifest (the lens-projection differential check in its cheaper manifest-check interim; the full lens is the open
  handoff-staging question and lands here). It does not define any slice; it composes slices whose formats the
  stage crates own, and it owns only the package layout, the manifest, and the differential-check contract. This
  completes the `vehje-runtime-*` family as the full Zig boundary: gen (per-language, our build), abi (per-script,
  wire), driver (embedding). The Zig `build.zig` content-validation step lives in the repo-level `runtime/` tree,
  never as a Rust crate.
- `vehje-runtime-abi` (extend heavily). The per-script wire family: the residual CFG-of-blocks arena (the block
  and function tables), the value-arena (children-first, no back-patch, region-table indirection), the
  reserve-commit two-function sink contract (`#[repr(C)]`, with the stdout-pipe variant), the diagnostics schema
  additions, the tier tag, and the one runtime-W batched-column `extern "C"` entry (W at least two, the
  `Outcome`-in-the-value-arena return). One crate, not two: the residual arena and the value-arena are one
  structural family and the record layout must have exactly one definition; internally it splits into `wire`,
  `value`, `sink`, and `entry` modules, and if the value side outgrows the file-size smell it splits as files
  under one crate, never a second crate with a copied layout.
- `vehje-runtime-driver` (keep, extend). The compiler-side embedding and dispatch over the ABI, plus the safe
  value-arena reader and the validation pass over what crosses back. It remains the one shipping crate with a
  `std` feature, for the dlopen of the Zig runtime.
- `vehje` (keep, extend). The two public contracts (`Grammar`, the `Target` re-export), the orchestration that is
  the dev-time language compiler (driving the signature through the check and lower interleave to `vehje-runtime-
  gen` for the language artifact, and a bundled program through resolve, check, lower, and emit for the per-program
  path), the distribution-composition entry point, and the reference distribution. The orchestrator owns the
  check-and-lower interleave (Stage 3, proof discharge folded into lowering), which is routed through `vehje-ir`
  grade annotations so `vehje-check` and `vehje-lower` stay acyclic and neither imports the other; a `vehje-ir`
  fold-observer trait is the seam if per-node alternation is later needed.

Three reserved seats are not created this round: `vehje-lex` and `vehje-syntax` (opt-in front-end building blocks;
the lint scopes already exist), created when a live consumer proves the shared shape per the committed input
contract and D1; and `vehje-stencil` (the copy-and-patch stencil-extraction toolchain, a dev-tool with a std role,
deferred, whose output is bytes for the stencil slot `vehje-runtime-gen`'s package schema reserves from day one).

The dependency layering is a clean topological order with no cycle. Foundations: notko, arvo (with arvo-bits,
arvo-bitmask, arvo-graph, and the content-hash primitive), hilavitkutin-api, hilavitkutin-str. Then `vehje-ir`
(foundations only) and `vehje-fixpoint` (foundations only, no `vehje-ir`); `vehje-signature` and `vehje-resolve`
on `vehje-ir`; `vehje-check` on `vehje-ir`, `vehje-resolve`, `vehje-fixpoint`, `vehje-signature`; `vehje-lower` on
`vehje-ir` and `vehje-fixpoint`; `vehje-schedule` on `vehje-ir` and arvo-graph; `vehje-codegen` on `vehje-ir` and
`vehje-check`; `vehje-runtime-abi` on `vehje-ir`; `vehje-runtime-gen` on `vehje-signature`, `vehje-check`,

## What ships as implementable core now, and what is deferred

The docs-equal-design discipline requires that a crate's DESIGN document name only types that exist in source (the
design-doc-source-mismatch lint), so the doc phase and the implementation co-advance: the DESIGN documents describe
the shipping contract, the BACKLOG documents hold the designed-but-deferred pieces (unbackticked, so the lint
ignores them), and the SHAME documents hold known gaps. This is not a retreat from the maximal design; it is the
honest split between what is built and what is designed-and-owed, which the mockspace surface is built to carry.

Ships as implementable core now, because it is settled and proven feasible: `vehje-ir` with the twelve forms, the
grade vocabulary (family, effect, lease, binding-time, and assurance grades as concrete enums and bitmasks reusing
the AccessSet machinery), and the one structural-hash definition; `vehje-signature`'s language-definition data
contract; `vehje-fixpoint`'s semi-naive engine core and its D6 differential and incremental layer (both benched);
`vehje-resolve` with the resolution side-table; `vehje-check` with the depth-lease floor, effect inclusion, and
the `Checked` witness (the immutable floor metatheorem is the proven valve, the binder rule bounds the naming set);
`vehje-lower`'s cheap bounded subset and its rule-table slice; `vehje-schedule` as-is; `vehje-codegen` (the output
machinery with the witness now imported from `vehje-check`); `vehje-runtime-abi`'s wire, value-arena, sink, and
batched-column entry surface (the `extern "C"` export lands with the panic-handler story, but the type surface and
layout are now-work); `vehje-runtime-gen`'s package and manifest in the manifest-check interim; `vehje-runtime-
driver` with the safe reader; and `vehje`'s public contracts, orchestration, and reference distribution.

Deferred to BACKLOG, because it is experiment-gated or frontier research, each with a named additive landing site:
the dev-time equality-saturation e-graph lands as the second stratum inside `vehje-lower` behind the same fold
interface (gated on the semantic-definition coverage experiment; the extractor bound to the iterative work-stack
discipline); the assurance-indexed gradual logical relation is a proof-document artifact (`lambda_veh`), its
grade-0 operational shadow the differential-check harness that `vehje-runtime-gen`'s contract already carries; the
copy-and-patch stencil toolchain is the deferred `vehje-stencil` crate producing bytes for the stencil slot
`vehje-runtime-gen`'s package schema reserves day one; the mutable-consumer lease extension grows inside
`vehje-check` and `vehje-ir` additively (the qualifier lattice), its per-reference generational residual a
wire-format flag in `vehje-runtime-abi` that is schema-now, semantics-later, gated on the avoidance-rate
experiment; the full multi-relation join and differential retraction beyond the additive-edit case grow inside
`vehje-fixpoint`; and the `Handle` multi-shot validation mechanism grows past its bounded-enumeration core. No
deferred piece requires a restructure; each is a stratum, a flag, or a future producer for a slot that already
exists.

## The doc-phase plan

The doc changelist rewrites the full documentation set to this consolidated design. The stale pre-round shape (the
eleven forms, the `Pure` / `Reads` / `Writes` effect lattice, the program-centric tier-tagged residual,
native-as-a-tier) is corrected everywhere. Each crate gets its README (the short feeder summary), its DESIGN (the
shipping contract, backticked types that will exist in source), its BACKLOG (the deferred pieces named above), its
SHAME where a gap needs an explicit escape, and its DEEPDIVE documents for the load-bearing mechanisms (the graded
spine, the reachability lease, certified generation, the value transport and the batched-column ABI, the
incremental scaling mechanism, the strategy portfolio). The root DESIGN is rewritten to the two-artifact model, the
identity, and the current crate taxonomy. Tier-1 public surfaces (the root README, rustdoc) carry no
workspace-internal vocabulary (no mockspace, no design-round references, no cross-repo paths), per the
documentation-writing rule. The doc CL locks; then the source changelist matches it and the implementation lands to
make every backticked DESIGN type real.
