## The through-line

The five decisions are not five independent knobs. Adopted together they collapse into one architecture whose
pieces reinforce each other, and the bias throughout is the bleeding-edge composition rather than the
conservative default, because the type system is vehje's core strength and the aim is the fastest and soundest
lang-spec-plus-runtime when taken to completion. Novelty here means a paradigm proven in the literature but not
yet in a production runtime, made load-bearing and implemented, and fitted to the other calls rather than taken
as-is.

## The five converged directions

### 1. ABI-topic re-voicing (settled)

The committed ABI topic (`202607192340`) is re-voiced under the three-codegens reframe (1513). `wire.rs` is the
binding-time-neutral IR image (produced by the Rust side for bundled scripts and by the composed runtime's
parser for arriving scripts, one interpreter over both). The `Tier` enum narrows to name only the internal
execution form (interpret-the-arena versus run-the-bytecode). Native leaves the tier axis entirely and becomes
one output-generation target. This is drift correction, and it clears the ground for point 4 by making "how does
the native target produce code for an arriving script" a well-posed question.

### 2. The graded (co)modal proof spine, scoped (settled)

The three axes (binding time, effect, lease) plus assurance are unified by construction, not by analogy, as one
graded (co)modal type system: binding time a graded necessity modality (Davies-Pfenning), effects a graded
monad (Katsumata), lease/usage a coeffect (Petricek-Orchard-Mycroft), combined by Gaboardi et al. (2016) and
demonstrated implementable in Granule. One graded soundness theorem with three corollaries, and the
axis-interaction rules (what happens when effect, coeffect, and binding time meet on `Apply`) fall out of the
typing rules rather than being unwritten case analyses. Scoping is the discipline: the grades live in the proof
and in the design document's "why these compose" section, never in the runtime types, which stay concrete
closed enums. The one place grading changes a design decision is the lease bit, which is a coeffect (per-operand
usage), not an effect (per-op world-change), recovering the per-operand precision the effect-only filing loses.
Target the full spine; the fallback if the theory proves too heavy is to grade only effect and lease, which
still gets the lease-as-coeffect win. Theory compacted in
`202607201854_synth_graded-and-comodal-type-systems.md`.

### 3. The handoff closed by construction, then checked (settled)

The Rust-to-Zig const emitter is the one uncertified step in the certified-generation story (the analogue of
CompCert's trusted pretty-printer rim). It is closed by making the Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig
comptime consts both generated projections of the one declarative language definition the whole architecture
centers on (the lens principle, Foster et al.), rather than one side serialising the other, and the projection
that reaches Zig is verified against the canonical definition by a content-addressed manifest at comptime
specialise time (proof-carrying-code on the data blob). That is a third certification at our build, on the
handoff. The manifest check alone is the cheap interim if the full one-source-two-projections story is staged in
later.

### 4. Copy-and-patch as the native tier for arriving scripts (settled; commitment gated on the experiment)

Native for arriving scripts is the one thing the current design silently cannot build (no compiler at the embed
site). Copy-and-patch (Xu-Kjolstad, the CPython 3.13 JIT; Deegen's baseline JIT) closes it: pre-compiled
machine-code stencils shipped as data, patched at load by a bounded relocation pass, no compiler present. Three
consequences make it the keystone: it lifts arriving scripts from interpret-or-bytecode to baseline-JIT native;
it collapses the data-versus-behavior tension into "all data, where some of the data is machine-code stencils,"
closer to the all-data taste than an interpreter-plus-escape hybrid; and it is generated from the same one
semantic definition Deegen proves (interpreter, stencils, and harness tests from one source), slotting into an
eBPF-shaped verify-then-patch split with a named minimal trusted base. Build mechanism is two-tool, matching the
already-blessed three-loci split: comptime keeps the `@Type` reification only it can do; stencil compilation is
a build step (a trusted optimising compiler at our build time, the native-tier analogue of the
content-validation build step). The direction is settled; the commitment gates on the pivotal experiment (the
semantic-definition coverage, below), with Deegen's tiered shape (stencil the hot, interpret the cold, tier on
heat) as the containment and the per-frame ceiling for ikiuni a bench question. Theory compacted in
`202607201854_synth_systems-and-codegen-frontier.md`.

### 5. Reachability types as the primary lease paradigm, fitted (settled)

Op's call, overriding the earlier "ship generational references, aspire to reachability types" lean: the type
system is vehje's strength, and adopting a paradigm proven in the literature but not in a production runtime and
making it implemented is the right kind of novelty, so reachability types (Bao-Wei-Bračevac-Jiang-He-Rompf,
OOPSLA 2021, and the polymorphic, flow-sensitive-effect, and avoidance successors) are the primary lease
paradigm, not a fallback, fitted to the other four calls. The fit is not forced: reachability types are a
coeffect, and the arc independently arrived at "the lease is a coeffect," so they are the same structure
approached from two directions.

The settled fit:

- The lease coeffect's grade (point 2) is a reachability qualifier, the set of binders a value can reach. Point
  2 established the lease is a coeffect with some grade; reachability types supply the grade, so without them the
  coeffect is a placeholder and with them it is concrete and inferable. "Free to Move: Reachability Types with
  Flow-Sensitive Effects" (2025) already fuses reachability with effects, which is the same region-and-effect
  unification call 1 needs, so reachability strengthens call 1's derive-the-lease-bit-from-the-effect move
  rather than conflicting with it.
- The qualifier is realised as a fixed-capacity bitmask over in-scope binders, the same AccessSet shape as the
  family and effect axes, with inclusion the same set primitive. Three axes, one bitmask machinery, one
  inclusion check; no_alloc storage is a bounded bitset per value, bounded by the depth cap times the per-scope
  binder width. Reachability inference reuses the existing typestate root rather than a parallel analyzer.
- The immutable strictly-LIFO depth-lease is the degenerate reachability qualifier (reachability collapsed to
  lexical nesting). This is the risk valve: ship the degenerate case first on the proven cheap metatheorem, then
  grow the grade lattice into the full qualifier additively, unlocking the mutable, aliased, and escaping cases.
  The full lease axis is not bet on unproven inference; the mutable-consumer extension is, over a proven floor.
- Reachability inference is compile-side and erased into certified region structure, so the runtime inherits
  proven-valid references with no collector, exactly as the other axes are compiled away.
- The closure-escape case the `Lambda` form makes first-class (the load-bearing region-soundness obligation
  immutability does not remove) is what reachability types were built for over Tofte-Talpin, so the primary
  paradigm is aimed at the hardest case the forms surfaced.
- Generational references (Vale's mechanism) demote to the gradual dynamic residual of the reachability coeffect
  at the avoidance boundary (the moment a value escapes and its type cannot be expressed without the escaped
  binder, named by the 2025 bidirectional-typing avoidance work, which is 1316's lease-inference-failure
  moment). Avoidance succeeds, static reachability proof (high grade); avoidance fails, a per-reference
  generational check (low grade) for exactly the un-placeable reference, not a whole-value collapse. This is the
  graded-assurance dial made concrete on the lease axis, so the residual has a home rather than being a parallel
  fallback.

Two design items and one added experiment gate the completion, not the direction:

- Design item: the value-arena tree-versus-shared-DAG decision. A reachability qualifier is richer than a scalar
  depth, so single-pass depth-min propagation weakens to a bounded fixpoint with memoisation over a shared DAG
  (still monotone, finite-height, terminating, not one-pass). Whether the arena shares subvalues or is a tree
  decides single-pass versus bounded-fixpoint, and it is load-bearing (this is the H6 sharing question).
- Design item: the avoidance boundary and its per-reference residual, where the real implementation work and the
  novelty-through-implementation concentrate.
- Experiment (added, alongside the semantic-definition coverage experiment): measure the avoidance/failure rate
  on real Lua-shaped census code. A low rate is the type-system strength realised; a high rate is a mostly-dynamic
  mutable lease learned early, before committing the mutable-consumer story.

Theory compacted in `202607201854_synth_regions-capabilities-and-substructural-memory.md` and
`..._synth_adjacent-theory-for-certified-generation.md`.

## The emergent overall shape

Taken together the five become one architecture:

- vehje is one declarative language definition, from which both the Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig
  runtime are generated projections, differential-checked by manifest (point 3). There is no trusted emitter;
  there is one truth, projected.
- The proof and design spine is one graded (co)modal judgment (point 2): binding time a graded modality,
  effects a graded monad, lease and usage a coeffect whose grade is a reachability qualifier (point 5),
  assurance itself a grade; one soundness theorem, axis interactions derived. Runtime types stay concrete;
  grades are the verification layer.
- The runtime is generated Deegen-style from one semantic definition into three artifacts (point 4): a tier-0
  arena interpreter, copy-and-patch native stencils, and the harness and differential tests. Native is a real
  tier for arriving scripts with no compiler at the embed site; data-versus-behavior collapses to all-data with
  some machine-code stencils.
- The untrusted-load path is an eBPF-verifier-shaped bounded abstract interpretation (tristate numbers), its
  cost bounded by the depth cap, the same finite-height quantity that already bounds the no-alloc frontier.
- The lease axis is a graded spectrum (point 5): reachability-proven for the static case (immutable core and
  much of the aliased case), the generational-reference residual for exactly the references inference cannot
  place at the avoidance boundary, one coeffect axis with the harness as its oracle.
- The trusted computing base is minimal and named: the stencil compiler at our build, the patcher, the load
  verifier, and the per-family retention declarations kept honest by the harness. Everything else is certified
  by construction or tested in the open.

This is a metacompiler with a unified graded proof of three axes, whose lease grade is a reachability qualifier,
generating a copy-and-patch JIT per language from one semantic definition, with a load verifier bounded by its
own lease-lattice height. No shipped system does all of it; every individual piece is proven somewhere, and the
reachability-types axis is the piece proven in the literature but not yet in a production runtime.

## Secondary theory-to-need mappings

Beyond the five primary calls, the synth docs carry theory that answers a real vehje need at lower criticalness.
Each is named with the need it answers and its nature (an owed-piece answer, a critical-path item, a call made
mechanical, a door to mark, or a grounding that lowers a perceived risk). These are recorded so the mapping is
not lost; none is settled as a decision yet.

- Effect-ordering discipline (owed piece, 03). Answered by A-normal form (Flanagan-Sabry-Duba-Felleisen, PLDI
  1993) as the baseline order (every intermediate named, order equals binding order, no sequencing form needed)
  plus the LMS effect-summary edges (already in the purity synth) for the pinned-versus-reorderable distinction.
  Owed-piece answer.
- Untrusted-load check (owed, 1315). Answered by an eBPF-verifier-shaped bounded abstract interpretation over
  tristate numbers (Vishwanathan et al., CGO 2022, a proven domain to borrow) with cost bounded by the depth cap
  (the same finite-height number does quadruple duty), plus a simdjson-style SIMD structural pass (Langdale-Lemire,
  2019) for throughput. This forces one format decision now: the value-arena must be alignment-and-stride
  predictable to admit the SIMD pass later, a cheap decision now and an expensive change later. Owed-piece answer
  with a time-sensitive format decision.
- The iterative validation and inference kernel (required by call 4, since Zig comptime recursion overflows the
  compiler). Answered by defunctionalization (Reynolds 1972; Danvy-Nielsen, PPDP 2001) as the principled route
  from the recursive walk to an explicit work-stack machine, and the functional correspondence
  (Ager-Biernacki-Danvy-Midtgaard, PPDP 2003) as the proof the iterative machine computes the same thing as the
  recursive specification. Owed-piece answer with a correctness proof attached.
- The streaming-spine proof (critical path, call 5). Answered by one-shot and linear continuations
  (Bruggeman-Waddell-Dybvig, PLDI 1996; Berdine-O'Hearn-Reddy-Thielecke, HOSC 2002): modelling the streaming
  producer as a defunctionalised one-shot coroutine collapses the cross-chunk lemma and the oversized-subtree
  proof into one continuation-linearity statement, proved once. Critical-path item (it is exactly the lemma call
  5 elevated to build-blocking).
- The macro, const-fold, and CSE compile stage (the macro-expander debt, which 1845 says still owes its own
  experiment arm). Answered by equality saturation (egg, PACMPL 2021; egglog, PLDI 2023) as a saturate-then-extract
  that unifies the three passes, with the LMS effect edges expressed as egglog extraction constraints so a rewrite
  never reorders an effect past a dependent read. The no-alloc, fixed-capacity e-graph is the research risk and
  the open sub-question. Owed-piece answer with a named risk.
- The input side under the reframe (owed, 03 piece 7: arriving scripts are parsed inside the composed runtime, so
  the parser is Zig or generated, not a Rust combinator). Answered by attribute grammars (Knuth 1968) plus PEG or
  GLL (Ford 2004; Scott-Johnstone) as a declarative grammar table a general parser interprets, with a bounded
  per-language lexer hook. The lease inference as a single bottom-up synthesised attribute is the same
  attribute-grammar frame, which is why op's one-pass constraint is the natural shape of an L-attributed
  evaluation, not merely an optimisation. Owed-piece answer.
- The assurance inventory made mechanical (call 6). Answered by quantitative type theory (Atkey, LICS 2018;
  McBride, 2016): the guarantee inventory becomes a computed grade vector on one assurance lattice
  (static-certified, structurally-certified, re-proven-per-instance, dynamically-checked, tested, unclaimed)
  rather than a hand-curated list, so "certified" is a point on a scale with an algebra. This ties directly into
  the graded spine (point 2). Call-made-mechanical.
- The algebraic-effects door (H7). Algebraic effects and handlers (Plotkin-Pretnar, ESOP 2009) are the semantic
  foundation under which the effect set, the host-call surface, and macro expansion are three handler instances.
  The recommended move now is to record the door (host-calls and macro expansion are the two handler instances a
  later algebraic-effects generalisation would unify) so a future maintainer wanting user-defined control
  (generators, async, backtracking, the coroutine and event needs ikiuni's future may bring) does not re-derive
  the one plausible twelfth Core form from scratch. Door to mark.
- The output-axis grounding (confidence, not a decision). MLIR and IREE (Lattner et al., CGO 2021), SPIR-V
  `OpCapability` checked by `spirv-val`, and Slang's multi-target IR are the production-scale existence proof that
  the output-generation spectrum and inclusion-not-coverage are shipped abstractions, so the output axis is the
  least-risky of the arc, and this is also the systems-audience framing that makes the design recognisable on
  sight. Grounding that lowers a perceived risk.
- The trusted build step (engineering precedent). Hermetic and reproducible builds (the Nix and Bazel lineage)
  are the precedent for the content-validation and stencil-compilation build steps as pinned, isolated, trusted
  members of the computing base, trusted because their inputs and toolchain are fixed. Grounding for how the
  build-time TCB items are trusted.
- The runtime-generation ergonomics (how runtime generation emits the per-language parts from a declarative
  record). Answered by the nanopass and MLIR-ODS grammar-driven default-clause generation already in the
  staged-metaprogramming synth: author only the clauses that change and auto-generate the pass-through clauses,
  which is the ergonomics for the family-fold extension (03's owed hook, where a family walks its own
  sub-structure). Owed-piece answer for the generation machinery.

## The bleeding-edge full-pipeline proposal (the spec to attack)

This section owns one maximal composition rather than a menu. It is the full theoretical spec, end to end from a
script arriving to a value crossing back, built on the five converged directions and the reachability fit, and
it fills the holes those left (the input side, the lowering, the value domain, the compile stage, the memory
model) with the most synergistic combination of the theory the synth docs carry. It prefers a novel adaptation
over a standard use wherever the standard use would leave a seam. Where theory reaches, it is named and used;
where it does not, the original work is named as original. This is the spec we attack and evaluate next, on
paper first; benching and sketching come after. The governing conviction is 04's: excellence is composing
established pieces until a new shape falls out that supersedes them.

### The spine: one graded judgment, and assurance computed from binding time

Every axis is a coordinate of one graded (co)modal judgment `Γ ⊢ e : A`, not a separate analysis. The context
`Γ` carries a per-binder coeffect grade (the binder's reachability qualifier, its lease); the conclusion carries
an effect grade (its `Permits` obligation, a graded monad, Katsumata) and a modal grade (its binding time, a
graded necessity modality, Davies-Pfenning); the type `A` carries, per property, an assurance grade. Effects and
coeffects are combined by the graded adjunction (Gaboardi et al. 2016), and Granule is the evidence the whole
thing typechecks. One soundness theorem; the per-axis theorems (staging soundness, the gradual guarantee, region
soundness) are its corollaries, and the axis interactions (what happens when effect, coeffect, and binding time
meet on `Apply`) are its typing rules rather than unwritten case analyses. The grades live in the proof and in
the design's coherence argument; the runtime types stay concrete closed enums.

The novel move at the spine, beyond adopting grading: the assurance grade is not an independent axis, it is a
projection of the modal grade crossed with the property. A property whose inputs are all available at an early
binding-time (modal) grade is discharged statically (high assurance, folded to a compile error); a property
whose inputs arrive late is a bounded dynamic residual (low assurance). So "discharge each property at the
earliest binding time its inputs are known" (the staged-gradual rule, the first Futamura projection) becomes a
computation of the assurance grade from the knowledge-source lattice. The guarantee inventory (call 6) is then
literally a computed grade vector (quantitative type theory, Atkey), not a hand-curated list, and "certified" is
a point on a scale with an algebra. Binding time is the knowledge-source join-semilattice (04 H3: sources are
{language-author, bundler, host-loader, runtime}, joined, not a chain), so bundling and partial bundling
(a template known at our build with data arriving at the host) is a join, not a special case.

### The one source: a graded algebraic signature carrying its own grammar

The single declarative language definition is a graded algebraic signature: the families are the operations of
an algebraic-effect theory (Plotkin-Pretnar), each operation carrying its effect grade and its per-operand
coeffect (reachability) discipline; the grammar is an attribute grammar (Knuth) attached to the same signature;
the value domain and the IR are two views of the same operations. The Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig
runtime consts are both lens-projections of this one signature (Foster et al., point 3), differential-checked by
manifest, so there is no trusted emitter.

The novel move: the node algebra has three projections, not one closed set plus parallel sets. The eliminator
forms are the IR you fold; the introduction (constructor) forms are the produced-value domain (04 H5a, the
value-node-kinds as the introduction fragment, so a family that adds a constructor gets its value-kind for
free); and the introduction forms compiled to machine code are the copy-and-patch stencils (below). One
declaration, three views kept consistent by construction, which is the recurring "one truth, many projections"
winner applied to the node algebra itself. The families as algebraic-effect operations is also the recorded door
(H7): host-calls and macro expansion are two handler instances of the same signature, so user-defined control
(generators, coroutines, ikiuni's future event needs) is a later handler, not a twelfth Core form.

### Stage 1, the input side: scannerless PEG emitting a graded IR in one pass

Arriving scripts parse inside the composed runtime, so the parser is a general Zig engine over a declarative
grammar table, not a Rust combinator. The grammar table is a scannerless PEG (Ford 2004), or GLL (Scott-Johnstone)
where a family needs general context-free power. Scannerless is the deliberate adaptation: PEG operates on
characters directly with ordered choice, so lexing folds into parsing as one attribute-grammar evaluation, which
dissolves the fixed-lexer problem the extension-point round flagged (custom literals and significant indentation
become grammar rules, not a per-language lexer hook). Packrat memoisation makes it linear-time, and the memo
table is bounded by the depth cap so an untrusted script cannot blow it up.

The novel move: the parse is the first graded computation, not a bare tree build. The synthesised attributes an
L-attributed evaluation computes bottom-up over the parse are the initial grades (a literal's binding-time grade,
its pure effect grade, a fresh lease). Parsing emits a graded IR directly, so parsing and initial grading are one
pass, which is the "lease as a synthesised attribute" insight generalised from the lease to every grade.

### Stage 2, lowering: binding-time-graded, effect-constrained equality saturation

Lowering, constant folding, macro expansion, and common-subexpression elimination are one stage, not four passes:
a binding-time-graded equality saturation (egg, egglog, 05 novelty 4). The rewrite rules are the lowering rules,
the const-fold rules, the macro-expansion rules (a macro is a rewrite handled at the script-build handler), and
the algebraic identities that expose CSE. The e-graph nodes carry binding-time and effect grades, and extraction
is constrained two ways: it respects the effect-ordering edges (LMS Reflect/Reify, so no rewrite reorders an
effect past a dependent read), and it optimises for the maximally-residualised form (the most discharged at the
earliest binding time). The output normal form is A-normal form (Flanagan et al.), so effect order is explicit in
the residual.

The novel move, and it is a real one: partial evaluation becomes an extraction objective. The first Futamura
projection ("specialise away what is early-bound") is normally a dedicated partial-evaluation pass; here it is the
extraction preference in the e-graph (extract the form that folds the most under the known binding-time grades),
so partial evaluation, macro expansion, const-fold, and CSE are one saturate-then-extract with a
binding-time-and-effect-constrained objective. No one has cast partial evaluation as effect-constrained,
binding-time-graded e-graph extraction; that composition is ours to build. The no-alloc, fixed-capacity e-graph is
original work (05 flagged that egg and egglog allocate), and the adaptation that makes it no-alloc is streaming:
the e-graph processes a bounded region window, saturates it, extracts, and moves on, so it never holds more than a
bounded frontier, bounded by the depth cap. The compile-stage e-graph and the value-transfer stage share this
bounded-frontier discipline (below).

### Stage 3, the proof discharge, folded into lowering: reachability coeffect and the avoidance residual

The proofs discharge during the graded fold of stage 2, not as separate passes. Reachability inference computes
the lease coeffect grades (reachability qualifiers as fixed-capacity AccessSet-bitmasks over in-scope binders,
reusing the family/effect bitmask machinery), the effect inclusion checks the graded monad against `Permits`, and
the binding-time discharge is the extraction objective. The effect-coeffect adjunction derives the per-operand
link/consume bit from the declared effect where the effect is precise, and reachability refines it where the
effect is coarse, so call 1's derivation and the reachability grade are one graded computation. At the avoidance
boundary (the 2025 reachability avoidance result, which is 1316's inference-failure moment), the property's
assurance grade drops and a per-reference generational-reference check (Vale) is the dynamic residual for exactly
that un-placeable reference. All of it is erased into region tags in the residual; the runtime carries no prover.

### Stage 4, runtime generation: Deegen-style triple emission from the one signature

The composed runtime is generated Deegen-style (05 novelty 2) from the one graded signature: for each family
operation, the generator emits an interpreter arm, a copy-and-patch stencil (05 novelty 1), and a differential
test, all three from the single semantic definition. The stencils are the introduction-forms-compiled projection
from the one-source triple, so the value domain, the IR, and the stencils are the same algebra viewed three ways.
Build mechanism is two-tool per the three-loci split: Zig comptime does the `@Type` reification only it can do; a
trusted optimising compiler at our build time extracts the stencils (the Deegen and CPython-3.13 shape), and only
the stencil data ships. Nothing but the artifact ships; the generator is dev-time only (Terra saveobj shape).

### Stage 5, execution and the load verifier as the graded gradual residual

Execution is tiered: interpret the tier-0 arena, tier up hot code to copy-and-patch native (Deegen's tiered
shape). Native is a real tier for arriving scripts because the stencils are data patched at load with no compiler
present, and the trusted base is the stencil compiler at our build, the patcher, and the load verifier.

The novel move: the untrusted-load verifier is the gradual residual of the graded system, not a separate safety
pass. For an arriving script the reachability, effect, and binding-time grades computed at lowering are
re-verified at load as a bounded abstract interpretation, over tristate numbers (tnum, 05 novelty 3, a proven
domain borrowed) for the numeric and bounds part and over the reachability-bitmask lattice for the lease part.
That is the AGT-derived dynamic residual of each axis, realised as the eBPF verify-then-patch split: the verifier
proves the arriving artifact safe, then the patcher assembles native. Its cost is bounded by the depth cap, the
same finite-height quantity that bounds the no-alloc frontier, so the verifier's path-explosion pain (the eBPF
verifier's number-one cost) is killed by a bound the design already carries. The value-arena is laid out
SIMD-friendly (aligned, predictable strides) so the structural half runs at simdjson throughput.

### Stage 6, value transfer: the one-shot coroutine shared with the compile stage

The produced value crosses through the reserve/commit sink (an iteratee, Kiselyov), a host-lent capability
(object-capability, Miller), whose backpressure is inherent. The streaming producer is a defunctionalised
one-shot coroutine whose suspended frames are the chunks, and frontier-boundedness is the linearity of its
continuation (04 H4, one-shot/linear continuations), which proves the cross-chunk lemma and the oversized-subtree
property as one linearity statement.

The novel move: this is the same bounded-frontier one-shot-coroutine abstraction as the streaming e-graph in stage
2. The compile-stage streaming saturation and the runtime value streaming are two instances of one
continuation-linearity theory, so the frontier bound, the depth-cap sizing, and the acyclicity argument are proven
once and used at both ends of the pipeline.

### The memory model: region tags, shared-implies-promoted, no collector

Reachability-proven leases become region open and close markers in the value-arena, freed on stack discipline, no
collector and no refcount. For the reachability qualifier to stay a single-pass synthesised attribute over a
value-arena that shares subvalues (a DAG, not a tree), the adaptation is shared-implies-promoted (04 H6b): a
shared subvalue is a chunk root, so its lease is finalised at emission (the meet degenerates to the promoted
region) and a later referrer takes the already-fixed lease. That makes the DAG lease inference single-pass by
construction and composes exactly with the chunker contract (cross-chunk implies root). CHERI (05 novelty 5) is
the optional hardware assurance grade for the lease axis: a CHERI output target enforces the reachability lease in
silicon (a region is a hardware capability, closing it revokes it, a use-after-close faults), which is additive,
not core, and raises the lease axis's assurance grade to hardware-enforced on that target.

### The synergies: one thing does many

The proposal is a shape, not a stack, because four things each do the work of many:

- The depth cap is one number doing six jobs: the no-alloc lease-frontier bound, the iterative work-stack bound,
  the untrusted-traversal depth bound, the load-verifier complexity bound, the packrat memo bound, and the
