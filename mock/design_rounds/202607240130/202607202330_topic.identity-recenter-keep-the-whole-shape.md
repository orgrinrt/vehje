# Topic: recentering the identity without unwalking a single step

**Phase:** TOPIC
**Round:** runtime output + lease proof

## Why this topic exists

A design can walk in exactly the right direction and still lose sight of where it is going. That is what happened
across the last three topics. The architecture they produced is sophisticated, exciting, and a close fit for what
vehje wants to be. But the reasoning that carried them there narrowed, step by step, until the record reads as if
vehje were a single-purpose JIT and native-lowering scripting runtime with a game engine as its reason to exist.
It is not that, and it never was. The tech is right; the identity slipped out from under it.

The correction is not a retreat. A previous attempt in this session to regain the identity over-compensated: it
started proposing to drop the native endgame, demote the JIT and copy-and-patch, diminish the converged shape,
and treat the last three topics as wrong turns. That is the opposite error and it is rejected here as firmly as
the drift it was reacting to. We keep the whole shape. We remove only the narrow focus that framed one part of it
as the whole. The metaphor that governs this topic: the walk was correct, the boots were wrong. You change the
boots and keep walking. You do not erase the ground already covered.

## What vehje is

Vehje is an embeddable, multi-input, multi-output IR framework whose identity is the type system as the
verification layer and certified generation as the mechanism. A grammar plugs in on the input side and lowers a
surface syntax into a shared IR; a target plugs in on the output side and emits. Vehje owns the shared Core, the
proof axes, the output machinery, and the runtime ABI. No single language is vehje; every language is a consumer.

The late progress of the last three topics is part of this identity now, not a detour from it. The three static
proof axes (family inclusion, effect inclusion, lease and region validity) are unified as one graded (co)modal
judgment: binding time a graded necessity modality, effects a graded monad, lease and usage a coeffect whose
grade is a reachability qualifier, assurance itself a grade. One soundness theorem, the per-axis results its
corollaries, the axis interactions its typing rules. The proof is discharged before lowering and then compiled
away into a doubly-certified runtime that carries no prover, the certified-generation resolution (Rust emits
validated data, the runtime's own compiler specialises to it, both certified at our build, neither at the
consumer). This graded, certified, statically-verified spine is the strength vehje is built to have, and it is
the reason the framework can be ambitious about everything downstream of it.

## Who it is for, and what that does not mean

Vehje serves a census of consumers, and it serves them equally. The representative and largest of them lean
toward authoring and templating rather than long-lived runtime logic: mockspace's columnar-query plus
procedural-document DSL, polka-dots' `.polka` for describing dotfiles, and their kin are closer to typst or
scribble than to a game's behavior scripts. They evaluate most of the build environment away and emit configured
data or documents, and their speed concern is the compile stage and the emit, so that mod and content stacks do
not bloat load times the way Clausewitz and RimWorld stacks do as content piles on. That end of the census is
real and it is first-class.

What that does not mean, and where the over-compensation went wrong: the simpler consumers do not cap the
ambition. We still want to lower to a JIT where it applies, still want native performance, still want the
bleeding-edge theory, the novelty, the efficiency, and the exploration. A framework that serves both a templating
DSL and a language that wants machine-code performance is stronger than one that serves only the first, and the
whole point of a spectrum of output targets is that the simple consumer and the ambitious consumer are the same
machinery aimed at different points. Serving the simple end is not a reason to abandon the ambitious end. It is a
reason to make sure the ambitious end is one option among many rather than the only door.

## Where native and the JIT actually sit

Output generation is a spectrum from interpretation to transpilation, and native is a real point on it, at the
far end where a script is lowered all the way to machine code. That is a genuine endgame target, not a phantom.
The `202607201513` line "there is no native endgame" is stale and wrong on this point: we do have a native
endgame. What that topic was right to kill was native as a privileged tier sitting above the others; what it was
wrong to conclude was that native therefore is not a real destination. It is one destination among several, and
it is ours to build.

Two corrections to how native was framed:

- The native lowering is ours, by whatever method we choose, and is not locked to Zig. The `202607201513`
  proposal to "generate Zig and let the Zig compiler own the platforms" is stale; there is no reason to bind the
  native output path to Zig specifically. We may emit Zig, or LLVM or cranelift IR, or machine code directly, or
  reuse the copy-and-patch stencils, per target and per what serves best. The composed runtime being authored in
  Zig and specialised by Zig comptime is a separate, settled matter and stays; the native output target's codegen
  method is open and method-agnostic.
- Copy-and-patch is the accelerator end of the native path, and it stays as a genuine highlight. Getting a
  baseline JIT and near-native performance almost for free, from the same one semantic definition that generates
  the interpreter, is a real win and a keystone feature. The correction is only that it is one keystone among
  several, not the primary purpose and not the lone reason vehje exists. It is a very nice thing among many.

## The last three, re-voiced (kept, not dropped)

Each of the last three topics contributed real, correct architecture. The only thing this topic changes is the
framing that let one part read as the whole.

**2001, the converged shape.** The five converged directions, the reachability-types lease fit, and the
full-pipeline proposal all stand. Copy-and-patch as a keystone feature is correct; the error was the surrounding
language that let it read as the primary keystone and lone purpose. It is one nice thing among many. The topic's
own closing description of the emergent shape (one declarative signature, one graded judgment, one semantic
definition projected many ways) is exactly right, and it is the description to carry forward.

**2055, the grounded stack.** This is the kind of work we want to do and the designs we want to strive for.
Doubling down on the theory and the newer explorations is desired, not a mistake. There is no reason the runtime
and the IR cannot live inside a relational engine, with the graded provenance-semiring spine and the rest of that
document. It was sophisticated and exciting, and it fits vehje's nature exactly. It is the tech under the hood
that empowers the aspiration of a framework serving many authoring DSLs, not a step away from that aspiration.
The only correction is that it was framed as the primary identity standing alone, when it is one part of the
primary identity. Right step, wrong boots; the step stays.

**2205, the Carmack rebuttal.** This is where the focus genuinely got lost, narrowing to runtime performance, the
native tier, the stencil toolchain, the interpreter hot loop, and one consumer's per-frame budget. But focusing on
runtime performance for a stretch was a worthwhile endeavour, not a bad one, and most of its conclusions and
threads stand, work, and fit right in. What is wrong is only that the reasoning path that produced them was a
narrow subsection of the whole presented as the whole. The conclusions are not wrong for having come through a
narrow door; they just need the full scope restated around them so future work does not mistake that door for the
building. The rebuttal's core stance also stays: we design through the hard parts rather than demoting them, and
Cluster C's honesty check (that the debate adopted more of the re-tiering than it admitted) is a note on rigor,
not a reason to abandon the maximal ambition.

## Cluster C's holes are where we work, not reasons to stop

The honest-keeper pass (`mock/research/202607202250`) found real gaps. None of them is a reason to drop or shrink
anything; each is a place the design now has work to do, which is exactly what a design phase is for:

- The `N*W` reachability bound does not follow from the `Reach` rules as written, because the propagation rule
  dropped the `InScope` filter, so the closure is `O(N)` per node until corrected. This is a rule fix in the lease
  design, and it is owed.
- The dual-locus "one generator, two projections" identity needs the differential test as a real gate, and the
  single-shared-object variant is the only layer that fully closes divergence. A decision on the assurance level
  is owed.
- The interpreter walk as a straight-line forward scan does not yet model branches, loops, or script-internal
  calls. The control-flow evaluation model is owed, and it is real design work, not a defect that condemns the
  approach.
- Zig 0.14's calling conventions do not currently expose `preserve_none`, so the register-pinned dispatch needs a
  sketch on the real toolchain before it is relied on. That sketch is owed.
- The seam between shipping only the load-verify residual subset and shipping a cheap runtime lowering subset is
  open, and it interacts with the simpler consumers, not only with a game runtime.
- The diagnostics and provenance schema (Cluster C's Part 2) is a full design that stands and should land in the
  wire format now, regardless of the identity framing.

These are the worklist. We answer them; we do not retreat from them.

## What stays, stated plainly

Everything. The graded (co)modal proof spine. Certified generation via Rust-emitted validated data and runtime
comptime specialisation. The relational-engine runtime and IR of 2055. Reachability-qualifier leases with the
depth-lease floor and the generational-reference residual. The value-arena transfer and the region proof. The
output-generation spectrum from interpret to transpile. The native endgame target, ours and method-agnostic. The
copy-and-patch accelerator, a keystone among keystones. The full-pipeline proposal of 2001 and its named original
work. We remove exactly one thing: the narrow framing that made runtime performance, native, JIT, and one hard
consumer read as the primary and lone identity. The architecture is the whole; that corner is one part of it.

## Addendum: the two-artifact model, and the "locus" clarification (2026-07-20)

The word "locus" was redefined several times across the recent topics and research, to the point of confusion.
This addendum fixes the canonical model so it stops drifting. It is spelled out because the drift it corrects is
subtle and kept recurring.

There are exactly two artifacts, and Rust lives in only one of them. Rust is entirely out of the runtimes.

1. The Rust language compiler. Dev-time only. It compiles the language spec you define (grammar, families,
   effects, lease rules, targets), proves that language's disciplines sound, and emits validated data (the family
   table, effect masks, lease-rule schema, wire layout) plus the proofs carried structurally. It never sees an
   end-user script, and it never ships.
2. The Zig composed runtime. The general engine (parser, analyzers, macro expander, interpreter, output targets)
   is authored once, by hand, in Zig. Zig's comptime specialises that hand-authored engine to the Rust-emitted
   data, and the Zig compiler certifies the specialisation at our build. This is the shipped artifact. It runs
   every script, bundled or arriving. No Rust, no rustc, no LLVM at the consumer.

The crux precision, which is exactly where the drift entered: Rust emits data, not Zig source. The generated Zig
is produced by Zig's comptime from that data, not text-printed by a Rust metaprogram. Topic 1627 explicitly chose
the comptime-specialiser shape and rejected "a Rust metaprogram emitting Zig source" as the hard-to-certify
(LMS-hard) route. The proofs then ride structurally in the specialised Zig: illegal states unrepresentable, total
dispatch over the family set, comptime-folded inclusion checks. rustc proves the data sound (first certification),
the Zig compiler proves the specialisation sound (second certification), both at our build, neither at the
consumer.

Three different "two-ness"es were being called "locus," and only the first is the architecture:

- Real and canonical: the Rust language compiler versus the Zig composed runtime. Two artifacts. This is the only
  "two" that is the architecture.
- Real but a different thing: inside the Zig runtime there is a compile stage (parse, analyze, macro-expand,
  lower a script) and an execute stage (run the residual). That is two stages of one Zig artifact, not two loci,
  and not Rust. It is the effect-axis compile/runtime split moved inside the runtime.
- The drift: the "dual-locus" framing invented a Rust-side per-script analysis locus ("Rust runs the analyses on
  bundled scripts, Zig runs them on arriving scripts") and had one Rust generator emit both a specialised Rust
  half and a specialised Zig half. Both are wrong. Rust never runs per-script analyses; the runtime does, for all
  scripts. And Rust-emitting-Zig-source is precisely the route 1627 rejected. The "two Zig things, a runtime and a
  bridge" that also appeared is a downstream artifact of trying to reconcile that false split; it dissolves the
  moment the false Rust-analysis-locus is dropped.

The fix is return to canon: one hand-authored Zig engine, comptime-specialised to Rust-emitted data, a single
backend. No Rust in the runtime, no Rust-emitted Zig source, no bridge. A host embedding the runtime over the C
ABI may itself be a Rust program, but that host is a consumer of the runtime, not part of it, and does not
reintroduce Rust into the runtime machinery. This drift was independently caught and corrected in the three
sibling research docs (`202607202345` solution 2, `202607202355` Finding 2, `202607202358`), all of which land on
the single-comptime-engine shape.
