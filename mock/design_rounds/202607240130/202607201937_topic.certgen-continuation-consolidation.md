## Part I: the parts that hold (verification and the settled calls)

### The paper trail's theory is sound (01)

Every theory citation in the panel's theory model (`08_theory_insights.md`) was re-verified: all sixteen
reference clusters name real, reachable papers, correctly attributed, each supporting the claim hung on it,
including the most falsifiable recent ones (Staged Gradual Typing, GPCE 2025; the KBS 2019 specialisation work).
The load-bearing move (derive the lease bit from the declared effect because region and effect are one system)
is soundly grounded and strengthened by papers 08 did not cite. Four attribution-precision notes to fold when
the proof document is drafted: Palsberg binding-time analysis is Palsberg and Schwartzbach; the gradual-effects
abstract-interpretation derivation is the JFP 2016 journal successor, not the ICFP 2014 origin; anchor the
staging-soundness theorem on the 2025 mechanised let-insertion work, not MetaML; anchor the lease-from-effect
move explicitly on Talpin-Jouvelot's polymorphic type-region-effect inference. One number-provenance caution:
the "$350 per source line" figure a search surfaces is seL4, not CompCert, and must not be attached.

### The seven calls settle as seven blesses (02)

Each of the seven open questions settled with a decision and a justification, folding in the three refinements
the theory model landed. The blesses: the depth-ladder lease schema; the three-loci split (content validation
to a native build step); spill as a host-lent sink policy; the hard depth cap as first-class; the cross-chunk

honest; everything else is certified by construction or tested in the open.

### The eleven Core forms hold (03)

Stress-tested against the full backdrop (certified generation, staged gradual verification, the lease axis, the

## Part II: the options and the decisions surfaced for op

The two audits settle nothing by design; each poked holes and offered concrete options, none binding. Together
with 03's one live contradiction, they surface the decisions below. Each names the fork; the detail is in the
cited document.

### Live contradiction to resolve: the ABI topic versus the reframe (03)

The committed ABI topic (`202607192340`) still encodes two framings the three-codegens reframe (1513) retired: a
program-centric residual crossing a C ABI per program, and native as the top execution tier. The
three-binding-time reconciliation dissolves it (the tier-0 arena is the binding-time-neutral IR image, produced
by the Rust side for bundled scripts and by the composed runtime's parser for arriving scripts; native is a
target, not a tier). The decision for op: re-voice the committed ABI topic under the metacompiler reframe, the
way 1513 re-voiced the transfer and lease topics, and move native out of the tier axis.

### The graded (co)modal recombination (04, the centerpiece)

The audit's central thesis: the arc carries three monotone structures (binding time, effect, lease) and unifies
them only by analogy ("each is an instance of staged gradual verification"), which is a filing system, not a
theorem. The recombination sitting in the banked theory unifies them by construction: binding time is a graded
necessity modality, effects a graded monad, leases a coeffect, combined by "Combining Effects and Coeffects via
Grading" and demonstrated in Granule, yielding one graded soundness theorem with three corollaries and axis
interactions that fall out of the typing rules rather than being argued. Options: adopt grading as the
proof-document spine (one metatheorem); adopt it only for the two axes that most want it (effect and lease);
or decline it and state explicitly that the three-lattice coherence is assumed, not proven. The theory is
compacted in the graded-and-comodal synth doc.

### The trust seam the differential gate misses (04, the sharpest fair catch)

Translation validation is placed on the kernel (where the mix equation already makes agreement near-tautological)
but not on the Rust-to-Zig const emitter, which is the unnamed member of the trusted computing base and the
exact analogue of the unverified pretty-printer rim CompCert trusts and the arc itself cites. Both certifications
can pass while the emitted consts are a faithless image of the validated table. Options: round-trip translation
validation on the handoff (re-serialise on the Zig side and diff by content hash); one declarative source with
the two ends as consistent projections (a lens); or a self-checking typed manifest verified at specialise time
(proof-carrying code on the data blob). Doing none leaves a certified generator whose one uncertified step is
the step that turns a proof into bytes.

### The systems tier under the design (05)

The arc is programming-languages-theory complete and systems-and-codegen thin, and two production-shipped systems
turn out to be the arc's own thesis, absent from the trail. Deegen generates an interpreter plus a baseline JIT
from one semantic definition at LuaJIT-competitive speed, which is runtime generation benchmarked, and its
single-semantic-source shape reframes the pivotal experiment. Copy-and-patch ships a code generator as
pre-compiled stencils patched at load with no compiler present, which is the one mechanism that makes native a
real tier for arriving scripts rather than a silently bundled-only target. The sharpest question back: native
for arriving scripts is the single piece the current design cannot build, and copy-and-patch is what the
frontier already shipped to do it. Further compositions, all optioned: the eBPF verifier with tristate numbers
as the load-check model, with the depth cap as its free complexity bound; the mutable-consumer lease fallback as
a shipping runtime residual (generational references or revocable capabilities) rather than research-only
reachability types; equality saturation for the compile-stage macro, const-fold, and CSE stage; a speculative
CHERI-backed lease axis; and the MLIR, SPIR-V `OpCapability`, and Slang framing that lets a systems audience
recognise the output and inclusion axes as shipped abstractions. The theory and systems are compacted in the
systems-and-codegen-frontier synth doc.

### The pivotal experiment, reframed (03, 05)

03 named the single highest-value next experiment: enumerate the L2 primitive vocabulary across the real census
families, because that decides the entire bridge shape (all-data if bounded, a generated-behavior escape if it
sprawls). 05 reframes it more directly: author the Deegen-style semantic definition and measure its census
coverage, which produces the interpreter and the stencils as byproducts rather than as separate later work.
Either framing, it runs before the bridge commits.
