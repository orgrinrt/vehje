Lines 62-77 — "Front-end review notes" block retires (the
problem the note predicts — `ResolveError` being non-`Copy` due
to owned `String` fields — is resolved by the sweep):

```
- ## Front-end review notes
-
- - **`ResolveError` is not `Copy`** because
-   `UnresolvedIdentifier.name` and `DuplicateDefinition.name` hold
-   owned `String`. This breaks the `E: Copy + Into<Diagnostic>`
-   symmetry that the rest of the front-end preserves
-   (`SyntaxError` is `Copy`; `Diagnostic` is `Copy`). The fix
-   lands naturally with the Symbol-interning round listed under
-   "Non-blocking follow-ups" above: once `Symbol::name` is an
-   `InternedSymbol` (or similar small `Copy` handle), the two
-   `ResolveError` variants become `Copy` at no extra cost and
-   `ResolveError` itself becomes `Copy`. Do not try to work
-   around the issue before the interning round lands — the
-   owned `String` is load-bearing while names are raw strings,
-   and short-term workarounds (e.g. splitting the enum into
-   `Copy` and non-`Copy` halves) create more surface than they
-   save.
+ ## Scheduler handoff for collection fields
+
+ The four skeleton-backed collection fields (`Scope::symbols`,
+ `ScopeTree::scopes`, `Resolved::resolution`,
+ `Manifest::dependencies`) are tracked in SHAME.md against
+ #131 (clause-schedule as hilavitkutin WorkUnit home) and #134
+ (persistence spine). Once the scheduler owns resolver state,
+ each field moves to a scheduler-managed `Column<T>` or a
+ `Map<K, V, N: Cap>` descriptor over an AccessSet-typed
+ read/write pair. Do not rewrite the fields inline before
+ #131 lands.
```
