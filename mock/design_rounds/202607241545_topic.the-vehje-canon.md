## 1. The centre: one idea carried all the way

Distilled to a single statement, and this is a genuine fusion of the mechanism, the assurance model, and the resource discipline, not a list of pillars:

> vehje discharges an operation, an effect, a macro, and a proof each by a handler at the earliest binding time its inputs are known, and then erases that discharge, so a language written once as a single graded definition is proven sound and specialised away stage by stage into two certified artifacts that carry validated data rather than source, and run with no prover, no heap, and no collector.

The reason this is one idea and not four is the whole point. Discharging an operation at the earliest binding time its inputs are known is partial evaluation, which is the first Futamura projection, staged. Discharging a proof at its stage and then erasing it is the same move on the assurance axis: prove before lowering, then compile the proof away. Servicing an effect, expanding a macro, and answering a host-call are the same move on the operation axis: each is an operation, a handler is what services it, and which stage provides the handler is exactly the binding-time coordinate. So "handler discipline," "certified generation, data not source," and "the graded proof spine" are not three commitments held together by taste. They are one discipline (discharge at the earliest binding time, then erase) seen on three axes.

The shorter mnemonic, which survived every reviewer in the panel unchanged and is retained as a fast check rather than as the full statement:

> vehje is one binding-time-directed handler discipline, proven statically and compiled away, projected from one signature into two certified artifacts, over a spectrum of outputs with no heap and no collector.

The mnemonic is a memory aid, not the drift test. The drift test is Section 10, and it is deliberately broader than the mnemonic, because two load-bearing parts of the identity (the consumer census, and native-never-the-driver) are not derivable from the handler discipline and a proposal can honour the mechanism while betraying them.

## 2. The frontier: what is genuinely new and load-bearing

This is the foreground. Each item is a discovery of the recent design and bench work, stated with its scope, including the parts that are proven and the parts that are open. Nothing here is dressed up as more settled than it is; carrying the scope is itself the discipline.

### A. The compile/runtime line is a four-point binding-time lattice, and const-inlining is staged partial evaluation

The question that opened this round was where the line between the compiler and the runtime actually falls, given that inlining a constant literally into the residual means the residual can no longer be fully interpreted. The answer, arrived at independently from a staging read of the shipped code (Rompf) and from the canon's own axis, is that the line is not a line. It is the four-point binding-time lattice already in the IR (`grade.rs:105-115`): the knowledge sources {LanguageAuthor, Bundler, HostLoader, Runtime}, joined as a lattice because a partly-bundled, partly-arriving script is a join, not a point on a chain.

Ahead-of-time compilation lives at LanguageAuthor (the runtime-generation package plus the Zig comptime specialisation, which together are the first Futamura projection as a build step) and at Bundler (the Rust side inlining a constant known at bundle time). Load-time compilation, not profile-guided just-in-time, lives at HostLoader: this is the sense in which one shipped artifact, the runtime, contains a compile stage, a debt the ABI design already confesses. Interpretation is an execution form at Runtime of the already-specialised residual.

The consequence for const-inlining is precise. Literal inlining mandates a specialisation stage at the binding time the inputs become known, never a native step. Fully-interpreted is coherent as a per-stage property (a residual can be interpreted at Runtime) and incoherent as a whole-pipeline property (the pipeline as a whole always specialises something away before Runtime). That an independent staging read reconstructed this axis from the code alone is the strongest corroboration the axis is right.

### B. One handler discipline, and the system is agnostic about how a handler is discharged

Effects, host-calls, and macro expansion are one handler discipline. An effect is an operation; a handler is what services it; a host-call is a runtime-effect operation the host handles; a macro is a compile-stage-effect operation the compile stage handles. The build-versus-runtime split is therefore not drawn by hand: it is "which stage provides the handler," which is the binding-time coordinate of item A. The twelfth Core form, `Handle`, carries this (adapted from Plotkin-Pretnar, made no-alloc).

The sharpening the panel forced, and op ratified: this is one type-level unification over three implementation discharges, and the system itself has no opinion about which discharge realises a given handler. The three discharges are a compile-time handler that emits IR into a builder (macro expansion, Rust side); a runtime host-call handler that returns a value once and needs no continuation (Zig side); and a runtime user-defined handler that resumes a continuation, possibly many times. Only the third needs the hard machinery, and it has no runtime home yet (see C). The unifying frame is the load-bearing claim: `Handle` is one dispatch discipline the system is agnostic about, and the three discharges differ only in operational feasibility. Stating it as three siloed mechanisms would be the exact unify-by-analogy move the round is praised for refusing; stating it as one dispatch with three realisations is the unify-by-construction that holds. The honest note travels with it: the third discharge is unbuilt.

### C. CR1: no-alloc bounded multi-shot continuations, carried with their scope

`Handle` is designed to support multi-shot resumption without a heap, via a host-lent bounded budget with a static fit-proof. This is named as genuine vehje novelty (op, 2026-07-20): no shipping effect system is known to do no-alloc bounded multi-shot this way. It enters canon carrying its operational scope, in the exact shape the bench results carry their caveats, because the runtime double-take showed it is proven only in its trivial half.

Proven: bounded multi-shot by re-execution of a pure defunctionalised continuation. The sketch `sk2` enumerates a bounded choice space with a mixed-radix counter, re-running a pure caller-supplied function per choice into a host-lent budget, fail-closed at comptime if the budget cannot fit. This is unconditionally no-alloc and is the well-behaved case. It is also, honestly, the McCarthy way to do bounded search, and it re-executes rather than captures.

Open, and not a refinement but the actual hard problem: continuation reinstatement without a heap (prefix sharing, capturing the live slice between the prompt and the operation and resuming it many times without re-running the prefix), and multi-shot resumption in the presence of other effects in the handled body. The second is a soundness obligation, not an efficiency question: re-execution is only sound when the re-run prefix is pure, which is why production effect systems make resumption one-shot by default. The check pass cannot yet even see the interaction: effect subtraction for a handled operation is a FIXME (`vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:301`), and the `Handle` form defers the operation-and-resumption representation entirely (`vehje-ir/src/node.rs:140`).

The resolution of the open half is a bench-decided fork, not a decree (op, ratified). The two candidate representations are segmented capture-and-reinstate over a host-lent budget, versus compile-time CPS or defunctionalisation of the handled body into a data structure the interpreter walks. Build both, measure capture cost, resume cost, and the ABI shape each forces, and let the winner decide the `Handle` clause encoding, which is downstream of that choice. The red test is owed now: a multi-shot handler whose body performs a second effect, asserting the check either refuses it or the design states the replay semantics.

### D. The graded proof spine, unified by construction into one soundness theorem

The static proof axes are one graded judgement, not several analyses filed under one word. Binding time is a graded necessity modality (Davies-Pfenning), effects are a graded monad (Katsumata), lease and usage are a coeffect whose grade is a reachability qualifier (Petricek-Orchard-Mycroft, combined by Gaboardi et al. 2016, with Granule as the evidence it is implementable), and assurance is itself a grade. There is one soundness theorem; the per-axis results are its corollaries and the axis interactions are its typing rules, not unwritten case analyses. Unification by construction, not by analogy, is the load-bearing distinction: a filing system gives you one vocabulary, it does not give you one theorem.

State of the spine (verified against source, labelled as state). The spine is largely unbuilt: the check pass sets the binding grade empty today (`binding: Knowledge::empty()` at `vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:311`). It is a living intent and a load-bearing design commitment, not a shipped fact. Inclusion (item, and Section 4) is the part of the spine that is genuinely implemented.

### E. Certified generation, doubly certified, data not source

The proof is discharged in Rust before any lowering, then compiled into a runtime that carries no prover. Rust emits validated data plus structural proofs, not source; the Zig runtime's own compiler specialises to that data at our build. Both artifacts are certified at our build; neither is certified at the consumer. Rust-emitting-Zig-source (the LMS-hard route) was rejected as hard to certify. Name the certified property per checker, because a certification claim whose perimeter is unstated is the failure mode this lineage exists to prevent: rustc's type system certifies the well-formedness, inclusion, and grading of the emitted data, and the Zig compiler certifies that the comptime specialisation type-checks against that data. Neither certifies semantic preservation of any lowering, and nothing yet certifies the Zig engine's own evaluation logic; those remain in the trusted base below. Certified is a dial, not a binary: assurance is a grade per property (static-certified, structurally-certified, re-proven-per-instance, dynamically-checked, tested, unclaimed), composing by lattice meet, so a proof and its differential test are one object at two grades. The lineage is CompCert's trusted-rim honesty (name the one uncertified step), proof-carrying-code on the data blob, and certifying (re-checkable per instance) rather than certified. This item is the assurance axis of the one idea in Section 1.

The trusted base, stated in one place, because the lineage this item claims (CompCert's trusted-rim honesty) is precisely the discipline of writing the list down. What remains trusted today: the Zig compiler (the entire second certification rests on it); rustc (the first); the C ABI marshalling across the boundary; the byte-image hash function used for cross-artifact identity; the Rust-to-Zig emitter seam, until the lens projection lands (item F); the caller-supplied family and effect sets, until the program-derived-set derivation lands (Section 4, inclusion); and the load-time verifier's own decode logic. Each trusted element has owed work that shrinks it (the lens projection, the set derivation, the verifier), listed in Section 11. "Doubly certified, no prover shipped" is a true statement, and this is its perimeter.

### F. One signature, many projections, and no trusted emitter

One declarative language definition (a graded algebraic signature carrying its own grammar) is the single source. The Rust compile-side typestate and the Zig runtime consts are, by design, lens projections of it, differential-checked, so that there is no trusted emitter. The node algebra itself has three projections: the eliminator IR you fold, the introduction value-domain (content-as-values), and the introduction-compiled stencils. The trust does not leak at the kernel (one source compiled twice, agreement close to a tautology); it leaks at the Rust-to-Zig emitter seam, and the lens projection is the designed closure. State: today the emitter seam is trusted entirely. The lens projection is unbuilt, `vehje-runtime-gen` ignores its signature argument, the interim manifest check is a whole-package byte hash, and the check pass reads no signature schema; closing the seam (building the lens projection and the differential check) is owed (Section 11). The design claim stands; the present tense does not.

This is sound only with the identity boundary stated explicitly, and the shipped hash confirms the boundary: within-stage identity is the interner-local structural hash, and cross-stage (cross-artifact) identity is the byte-image hash (`vehje-ir/src/hash.rs` states the word-fold is interner-local; `vehje-runtime-gen` hashes the byte image). A canonical statement that named the one structural hash as the manifest and fixpoint dedup identity would be unsound as written; the boundary is the correction.

### G. The IR-to-IR staged expansion

Macro expansion and constant folding are IR-to-IR passes: an operation whose handler is provided by the compile stage rewrites the IR, and the pass iterates. The shape corrected from the superseded topic (`202607241330`), per op's live calls:

Arena strategy is not a single-versus-double binary. It is: adapt to N host-provided allocation spaces. A single caller-lent arena, grown in place by recursive handler discharge, is the default hypothesis and dissolves the cross-arena reference concern. But additional host-provided buffers are a legitimate and possibly better shape (independent expansion or recursion loops run in parallel where they do not touch each other, or a buffer serves as scratchpad or temporary-value storage), and whether and where multi-buffer wins is a bench-decided fork, not an assertion. Once a win is confirmed, the count is exposed as host configuration and our end adapts to whatever the host provides (if the host gives 255 spaces, maximise utilisation); the count decision is never made for the host, because each host differs. The `NodeRef` arena-branding decision rides this same fork: if single-arena wins, branding dissolves; if multi-buffer wins, cross-space references need branding, and it is load-bearing again. One fork, decided in one act.

Recursive macros are supported. This is a deliberate correction of the panel's instinct to ban same-stage re-introduction: op wants recursive macro expansion. A recursion depth cap is always present as the safety backstop, unconditionally, and static cyclicity detection on the Rust typestate is the primary termination mechanism layered above it wherever it can be proven statically (preferred; investigate as a sketch, and it makes the hang concern moot). A feasible static detector never removes the cap: the cap is the always-on hang-guard for a mis-graded family macro that evades static detection, and static detection layers on top as the primary mechanism, not as a replacement. The cap defaults to 255 and is host-configurable (a host may allow only 2, or 99, or the full 255). This cap is a hang-guard against infinite recursion, diagnosed as a named error, and it is categorically distinct from the two caps the round killed: it is not cap-as-semantics (X3, where which residual you get depends on the cap, the non-determinism the round rejected), and it is not the depth-cap pun (X5, one cap doing six jobs). It is a safety backstop compatible with the well-foundedness semantics.

The terminator is binding-time well-foundedness, in three layers, and the fuel-cap dispute dissolves once all three are stated. First, binding-time grade well-foundedness terminates the stage dimension. Second, a within-stage per-operation productivity condition terminates same-stage recursion (a family macro can emit another same-stage macro call; the productivity rule bounds it), and this needs a checkable home as data on the signature's `Operation`, not a convention. Third, a budget-as-diagnostic (a deterministic named error at arena exhaustion) is distinct from cap-as-semantics and distinct from passthrough at the family-expansion seam, so exhaustion never reads as a truncated program. The round already killed cap-based termination for this class of unfold (`202607202055`, PE-as-extraction: a no-alloc cap makes lowering non-deterministic; determinism comes from confluence on the static fragment). That kill was of partial-evaluation-as-e-graph-extraction specifically; the principle it established (well-foundedness for determinism, not a cap) is general and governs the recursive handler discharge too. The static-cyclicity detection or the depth-cap safety backstop of the previous paragraph is the hang-guard, not the termination semantics; the two are compatible because the backstop only fires on a mis-graded or genuinely non-terminating input, and it fires as a diagnostic.

The IR-to-IR rebuild is a catamorphism, and the shipped `fold_core` visitor shape cannot carry it (the visitor shape cannot create new nodes, which `Anf` and `MacroExpand` both need). Those passes are no-op FIXMEs today (`vehje-lower/src/lib.rs`: `Anf` at `:385`, `MacroExpand` at `:400`, `Saturate` deferred at `:85`). This is the first domino of the owed implementation work (Section 11), because the CFG-of-blocks residual lowering is downstream of it.

### H. What the benches taught, and the bench-honesty meta-lesson

The measurements settled the architecture and corrected five reported numbers, which is exactly why settling which-is-faster forks by bench is safe: every architecture call survived correction, and only reported numbers did not. The through-line: the value is in the cheap binding-time-directed reducer (fold plus CSE plus DCE, always-on) and the vertical-SIMD batched-column C ABI (one runtime-W entry, W at least 2, roughly 4.8x, the single biggest lever), not in the e-graph and not in the copy-and-patch stencil. Native is a modest scalar multiplier and a rare compute-bound opt-in, never the driver. The authoring-and-templating majority is compile-heavy and trivial to execute, so the interpreter's dispatch cost, not any native multiple, is the number that governs the product.

The meta-lesson is load-bearing and stays in the soul (Section 5): a bench that measures the wrong thing is worse than no bench. The framework's own soul (partial evaluation, the first Futamura projection) can hide inside its own benchmark, because a compiler that specialises an interpreter into native and then compares native to native measures a partial-evaluation artifact, not a speedup. The honest re-measure forces the workload across an opacity boundary. The corollary op added and ratified: benches are provisional and limited. Trust the data for exactly what it measured, extrapolate nothing further, treat the current benches as rudimentary, and keep building better and more representative ones.

### I. The maximal-shape, unify-by-construction ethos

The deepest layer, and the one most easily lost. Design the unification now, completely, before any code: a hole in a design is filled by finishing the design, not by choosing a design with fewer holes because it is easier today. Reject re-tiering as the defer instinct: a sound floor and a sufficient floor are different claims, and the floor being buildable today is not permission to stop designing above it. Compose established pieces until a new shape falls out that supersedes the pieces it was built from; never novelty for its own sake. And the sharp corollary that is itself a design instrument: a design that congratulates itself on reaching the field's standard answer has told you exactly where to look for the missed recombination. When a design reaches the field's default, treat it as the signpost to the missed idea, not as arrival. The counterbalance is equally load-bearing: keep the whole shape, reject both the drift (narrowing to one exciting corner) and the over-correction (dropping the ambitious endgame).

### J. op's fresh convictions (2026-07-24)

Two standing convictions op set this day and ratified into canon. First, N-buffer host-lent resource adaptivity (item G): the arena strategy adapts to however many allocation spaces the host provides, benched to find where multi-buffer wins, never limited to one or two by decree. Second, diagnostics are first-class, everywhere, not only at the terminator: a full diagnostic set of informative errors and actionable suggestions, with span links that integrate with an LSP and the rest of the tooling. This is a standing canonical requirement, as load-bearing as the strictness it accompanies: strict-by-design without first-class diagnostics is a wall; strict-by-design with them is a guide.

## 3. The base: the foundational identity the frontier rests on

These are settled and are the ground the frontier stands on, stated briefly because they are the base, not the headline.

Vehje is an embeddable, multi-input, multi-output IR framework. It is not a language. A grammar plugs into the input side and lowers a surface syntax into a shared IR; a target plugs into the output side, declares what it supports, and emits. Vehje owns the shared Core, the proof axes, the output machinery, and the runtime ABI. No single language is vehje; every language is a consumer, the rich general-purpose one included, named later, on equal footing with jomini, w3, ts4, lua, polka, ikiuni, viola, and mockspace.

The identity holds four facets. First, a framework serving a census of consumers equally: the representative and largest consumers lean toward authoring and templating (mockspace's columnar-query and procedural-document language, polka's dotfiles), evaluating the build environment away and emitting configured data or documents, so their speed concern is the compile stage and the emit, and that end is first-class; the game-runtime consumer (ikiuni) is one consumer, not the reason vehje exists. Second, a spectrum, not a single purpose: the templating end and the machine-code end are the same machinery aimed at different points of one output spectrum. Third, two artifacts, Rust in only one: a dev-time Rust language compiler (compiles a language definition, proves its disciplines sound, emits validated data plus proofs, never sees an end-user script, never ships) and a shipped Zig composed runtime (one hand-authored engine, comptime-specialised to the Rust-emitted data, running every script). Fourth, the type system is the verification layer and certified generation is the mechanism: illegal states are unrepresentable, dispatch over the family set is total, inclusion is comptime-folded, and both certifications happen at our build.

Open families are traits: the Rust trait system is the open definition system, so the framework Core stays small (a closed vocabulary the framework owns) while consumers carry their richness (the families they extend it with). Taken whole, the framework generates a native output point per language from one definition by whatever method benches best, with a load verifier bounded by its own lease-lattice height. That whole-picture framing is the ground; it is stated plainly here rather than as a headline, because the mechanism (Section 2) is what the framework actually is now.

The consumer census is a needs-signal, not a design target. This is a ratified distinction (op, VII3): the census tells us what needs to be served, so we anticipate and match those needs, but the design is driven by the benches, the prior art, and the maximal shape, never tailored to a specific consumer. Vehje is not any one consumer's tool. A design choice that reads as "because ikiuni needs it" or "because mockspace does it this way" is drift; the census grounds what the framework must be capable of serving, and the shape that serves it is chosen on the merits.

No heap, ever, on both sides. No null: absence is an explicit `Maybe` value form the consumer matches on, not a nullable reference (notko's fallibility ladder). No garbage collector and no reference counting: the lease and region proof is discharged statically, so the runtime inherits proven-valid, non-null references. These are the base the frontier's resource discipline (host-lent budgets, N allocation spaces, bounded continuations) is built on, and every borrowed mechanism pays the no-alloc port as its standing tax.

## 4. The soul: the load-bearing convictions, re-verified

Each conviction below was re-verified against the current settled state (the benches, the research, the panel's converged conclusions) rather than carried forward unexamined, per op's explicit mandate: the old statements predate the benches and may be stale, may hold, or may need augmentation. Where a conviction makes a claim about what currently ships, that claim is verified against source and labelled.

The type system is the verification layer, and the runtime is a dumb evaluator that inherits proven-safe programs. This is why vehje is written on this stack at all.

Certified generation: prove before lowering, then compile the proof away (Section 2E). Rust emits validated data, not source; both artifacts are certified at our build, neither at the consumer.

One graded (co)modal proof spine, unified by construction (Section 2D). State: largely unbuilt (the check pass sets the binding grade empty, `vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:311`); a living intent, not a shipped fact.

Inclusion, not coverage. A target declares the family set it supports and the effect set it permits; a program using anything outside those declared sets is refused with the offending construct named, never silently degraded. State: this is the implemented part of the spine. The sealed `Checked` witness exists (`vehje-typecheck/src/lib.rs:80`), minted only through `mint_checked` (`:158`) whose bounds require the program's families and effects to be contained in the target's (`ContainsAll`, `:164`). The program-derived family and effect sets are still half caller-supplied (a FIXME at `:154`), so inclusion is shipped in shape and half-wired in derivation.

The one generative idea: effects, host-calls, and macro expansion are one handler discipline, stated as one type-level unification over three implementation discharges, only the third needing continuation machinery (Section 2B, 2C).

Output generation is a spectrum from interpretation to transpilation; native is a real point on it, not a privileged tier (bench-backed).

Three named codegens, the bare word retired: runtime generation (per language, dev time), macro expansion (IR to IR, per script, compile stage), output generation (IR to output, per script). Collapsing these into one word is what kept derailing the design.

No heap, no null, no collector (Section 3), expanded now to N host-provided allocation spaces (Section 2G): the host-lent bounded budget is the one universal resource discipline, and its uses are the reserve/commit value-transfer sink, the reachability lease, and the bounded continuation. The three are one shape only up to their cost: the sink and the lease are cheap; the bounded continuation carries a re-execution cost and open reinstatement and under-effects cases, and is not presented beside the other two as the same cheap shape (Section 2C).

Strict by design, paired with first-class diagnostics (Section 2J). Lease-inference failure is a compile error that forces an explicit annotation, never a silent fall back to a nullable or collected value; and the error is informative and actionable, span-linked, not a bare refusal.

One truth, many projections (Section 2F). Sound only with the within-stage-structural / cross-stage-byte-image identity boundary stated; the shipped hash confirms the boundary. State: the emitter-seam closure (the lens projection that makes the projections differential-checked) is unbuilt, and the check reads no signature schema, so today the emitter seam is trusted and the differential check is owed (Section 11). The identity boundary is confirmed in source; the projection closure is intended.

Generative freshness, one discipline at two binding times (added per SPJ and Wingo, ratified). Macro hygiene (fresh binders so capture is structurally impossible, because resolve runs after expansion) and multi-shot-frame freshness (fresh continuation state per resume, so one resumption cannot corrupt another) are the same discipline: generative discharge is fresh by construction and bounded by a host-lent budget, and exhaustion is a named diagnostic distinct from a completed expansion or a completed resumption. This single statement closes the terminator's within-stage layer, the hygiene hole, and the multi-shot-freshness question at once, because they are one discipline seen at four sites: hygiene at macro expansion, freshness at continuation resumption, the terminator's within-stage productivity layer, and the budget-exhaustion diagnostic. Hygiene is the panel's largest open soundness item and was absent from both candidate enumerations; it is soul-shaped ("illegal states unrepresentable" applied to generation) and enters canon with a red capturing-macro test owed.

One shape does the work of many, but count the bounds honestly. The aesthetic (find the shape where one decision buys many) is soul. Its most-cited instance was a pun and is retired: the "one depth cap doing six jobs" was two of the six being a width or a length wearing the word "depth," replaced by three genuine finite structural bounds (environment-width, nesting-depth, input-length), each doing real work, AARA-inferred and folded into the graded spine. Hold the aesthetic; count the bounds as three. Three distinct things wear the word "depth" or "cap" and must never be conflated: the finite lease-lattice height that bounds the load verifier (a real bound of the graded spine); the retired six-jobs pun (dead); and the recursion-depth hang-guard backstop of the IR-to-IR expansion (Section 2G, a diagnostic, not semantics). Different mechanisms, one unfortunate shared word.

## 7. What the benches taught, in detail

The settled results, each with its honest caveat, later and more rigorous re-measures governing. Every number is trusted for exactly what its cell measured (Section 2H, benches-provisional).

Native is a modest scalar multiplier, stated as a range with its baseline index, not a pinned scalar. Roughly 1.5x to 2.0x over a good interpreter for scalar code, depending on interpreter form and dispatch predictability, growing on branchy programs, with the 10x class reserved for vectorisation (which copy-and-patch cannot do). The committed `carrier_native_ceiling` cell reports the switch interpreter at about 2.0x of native and states 2.0x is the floor at the interpreter's best case (a maximally predictable stream), and a PMU cell gives about 1.49x; the spread is unexplained in the record and owed as a measurement (one cell, both baselines, predictable and unpredictable streams, sizes past predictor capacity). The qualitative claim (native never the driver) is safe under every number on record. The earlier "doubly-superseded 2.0x" framing is dropped: the 2.0x is the switch-baseline number the carrier cell itself reproduces, not a stale intermediate.

Dispatch is tier-and-shape-dependent, not a flat winner. A plain switch is fastest for vehje's lean roughly 25-primitive straight-line IR (the branch is well-predicted, and threading's machinery costs more than it saves for so few op kinds), but preserve-none tail-threading wins on real control flow (the CFG-of-blocks regime, loops and branches). So: switch inside straight-line blocks, threading at the CFG terminator transfers. The consolidation's flat "switch beats threading 1.7x" is the stale form; the tier-dependent split is the truth.

The optimiser is fold plus CSE plus DCE, not the e-graph. Equality saturation's marginal contribution measured zero-or-negative and it is parked behind a named trigger; `cse+eqsat` is bit-identical in node count to `eqsat` alone; the once-reported "142x win" was eqsat-alone pessimization presented as a speedup. DCE is a named third arm. CSE is kept for node-count reduction (76% fewer nodes), not for latency (LLVM already CSEs the recompute). The consolidation's framing of the e-graph as "the one genuinely original research piece" is drift.

The batched-column C ABI is the one ABI-shaping result. Vertical/SoA SIMD is the biggest single win (about 4.8x on a symmetric baseline) and survives the FFI boundary byte-exact (2.3x to 4.1x); the crossing is nearly free (about 9 ns warm). The runtime exposes one runtime-W batched-column entry taking W at least 2, no per-W symbol zoo, because the runtime, not the ABI, owns the SIMD width. The first-pass over-claim (a circular W-at-least-8 threshold, a dispatch-amortisation figure mislabelled an ABI property, a cheap-payload regime never built, thermal drift) is recorded as measurement precedent.

Recursion is a hard compiler wall; the kernels are iterative. Recursive Zig comptime faults at about 2500 depth; the iterative defunctionalised work-stack folds 100k nodes clean. This promotes defunctionalization from the principled route to the only route, and binds the comptime kernel, the load verifier, and the extractor alike.

Native, refined: direct instruction selection compiles about 2x cheaper than the copy-and-patch stencil at equal warm speed, and the authoring majority produces short-lived residuals, so direct isel is the native point and copy-and-patch is de-prioritised (demoted, not deleted; the unmeasured compile-versus-warm breakeven decides whether a run-many-times residual ever justifies it).

The pieces compose with predictable additive cost. A 50,000-node script runs the full pipeline end to end in about 3.4 ms, compile-stage-dominated (about 3.24 ms) with a cheap runtime (about 127 us), matching the authoring majority's compile-heavy, trivial-execute profile exactly; incremental scaling answers the load-bloat concern (about 4 ms cold, 0.003 ms warm, 7.7x parallel). Register beats stack every profile (1.41x to 3.06x), so the middle tier is predecoded-register plus fold/CSE/DCE. The wire format keeps operands inline (pool indirection is the measured expensive part); the exact inline count washed to null on the proxy corpus and is a measure-then-lock decision against a real census corpus.

## 10. The drift test

This is the section a future reader applies to decide whether a proposal, a document, or a piece of code has drifted from the canon. It is written to be unambiguous, because it is what everyone downstream will read and draw conclusions from.

A proposal drifts from the canon if it violates the identity or the mechanism. The two are checked jointly and independently, and passing one does not excuse violating the other, because neither is derivable from the other. Do not use the one-sentence soul (Section 1) as the test: it is a mnemonic, and it is genuinely possible for a proposal to serve the binding-time-directed handler discipline perfectly and still drift, by betraying an identity commitment that the handler discipline does not imply.

The identity check (Section 3). A proposal drifts if it: recenters vehje on a single consumer, or treats any one consumer (ikiuni, mockspace, or another) as the reason vehje exists, rather than serving the census equally with the authoring-and-templating end first-class; narrows vehje to a language rather than a framework every language is a consumer of; drops or demotes either end of the output spectrum (the templating end or the native endgame); reintroduces a heap, a garbage collector, a nullable reference, or a trusted emitter; or lets a consumer's specific need, rather than the benches and the maximal shape, drive a design choice. The census is a needs-signal to anticipate and match, never a design target to tailor toward.

The mechanism check (Sections 2, 4, 5). A proposal drifts if it: unifies by analogy (a shared vocabulary) where the canon unifies by construction (one theorem, one signature, one graded judgement); reaches for a mechanism the inverse killed (a fuel cap as expansion semantics, cap-driven non-determinism, unify-by-analogy, lease-as-effect, a differential gate on the kernel instead of the emitter seam, Rust-emitting-Zig-source, a re-tiering of the ambitious mechanism off the critical path on grounds of effort); asserts a which-is-faster or which-shape-is-cheaper answer instead of benching it, or extrapolates a bench past what its cell measured; treats a demoted prior-art cite (copy-and-patch as keystone, eqsat as frontier, tail-threading as blanket winner) as still-married; presents the bounded continuation (CR1) as a settled cheap shape rather than carrying its scope (re-execution proven, reinstatement and under-effects open); ships a generative discharge (a macro, a resumption) that is not fresh by construction and bounded by a diagnosed budget; or states a cross-stage identity as the within-stage structural hash rather than the byte-image.

When identity and mechanism both hold, the proposal is on the canon. When either fails, it drifts, and the specific clause it failed names the correction. The mnemonic and the centre (Section 1) are the fast orientation; this section is the actual gate.
