# Doc changelist: complete the per-crate deepdive set

Doc-template additions for round `202607240900`. Adds the missing topic-specific deepdives so every crate's
documentation carries the design rationale and the bench/research evidence behind it, not only the DESIGN and
README. Nine deepdives, one per crate that lacks one. Each is grounded in that crate's DESIGN plus the relevant
findings from `202607240015`/`202607240700` and the `research/*_synth_*.md` trove. No existing doc is rewritten;
these are additive.

## vehje-ir/DEEPDIVE_ARENA_AND_HASH.md.tmpl (new)

The no-alloc caller-provided arena model (node arena, child-index pool, span and grade side-tables), the twelve
Core forms as a fixed-width slot, and the structural hash. The measured rationale: operands stay inline because
pool indirection costs 10.7 ns/node against 4.4 ns inline; the exact inline count is an open measure-then-lock
decision (the 24-byte three-operand flip washed to a null result under the carrier re-measure); the hash folds
leaf content and is interner-local.

## vehje-fixpoint/DEEPDIVE_RELATIONAL_ENGINE.md.tmpl (new)

The relational engine and the incremental scaling layer. Whole-column evaluation (the measured winner on
realistic shallow graphs, about 50 ms at 8M nodes, delta bookkeeping not repaid), congruence closure for the
e-graph client, and the D6 incremental numbers (4 ms cold, 0.003 ms warm, 7.7x parallel at 8 threads) as the
concrete answer to the load-bloat concern.

## vehje-lower/DEEPDIVE_LOWERING.md.tmpl (new)

The cheap stratum and the deferred saturation stratum. The node-count-reduction win (76% fewer nodes, the
design-relevant result rather than a per-op time win), the redirecting `Rewrite` and the variable-free CSE
capture-safety, and why the e-graph extractor is an iterative work-stack (recursion is a hard Zig-comptime
wall, SIGSEGV at about 2500 depth).

## vehje-codegen/DEEPDIVE_OUTPUT_SPECTRUM.md.tmpl (new)

The output spectrum (interpretation to transpilation as one axis) and proof-directed strategy selection. The
native ceiling (1.0x to 1.5x for scalar code, the 10x class reserved for a vectorising JIT), plain-switch
dispatch winning for the lean op vocabulary (tail-threading measured-and-rejected), and the tier-dependent
branch-strategy map.

## vehje-signature/DEEPDIVE_GRADED_SIGNATURE.md.tmpl (new)

The language-level graded algebraic signature and its split from the per-program `vehje-ir` (what a language
is versus what a program is): the operation catalogue with grades, the lease-rule schema the check pass reads,
the target declarations, and the attribute-grammar seam.

## vehje-resolve/DEEPDIVE_SCOPE_CHAIN.md.tmpl (new)

The borrowed, non-allocating scope chain (the flat shadow-stack at 0.91 ns per reference, 6x to 14x over a
scope-chain walk over an owned table), the recursive-`Let` rule, and the family-extension resolve seam.

## vehje-schedule/DEEPDIVE_PASS_DAG.md.tmpl (new)

The compile-side pass DAG over `arvo-graph`, its narrowed remit (ordering consumer family passes against the
Core stages, since the compile-and-verify side is one object rather than a resolve-and-check peer pipeline),
and why the runtime side uses hilavitkutin's morsel execution instead.

## vehje-runtime-gen/DEEPDIVE_PACKAGE_MANIFEST.md.tmpl (new)

The validated-data package (the composed per-language artifact), the content-addressed manifest, and the
deferred lens-projection handoff (the manifest-check interim shipping first).

## vehje-runtime-driver/DEEPDIVE_DISPATCH_AND_DECODE.md.tmpl (new)

The tier-tagged dispatch over the wire ABI, the safe reader and its typed structural decode (the monotone
child-index acyclicity check), and the runtime-artifact embedding (static link versus the std-feature dlopen).
