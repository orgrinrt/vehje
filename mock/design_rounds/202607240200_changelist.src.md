the graded check pass, the generic relational engine, the cheap lowering subset, the output machinery, the wire
family, the runtime generation package, and the orchestration, across twelve crates (eight extended, one renamed,
three new). The experiment-gated frontier stays deferred behind `// FIXME:` markers and BACKLOG, so the workspace

hash is over `arvo_hash::ContentHash`, the fixpoint relations are over `hilavitkutin_api` columnar contracts, the

## Discipline

Every crate compiles `no_std`, alloc-free, lint-clean (the `arvo-types-only`, `no-bare-*`, `no-alloc`, `no-std`,
`forbidden-imports` gates). Deferred behaviour carries a greppable `// FIXME: <reason>` at the spot (the dev-time
e-graph, the mutable-consumer lease, the stencil producer, the full multi-relation join, the `extern "C"` export
with the panic guard). Tests are smoke tests that confirm the wiring compiles and the happy path holds; strict,
fail-first tests for the graded inference follow as the inference fills in. Expert-review the larger units (the
grade vocabulary, the graded check pass, the relational engine, the wire family) before lock. Lock
(`cargo mock lock`) when the core compiles and its DESIGN types are all real, then `cargo mock close`.
