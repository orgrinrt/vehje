# Topic: move the ANF catamorphism to a sibling module

**Date:** 2026-07-25
**Phase:** TOPIC
**Scope:** vehje-lower

## Current state

Landing the node-creating ANF catamorphism grew `vehje-lower/src/lib.rs`
to 482 non-blank, non-comment lines, over the 400-line push-gate limit.

## Decision

Move the ANF catamorphism (the `Anf` type, the `normalize_term` /
`flatten` / `atomize` / `atomize_list` / `normalize_list` fold, the
`record` and `dummy_ref` helpers, and the `BIND_CAP` / `ARG_CAP`
scratch caps) to a sibling `src/anf.rs`, referenced from `lib.rs` by
`mod anf;` and re-exported (`pub use anf::Anf;`). This is a pure
code-organization refactor along the natural seam between the redirecting
passes plus the `Lower` entry (which stay in `lib.rs`) and the
node-creating fold. No design change, no public-surface change, no
doc-template change.
