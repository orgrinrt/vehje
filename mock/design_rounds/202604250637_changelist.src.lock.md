# Source changes

## `mock/runtime-zig/build.zig`

Rename the shared library artefact target from `clause_runtime` to `vehje_runtime`. The output filename derives from this and is referenced as `libvehje_runtime.{so,dylib,dll}` throughout the locked DESIGN, README, and BACKLOG. The Rust loader (`vehje-runtime-driver`) already expects the `libvehje_runtime` filename per its existing tests; this brings the Zig side into agreement.

## `mock/runtime-zig/src/runtime.zig`

Rename the three skeleton `extern "C"` exports:

- `clause_runtime_new` to `vehje_runtime_new`
- `clause_runtime_free` to `vehje_runtime_free`
- `clause_runtime_execute` to `vehje_runtime_execute`

These names match the existing `pub extern "C" fn vehje_runtime_*` declarations in `mock/crates/clause-runtime-abi/src/exports.rs`. The Zig-side names had been left at their pre-rename `clause_*` form, leaving the FFI symbol contract mismatched. This rename makes the symbols match.

The associated constants (`CLAUSE_RESULT_ERR`, `CLAUSE_RESULT_NULL_HANDLE`, `CLAUSE_RESULT_INVALID_INPUT`) stay in their current form because they are tied to the deferred `ClauseResult` ABI type rename. Both sides flip together in the cross-repo coordination round.
